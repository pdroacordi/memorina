# Water: painted bodies, projections, reflection, motion, hazard, ice and rain

Moved verbatim from `CLAUDE.md` ("Water") on 2026-10-02.

Design 03 §6: water is computed, not drawn. Everything lives in `scenes/world/environment/water/` (the pool's FREEZE glue in `scenes/world/interactables/freezable_water/`), tuned by `WaterProfile` (motion), `WaterLook` (palette LUTs in `assets/sprites/world/water/`, reflection, caustics) and `IceProfile` under `resources/world/water/`.

- **Water is PAINTED, not placed.** A `WaterLayer` (a `TileMapLayer` of 16 px water cells, `water_tiles.tres`) is dropped into a room from one of three presets - `water_pool_layer.tscn`, `freezable_water_layer.tscn`, `water_lake_layer.tscn` - and painted: into a pit for a pool, over the ground and down to the room's bottom for a lake. At runtime `WaterBasins` (pure, tested) reads the cells the way poured water would settle - one flat surface per 4-connected group at its highest row, each run of columns reaching it one body, each column as deep as its cells run down unbroken - and the layer instances its `body_scene` per basin, sized and placed, with the basin's stepped floor (the data texture's b channel, clipped by every shader). A cell no surface can reach is warned about, never drawn. `surface_inset` is the dry lip between the top of the cells and the waterline, and the mirror axis is derived from it (`-inset/2`). A layer has no profile/look overrides: another kind of water is another preset with its own body scene. A body's look is pushed by NAME - every `WaterLook` property is named after the uniform it feeds, and each material takes only the ones its shader declares - so a new look field needs no push code, only a matching uniform. `WaterBody.fit_area()` sizes anything that must cover the water (volume, hazard, a song receiver). The painted swatch is the editor preview; at runtime the layer is `enabled = false` (which hides its tiles, not the bodies under it).
- **A body of water is world art at one z.** `WaterBody` composes what it finds under it - a `Surface` always; a `Veil`, a `Volume` and a `Hazard` when bodies can fall in (`water_pool.tscn`) - so what water does is its scene's choice. Every `WaterQuad` draws at absolute `WaterQuad.Z` (50): **anything the water should reflect draws below 50, anything that must cover water draws above it.** The greyhush greys water like any world pixel. The body's origin is the centre of its rest waterline; `surface_rest_y()` is the one place the surface line comes from.
- **Two projections.** A POOL in a pit is a cross-section seen edge-on (`water_surface.gdshader`): its waterline is a profile that waves and splashes, and what is behind shows through by depth. A LAKE in front of the land is a plane seen from above (`water_lake.gdshader`, `water_lake.tscn`): opaque (it hides the bank's dirt), a straight far-shore line, a reflection torn into row bands that grow and slide more toward the viewer, and 1 px glints; it runs to the frame's bottom and should run off the room's sides (a lake ending mid-screen reads as a pasted rectangle). A lake has **no `WaterProfile`** - nobody sees its waterline, so it simulates nothing - but each of its columns keeps its own clock at the memory over it (the data texture's g; its r is 0, flat), because a room-wide lake on one clock kept its grey end moving at the pace of its remembered end. The shared reflection code is `water_reflection.gdshaderinc` (kept out of `water_common`, which the veil includes and which must never declare a screen texture).
- **The reflection has two sources and no camera.** The world comes from the automatic screen copy (taken once per canvas layer - no `BackBufferCopy`; `docs/knowledge/gotchas/screen-texture-copy-scope.md`). Ivo and the guardians are culled out of the world canvas, so they come from the creature pass, which `CreatureMask` publishes as the `greyhush_creature_pass` global. The mirror is computed in WORLD space and carried to the screen by derivatives (`SCREEN_UV + (m - world_pos) * uv_per_world`), so zoom, shake, stretch and a camera moving under a pause all cancel out; never upload a camera transform to it (it would go stale under the paused lesson push-in). `mirror_axis_offset`: water `b` pixels below a bank uses `-b/2`, so the reflection starts at the bank's top as in the reference; a `WaterLayer` sets it from its inset.
- **Two grids.** Art decisions (waterline, depth bands, the reflection's row bands, caustics, ice) are made in WORLD texels; memory decisions (the reflection gate, the edge stipple) in GAME pixels on the greyhush's Bayer grid (`dither_common.gdshaderinc`, shared). Never `FRAGCOORD`, never `TIME` (`water_time` is the body's pausable clock). The authored reflection strength is POSTERISED; the stipple is spent only where it fades - with memory (`reflect_memory_low`..`high`) and at the screen edge - or the whole reflection becomes a checkerboard.
- **Motion is a spring field gated per column by memory, with NO stop threshold** (user decision, 2026-09-22): grey water moves in proportion to how remembered it is and is fully still only at exactly 0 - unlike `MemoryClock`, which does threshold. `WaterSurfaceField` (pure, tested) couples springs on each column's distance FROM THE SWELL (coupling raw heights smooths the swell away: `docs/knowledge/bugs/water-swell-flattened-by-spread.md`); splashes write DISPLACEMENT, so a crest raised in water at memory 0 stays bit-for-bit. Springs, not analytic waves, because a pulse covering half a pool would leave a phase scar that never heals. Reflection strength is read at the WATER's pixel: forgotten water shows nothing; the lighter top line is always drawn.
- **Wind piles a crest** (`WindCrest`, `systems/air`): added to the springs' target like the swell, never higher than the bank it leans on (read from the room's map), stepped off screen while it stands, and pinned into a ramp by ice (the water trial's frozen wave, design 02 §8.4).
- **Ivo does not swim.** Water is a hazard: a `HazardZone` (`scenes/combat/hazard/`, the pool's `Hazard`, on the `Hazard` physics layer, starting `WaterBody.hazard_depth` below the surface so the fall visibly goes in) detects the BODY and calls `Character.receive_hazard()`. Never the Hurtbox: the hurtbox means "can be hit", and a roll's i-frames key it unmonitorable - that hid Ivo from the water and made the pool floor his safe ground. No i-frames hold anyone above water. `Character` just takes the damage (`_wound`), a `Guardian` ignores it (its health is inert), `Player` takes 1 HP, loses control and sinks (`fell_into_hazard`; his input timers keep ticking so presses made under water expire there). `Game` holds the sink for `hazard_sink_hold`, fades out, `Player.respawn()`s him on `SafeGroundTracker.last_safe_position()` with `hazard_grace` i-frames, swaps to that ground's room itself if it is another one (`_enter_room`), `GameCamera.snap()`s and clears. Each fall is its own beat (`_hazard_beat`): control returns while the screen clears, so a second fall mid-clear is normal, and `Fade.faded` - not a tween's `finished`, which a killed tween never emits - is what every fade is awaited on. A fall on his last HP is the ordinary death. `SafeGroundTracker` (`scenes/characters/components/`) records only firm ground under BOTH probes (a margin wider than the feet), never a collider in the `unsafe_ground` group (`IceCollider` joins it), never a spot inside a hazard, and is switched off while he sinks.
- **Submersion is `WaterVeil`**, a `blend_mul` `WaterQuad` on the creature layer (`CreatureMask.join_layer`) sharing `water_common.gdshaderinc`, so the waterline crosses Ivo on the same row as the ground behind him. It never reads a screen texture; multiply keeps transparent pixels transparent.
- **FREEZE** (`FreezableWater` = `water_pool` + `IceFront` + `IceSheet` + `IceCollider`, glue only): `SongReceiver.song_entered(song, origin)` starts the ice where the pulse was lit. **Growth needs living water** - the fronts and the setting run at the memory under them, spent column by column so a front never leaps a dead column (`docs/knowledge/bugs/ice-front-leaps-dead-columns.md`) - and a column freezes only while it holds water (`WaterBody.wet_columns()`; the front crosses a dry one). The surface is held (`harden_at`) before the ice looks solid and carries weight (`solid_at`; asserted in that order). **The thaw ignores memory** (user decision): a front from the origin (or the banks, `FROM_EDGES`) after `thaw_delay`, so the ice melts behind the player and a song in a dead place never leaves a permanent bridge. **Ice is its own sheet** (`architecture/ice-is-its-own-sheet`): when ice first takes a column, `WaterSurfaceField` pins it at its height above the swell (calm water freezes flat, a crest keeps its shape) and `IceSheetShape` (pure, tested) captures a band from that top down `IceProfile.thickness`; the band keeps its world height whatever the water does under it. `IceSheet` draws the bands at absolute z 51 (`ice_sheet.gdshader`: crystals stipple in by solidity on the texel grid, `WaterLook.ice_ramp` by depth; no screen texture, `TIME` or `FRAGCOORD`) and the water shader draws no ice. `IceCollider` is a chain of `SegmentShape2D` along the bands' tops, one per `segment_width`, one-way up to 45 degrees and a wall above, solid only while every column in it is solid, toggled deferred and only on change; a segment is laid only while it is not solid.
- **The level moves** (`WaterBody.set_level`): a body is painted at its HIGHEST level and can sink to dry. Moving it moves its origin, so everything reading `surface_rest_y()` follows; the floors keep their world height (`level_range()` is painted top to deepest floor), so the water gets shallower over the same stepped bed, a column whose floor is above the level is dry, the mirror axis stays halfway up the growing bank, and a dry body hides and stops taking bodies. `surface_y(x)` is the waterline with its waves (what floats sits on it), `drip(x, depth)` a raindrop's dent, `WaterBody.at(node, point)` the body under a point (group `WaterBody.GROUP`).
- **Chuva's weather is `RainFall`**, the song's `PulseEffect`: drops fall EACH TO WHERE IT LANDS - a ray finds the ground, a load or a Redoma's shell when the drop is born, a pool's live waterline stops it as it falls (the rain raises the water it falls into), a lake takes it anywhere across its plane - and splashes there (`SpriteBursts`), denting the water; a drop crossing the top of a character on screen (Player and Enemy bodies, found once a frame) bursts on its head. Drops are only born over the screen; everything is clipped to the season mask. A darkened storm sky was tried and cut (the user, 2026-09-30): a dithered multiply over the whole pulse speckles the screen with Bayer dots - the grey's rule (no high-frequency texture) holds for weather too.
- **Chuva raises water to its REACH** (03 §6.5: "poças sobem", the level authored, never computed): room map `r` painted above `~` or `f` water in the same pit joins it - the painted water is the rest level, the top `r` row the reach (`RoomMap.reach`, resolved by the parser; `WaterBasins.Basin.rest`; `WaterBody.rest_depth` / `rest_level()`; a `WaterLayer`'s `rising_body_scene`: `rising_pool.tscn` for `~`, `rain_basin.tscn` for `f`). An `r` group alone is a dry basin; over a lake it is an import error (`takes_reach`). `architecture/a-pool-rests-below-its-painted-reach`; the trial is the water trial's tank.
- **`RainBasin`** (glue on a `FreezableWater`, `rain_basin.tscn`, or on a pool, `rising_pool.tscn`): rests at its rest level (dry for a lone `r`) until a RAIN pulse covers it; it rises while lit and sinks back to rest when the last pulse leaves, at the memory under it, and under ice it never rises but keeps draining: Chuva then Congelar is ice where there was no water, and the water sinks under it while the thaw comes from the origin - two clocks (design 02 §8.4, Combinado 1). A `Floater` frozen into the ice stays where the ice holds it. It sets its rest level deferred, after a `FreezableWater` (when there is one) has sized its receiver from the full water. A `Floater` (room map `O`, the fallen log) lies where placed until water reaches it, then rides `surface_y() + draft`, a one-way platform on Props: the dry basin's log carries Ivo up to its passage. Water a shell holds back is not under it.

## Current limits (from the old "Known gaps", 2026-10-02)

- The editor shows painted water as its swatch; shaded water exists only at runtime.
- A lake has no shore art: run it off the room's sides.
- Ripple state is lost when a room is evicted. `IceProfile` timings are first guesses.
