# Air: one physical channel for wind, currents and the gale

Moved verbatim from `CLAUDE.md` ("Air") on 2026-10-02.

Design 03 sections 5.3-5.4 and 6.1: wind, a current, a song's gale and the weather push "pelo mesmo canal fisico". That channel is **`Airflow`** (`scenes/world/environment/wind/`, in `game.tscn` under `World`, found by group): `AirflowSource`s register and `sample(point)` sums them into a **velocity** (px/s), scaled by the memory at the point - air in the grey has stopped. `AirflowShelter`s (the bell jar's shell, later benches) still the air inside them.

- **Wind is a velocity the air carries, never a force.** `AirflowBody` (a component, runs at physics priority -1 so there is no frame of lag) samples the air at its body and calls `Character.push(wind)`, which accumulates `carry()` for the frame; the body's own motion decides what it means. `LocomotionComponent.ground_update/air_update(delta, axis, wind_x)` steer toward input speed PLUS the wind, so a tailwind carries a jump further and a headwind shortens it - bounded, where an added force against capped braking was either nothing or a runaway. On the ground, feet ignore anything under `LocomotionStats.wind_deadzone` and slide at `wind_grip` of the excess, so a breeze never breaks a standing performance and a gust does, through the existing `is_still()` (design 5.4 item 3, no new rule). Vertical air is `lift_update`. A RigidBody2D is dragged toward the wind's velocity along it. Guardians have no `AirflowBody` (weather drops in a boss fight, design 5.5). A guardian move may carry Ivo through the same `push()`: `GuardianGust` (GRD-04, the autumn scarecrow's gust) pushes `GuardianAttack.push_speed` px/s along the floor while the move swings, with no `Airflow` source, so it is the same in every arena and at any memory.
- **A headwind slows the step under the deadzone** (MEM-16): `LocomotionComponent.headwind_factor` takes up to `LocomotionStats.headwind_slow` (0.25) of move speed at `wind_deadzone`, linearly below it, when the axis walks into the wind; tailwinds and standing are untouched, and drift above the deadzone adds as before. It is 0 by default: only Ivo's `ivo_locomotion_stats.tres` sets it, so creatures' lunges are unchanged.
- **Standing in a wind he faces, Ivo braces** (`is_bracing()` with no axis, Memorina not drawn, `brace_wind` 60 or more; the `brace_stand` clip holds push frame 7, "Hold one push frame (Recommended)", user, 2026-11-11); his back to it, he does not. **The wind never slides a standing Ivo off a ledge**: `Player._hold_the_lip` zeroes a wind slide when a ray under the body's downwind edge finds no ground one step ahead, or, braced standing, under the rear boot's heel (`Player.BRACE_HEEL`, pinned to push frame 7 by `ivo_sheet_test`), so both boots stay on the stone (the capsule's round bottom would still touch the corner). Walking off, or the wind in the air, still carries him. `is_still()` is false in the frame a slide was held and the next, so a gust still breaks a song at a lip. User, 2026-11-11: "Headwind slows the walk (Recommended)", "Brace pose + stop at ledges (Recommended)"; lees "Not yet (Recommended)" (placed when a winter puzzle needs a song under the sky, with visible art).
- **Ivo is half sheltered while the instrument is out** (`Player.memorina_shelter`, pushed into `AirflowBody.exposure`; design 5.4 item 2), and leans into a wind he walks against (the `brace` clip, `Player.is_bracing()`).
- **`WindZone`** is a natural current: a box standing on its cell (room map `W`), a direction, a speed and a `WindProfile` (calm/rise/gust/fall - the calms are the window to play in). It draws directional streaks and leaves in layers, so it reads as the world's doing. A FREEZE pulse over it stops it and its streaks hang, icy.
- **Vendaval (`GALE`) is `GaleField`**, the song's `PulseEffect`: a `GaleWind` blowing ONE WAY across the whole disc - the way Ivo faced when the song ended (`ColorPulse.performer`'s `facing`; the user's decision, 2026-09-30) - horizontally, still in an eye (48 px), peaking a third of the way out and gone at the clean disc (`GaleShape`, pure and tested). Played into a current it simply adds to it; aimed wrong, it blows the load away from the plate. It reads as the SONG, not the world, by colour and by bounds rather than by geometry: curling pixel-art gusts, dashes and leaves in the season's tint (`SpriteBursts`, clipped to the season mask), living only inside the pulse and dying with it.
- **`SpriteBursts`** (`song_effects/bursts/`) is the one pool for short pixel-art sprites a song throws off - a gust, a splash - each a `SpriteStrip` resource (`resources/world/effects/`: texture, frames, life, fps, anchor, stepped fade), drawn at whole pixels in world coordinates on the world's clock. The strips are drawn by `tools/art/draw_procedural_sprites.gd`.
- **Wind piles water**: a pool's `WindCrest` (pure, tested; `architecture/wind-piles-a-bounded-crest`) reads the mean air over its columns and builds one signed crest against the downwind bank at the pool's memory (`WaterProfile` Crest group: 160 px at most, half the pool's width, a 256 px wedge, 15 s to build under 150 px/s or more, 4 s to settle), added to the springs' target like the swell, so splashes ride on it and it holds still in the grey. Congelar pins it into a ramp (design 02 §8.4, A Onda Parada: the water trial's frozen wave). It reads the wind and never pushes a body. Before it, `WaterBody._blow` dragged the surface about 1 px - and toward the upwind bank.
- **Weather in a grey place is weak and slow**: a `WindZone`'s push and its clock both scale with the memory over it, so a puzzle that needs its wind outside a pulse must stand in a remembered spot. The winter trial's squall has its own `MemorySource` (`SquallMemory`, strength 1) for that; at the trials' 0.35 its gusts never broke a song (`bugs/the-winter-squall-never-breaks-a-song-at-the-trials-memory`).
- **Puzzles are sized by `JumpReach` with the same wind** (`JumpReach.wind`, steered exactly as `air_update` does); `tests/scenes/world/rooms/trials/autumn_trial_test.gd` proves the autumn chasm beats a jump, the current alone and the gale alone, and yields to the gale played into the current.

## Regional weather (MEM-05, `architecture/regional-weather-push`)

- **`RegionWind`** (`scenes/world/environment/weather/`, `World/RegionWind`, PAUSABLE, physics priority -2, before `AirflowBody` at -1) is the one regional `AirflowSource`. `Game.room_changed` hands it the current room's `Region.weather` (`WeatherProfile`: `speed` px/s at full gust and memory 1, `gusts: WindProfile`, `fight_scale`, `fight_ease`, `gust_particles`) and `Region.weather_wind` (-1 west, 1 east). A null profile is no push.
- **Only open sky of the current region blows**: `wind_at` is zero outside the region's rooms with `Room.has_sky()` (not interior, not `covered`) and their sky bands (`RoomEdges.bands`), and inside a `WeatherShelter` (legend `l`, a lee that stills regional air only; currents and the gale still blow in it). Horizontal only, so no jump height changes. Benches stay full `AirflowShelter`s.
- **One clock, `WeatherGust`** (pure, tested): it advances by the memory sampled at Ivo, so grey weather stops and a pulse wakes the cycle locally (`Airflow` still scales the size by local memory). It **restarts at calm** whenever Ivo enters weather from no weather: a walk-in from a covered room, a door out, a bench or death build, a region cut (design 03 §5.5). In a guardian fight it eases to `fight_scale` and restarts at calm when the fight ends.
- **Calm air is still air** (`calm_strength` 0): the reachability audit stays exact in a calm and a gust is a timing obstacle. `winter_weather_bounds_test` holds the bound with `JumpReach.wind`: calm headwind <= 3 px/s, `calm_time` >= a song plus a jump's airtime, the peak breaks a drawn song at memory 1, the fight peak stays under `wind_deadzone`. **No puzzle may depend on the regional wind**: the player can turn it to 0.
- **The snow reads the same gust**: `RegionWind.drive()` (signed strength x fight x intensity) drives `RegionWeather`'s gust layer sideways; no sideways snow is the calm. Weather never makes a colour pulse: `region_wind.tscn` holds no `MemorySource`.
- **Intensity**: `Settings.weather_intensity()` (0..1, `systems/settings`) scales the regional push and every ambient weather layer, never a `WindZone`, the gale or a guardian's gust.
- Today: `winter_blizzard.tres` (speed 220, `blizzard_gusts.tres` calm 6 / rise 1.5 / gust 2 / fall 1.5 s, calm strength 0, fight 0.1 over 1 s) on threshold, frost_edge and frozen_lake, all west. First guesses; at today's memory 0.3 the peak is 66 px/s and a calm lasts ~20 real s.
- User decisions, 2026-11-11: "Winter only (Recommended)" (other seasons are MEM-06, data only); "Fixed per region (Recommended)"; "All west (Recommended)"; "Yes, a slider (Recommended)"; "Yes, every region (Recommended)" (no falling weather under rock); village weather "Later item (Recommended)"; gust sound "Later (Recommended)". Orchestrator calls: the slider scales regional weather only; a gust may start while Ivo is airborne (the rise is the warning).
