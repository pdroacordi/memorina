# Props and ambient motion

**Never break:** a prop never collides, never carries gameplay and never joins a `CanvasPass`; ambient motion runs only on the memory clock, through `AmbientMotion` (never an `AnimationPlayer` on scaled time, which keeps moving in the grey).

Design 04 sections 5-6, WORLD-08 (WORLD-10's villagers and animals are props of the same kind). Plan, options and the village prop list: `architecture/props-and-ambient-life`.

- **Placement.** Props are hand-placed `Sprite2D` scenes under a `Props` Node2D (`z_index` -10) in a room's contents scene, after `Backdrop` and before `RoomMap`. Draw order by z: `Backdrop.Z` -30, WORLD-07's `[back]` walls -20, props -10, ground and entities 0, water 50. A surface Ivo stands on is a `.room` cell, never a prop. **Pier posts** (WORLD-29, 2026-10-13; `props/frozen_lake/pier_post.tscn`, `tools/art/draw_post.py`): a prop standing in a lake is two sprites, the post from under the deck's plank to the waterline (under `Props`, so the lake reflects it) and its foot in the water, a child at absolute z 52 (over water 50 and ice 51, under the void 60); the origin is the waterline, the lake's cell top. The fishing village's deck stands on 22, 80 px apart.
- **A prop sheet:** frames side by side, season bands stacked; nature props wear `prop_seasonal_material.tres` (3 bands, `(2, 0, 1, 0)`), built props none. `vframes` equals the band count and row 0 is band 0, so `frame` 0..hframes-1 never leaves it; every `sequence` entry stays below `hframes` (a higher one shows another season's art).
- **`AmbientMotion`** (child Node of the prop's Sprite2D, PAUSABLE even under an ALWAYS parent): each physics frame, on screen and visible, it samples `MemoryField.sample` at the prop (plus `sample_offset`), advances its own time by `delta * memory` (`AmbientMath.advance`) and writes `frame` (`AmbientMath.frame_at`, with an optional `sequence` and a stable per-position `phase`). Below `stop_threshold` (0.2: "Stop below 0.2 memory", user, 2026-10-09) it holds its frame; a pulse resumes it from there. Off screen nothing advances. It asserts it is never under a `Character`.
- **`FactStaging`** (STORY-03, `systems/story`): a Node2D under `Props` that shows one of its children by save facts (a pose of the daughter, the wall guitar, the mentor), cross-fading on a change. It is staging, not gameplay; never put `RegionPresence` on its direct child.
- **`RegionPresence`** (child Node): shows the parent while `Region.current_baseline() >= min_baseline`, re-applied on `Region.memory_changed`. Villagers who leave as the village falls to 0.6 get 0.7 (WORLD-11).
- **A villager who talks** (STORY-08, `systems/dialogue`): the talk is a `Talkable` (legend `t`) placed beside the prop, never on it or in it; the prop stays set dressing and does not turn to Ivo. The Talkable's `presence` param composes its own `RegionPresence` at the villager's baseline, so both leave together. WORLD-10's "Idle villagers, no dialogue" (2026-10-08) is revised by "Story cast + some villagers (Recommended)" (user, 2026-10-15); the villagers' lines are STORY-09.
- **Rotation** (windmill sails, the water wheel, the mill's gear) is baked into frames on the 1x grid ("Baked pixel frames", user, 2026-10-09): `tools/art/bake_rotation.gd` (pure `RotationBake`, `rotation_bake_test`) halves a 2x still to art px, and per frame upscales x4, turns about the still's own centre by nearest sampling and shrinks keeping each block's most common colour, over one symmetry period (`--period`, 45 for 8 spokes). Every point is read through the first sector of its angle, so a still that is not exactly symmetric still closes its loop; the tool prints the wrap ratio (the last-to-first step over the median step), held under 1.2 by `rotation_bake_test` (`bugs/the-baked-wheel-and-gear-hitch-at-their-loop-seam`). Speed is data: fps = turns/s x symmetry x frames (the wheel and the gear: 9.6 fps, 0.1 turn/s). The pivot is the frame's centre, the scene's origin.
- **Lit windows, flames, smoke** are frames greyed like everything else: only characters escape the grey.
