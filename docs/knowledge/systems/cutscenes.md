# Cutscenes

**Never break:** A scene is data played between two rest states: `FactStaging` stays the only writer of its children's visibility and the rest after a scene is derived from `SCENE_SEEN`, written before the stagings uncover in the same frame. A `StoryActor` exists only during a scene, never carries a `GreyhushShield` or an `AmbientMotion`, and moves only through `Character.teleport` and its `ScriptedController`. The director runs on the pausable, scaled physics clock, writes nothing to `WorldFreeze`, reads no input, and writes nothing once it is out of the tree. A skip lands the scene and never chooses for the player: it stops before a talk. `PlayerInput.blocked` has one writer, `Player._refresh_block()`: a talk, else a scene, else the map.

## Summary

STORY-15, built 2026-10-16 from the plan `architecture/cutscenes-are-data-played-between-two-rest-states` (doc 06 Pilar 1). A `Cutscene` is a tree of steps (`resources/cutscene/`); the pure `CutsceneRunner` walks it; `World/CutsceneDirector` begins each leaf step, polls the task it returns, and ends the scene. Actors are `StoryActor` scenes in a contents scene's `Cast`; marks and visuals live beside them. A scene starts from a talk's cue (`CutsceneBeat`) or a room entry (`CutsceneTrigger`). The demo is the daughter going to bed after the failed song.

## Data (`resources/cutscene/`, one `class_name` per file)

- `Cutscene`: `id` (the SCENE_SEEN id), `steps`, `skippable`. `CutsceneCatalog` (`cutscene_catalog.tres`) lists them; `validate()` names duplicate or empty ids, a talk inside a `CutsceneTogether`, a talk with `wait = false` that is not the scene's last step, two steps of one together moving the same subject, and a SCENE_SEEN fact naming no catalog scene. The director asserts it in debug builds.
- Containers: `CutsceneSequence`, `CutsceneTogether` (all at once, ends with the last), `CutsceneBranch` + `CutsceneCase` (`when`/`unless`; the first case that holds plays, read when reached).
- `CutsceneStep`: `begin(context) -> CutsceneTask`, `land(context)` (the end state at once), `subjects()`, `names()` (the ids that must resolve). Leaves: `CutsceneWait`, `CutsceneBars`, `CutsceneCamera` (`targets`, `offset` from their mean feet, `zoom` 0 keeps, `seconds`), `CutsceneWalk` (`actor`, `mark` or `x`, `pace` 0..1), `CutsceneFace` (`toward`, `away`, or `side`), `CutscenePlace`, `CutsceneGesture` (`clip`, `wait` for its settle), `CutsceneEmote` (`Emote { ELLIPSIS, NOTE, EXCLAIM }`), `CutscenePose` (Ivo, `DialogueBeat.Pose`), `CutsceneTalk` (`talk` set, `speaker`, `wait`), `CutsceneSound`, `CutsceneMelody`, `CutsceneSilence`, `CutsceneMusic` (background music at SCENE layer, never waits; `systems/music`), `CutsceneMusicMark`, `CutsceneMuffle`, `CutsceneFade` (`colour`), `CutsceneShow` (a `CutsceneVisual`'s alpha), `CutsceneBloom`, `CutsceneFray` ("Added for the flashbacks"). No grant step: items come from talks, skills never from a scene.
- Ids: `StoryActor.actor_id`, `CutsceneMark.mark_id`, `CutsceneVisual.visual_id`, `MemoryBloom.bloom_id`, and `&"ivo"`; found by group inside the host's contents scene (`host.owner`), so neighbour rooms' ids stay apart (`CutsceneCast.find_in`).

## Runtime (`scenes/world/cutscene/`)

- **`CutsceneRunner`** (pure): `next()` returns the leaf ids to begin now, `done(id)` ends one, `is_finished()`, `to_land()` ends every running leaf and every leaf up to the next `CutsceneTalk` and returns them in order. Ids, not steps, so one step resource can appear twice.
- **Tasks**, the only three kinds: `CutsceneTask` (over at once), `CutsceneWaitTask` (scaled seconds; an optional `on_end` runs once, at the end or when stopped), `CutsceneUntilTask` (polls a Callable, `on_stop`). Nothing awaits a tween.
- **`CutsceneContext`** (virtual services) and **`WorldCutsceneContext`**: `point_of`, `walk`, `place`, `face`, `gesture`, `land_gesture`, `emote`, `pose`, `bars`, `frame`, `talk`, `sound`, `melody`, `silence`, `music`, `music_position`, `fade`, `show_visual`, `stage`, `bloom`, `fray`; `muffle`; `restore(clear_fade, cut)` undoes all but the record (frays calmed, blooms hidden, a muffle lifted). **`CutsceneCast`**: an actor enters on its first step (`actor(id)`); `point_of` an actor that has not entered reads its rest child; `release_all()` uncovers every staging, then the actors leave.
- **`CutsceneDirector`** (`World/CutsceneDirector`, INHERIT, group `cutscene_directors`, `find(tree)`): exports `catalog`, `ivo`, `camera`, `letterbox`, `fade`, `dialogue`; signals `scene_began`, `scene_ended`, `ivo_driven(controller)`, `ivo_posed(pose)`. `play(scene, host) -> bool` refuses while a scene plays, so no scene runs inside a talk inside a scene. Each physics tick: a requested skip, the tasks, then up to 256 newly reached leaves begun (an instant step costs no frame).
  - **End:** SCENE_SEEN (`SaveSystem.mark_scene_seen`), then `restore`: stagings uncover, actors leave (process disabled deferred), visuals reset, voices and bubbles stop, the bars close (0.4 s), Ivo's driver and pose are released; the frame eases back to Ivo over 0.8 s and `follow(ivo)`.
  - **Skip** (`request_skip()`, only while `can_skip()`: skippable and no talk step running): lands on its next unpaused tick. If that ends the scene, the bars and the frame cut back at once (a skip is a cut already).
  - **Drop** (`interrupt()` from `Player.hurt` and `fell_into_hazard`; `on_room_changed` when the host is not in the new room's contents): the same restore without the record, and a fade the scene left black is cleared; the rest shown before (a stand-in included) shows again.
  - **Out of the tree** (a death's `SceneSwap`): `_exit_tree` forgets the scene and writes nothing.
- **`CameraRig`**: the Node2D the camera follows during a scene; it moves on `TWEEN_PROCESS_PHYSICS`. The first camera step places it on the shown frame (`GameCamera.rig_point_for(shown_centre())`) before `follow(rig)`, so the take-over does not move the frame.
- **`CutsceneBeat`** (`extends DialogueBeat`, hold STILL, asserted in `start()` and checked by `cutscene_rooms_test`): its `start()` asks the director to play `cutscene` and `finish()`es on `scene_ended`, so the talk resumes under the start gates. A refused play finishes at once with an error.
- **`CutsceneTrigger`** (contents scene; `cutscene`, `when`, `unless`, `delay`, `repeat`): `Game.room_changed` → `CutsceneDirector.on_room_changed` arms the room's first trigger whose gates hold and whose scene is unseen, read from `room.contents()` and not the tree's group, because contents made on this visit are added deferred (bugs/a-cutscene-trigger-in-a-first-visited-room-never-arms). Its `delay` counts once it is inside the tree, and it plays once `Player.can_begin_scene()`. Leaving the room disarms it (`cutscene_trigger_test`). Placed: village_house's `Triggers/OpeningMorning` (`from_black`) and `Triggers/HomeReturn` (STORY-16).
- **Bodies** (GRD-06): a `Character` that is not an actor joins `CutsceneCast.BODY_GROUP` with a `cutscene_id` (only `Guardian` has the export, set on the mentor's instance in the lantern). `CutsceneCast.find_body` / `point_of` resolve it after actors, so `CutscenePlace`, `CutsceneFace` and `CutsceneCamera` work on it; gestures, walks and emotes stay the actors'. It never enters or leaves: it is the same body before and after the scene (`cutscene_rooms_test` counts its id).
- **`CutsceneMark`** (Marker2D), **`CutsceneVisual`** (hidden at rest; children are its picture; no `PointLight2D`), **`EmoteBubble`** (a word-less bubble drawn in the world at 2x, so it greys with the place; it follows its target on the pausable clock).

## Actors (`scenes/characters/story_actor/`)

- `StoryActor extends Character`: collision layer 0, mask ground; an inert `Hurtbox` (layer 0) and `Health`; `Controller` (`ScriptedController`) and `Locomotion`. Hidden and DISABLED at rest. `enter()` teleports onto its staging's shown child, faces the child's `flip_h` (a `Sprite2D` still or an `AnimatedSprite2D` figure, STORY-05), asks the clip named like the child (`Standing` → `standing`), covers the staging and shows itself; `leave()` hides it and disables it deferred.
- **`StoryAnimationResolver`**: the walk clip while walking; else the clip asked, held on its settle clip once a one-shot ends (`settles()`); `is_settled()`. A walk asks the idle clip, so a one-shot never replays after it. Each actor's subclass owns its clip consts (`systems/animation`).
- **`ScriptedController`** + pure **`ArriveMath`**: brake when the braking distance plus one step reaches the gap; within `SETTLE_PX` (2) the walk is over and the body teleports onto the mark. `arrive_math_test` walks Ivo's and the daughter's locomotion from 3 to 400 px at three paces: never more than 1 px past.
- Actors are in the root canvas (no `GreyhushShield`), greyed like the props they stand for, and animate on the scaled clock even where the place is grey (user, below); the rest pose after the scene is a prop again and follows the memory clock.
- `cutscene_rooms_test`: every staging child an actor stands for has a clip of the same snake name.

## Ivo

- `Player.begin_scene()` / `end_scene()` (wired from the director's signals), `drive(ScriptedController)` (`move_axis()` reads the driver; `_steer_driver` teleports onto the mark on arrival), `stage_pose(pose)` (shared with `pause_talk`), `can_begin_scene()`, `is_in_story()` (talk or scene), `is_in_scene()`.
- **The block is derived** (`_refresh_block`): in a talk, the talk's listening (or the map); else in a scene, blocked; else the map. `block_input` / `unblock_input` set the map's part only. So a NOTES beat inside a scene still gets its notes, and a scene ending under the map leaves him blocked.
- In a scene, doors, talks, pickups, benches, the Memorina and the map are refused (`is_in_story()`); the pause and the notebook hold over it; `NoticeHold.applies` (both HUD notice watchers) reads `is_in_story()`.
- A scene walk under `Player.STROLL_PACE` (0.75) plays his `walk` clip (`ivo_walk.png`, STORY-16); faster, his run.

## Camera, letterbox, fade

- `GameCamera.zoom_to(z, seconds)` (0 cuts; `focus`/`push_in` keep `_tween_zoom`), `shown_centre()`, `centre_for(subject)` (its feet, look-ahead and current framing), `rig_point_for(centre)`. Following a marker freezes the offset (a running look-ahead tween is killed). Room clamps still apply: a one-screen interior cannot pan at zoom 1 but can at 2 (`camera_rig_test`).
- `Letterbox` (`scenes/ui/letterbox/`, `CanvasLayer/Letterbox` after `LessonCinematic`, ALWAYS, scaled PAUSE_PROCESS tweens): `open(seconds)`, `close(seconds)`, `is_open()`, `shown()`. `LessonCinematic.letterbox` uses it; a lesson and a scene never overlap (the Memorina is refused in a scene).
- `Fade.to_black(seconds)` / `to_colour(colour, seconds)` / `to_clear(seconds)` (0.5 by default; 0 cuts), `is_black()` (any cover shows). A cover from clear starts in its own hue and a clear keeps it, so a white fade never passes through grey.

## Skip

`PauseMenu` has a hidden `SkipScene` entry ("Skip scene" / "Pular cena", `PAUSE_SKIP_SCENE`) between Resume and Quit to title; `open()` shows it when `can_skip` (set by `Game._ready` to `CutsceneDirector.can_skip` through `Screens.set_scene_skip`) answers true, and the box grows by one entry. Pressing it emits `Screens.skip_scene_requested` (→ `CutsceneDirector.request_skip`) and closes the menu.

## Save

`SaveFact.Kind.SCENE_SEEN` (appended; `id` is the scene id). `PlayerData.scenes_seen: Array[StringName]`; old saves load empty. `SaveSystem.is_scene_seen` / `mark_scene_seen` (emits `progress_changed`). Live data like heard: a bench commits it, a death forgets it. The playtest runner's `"scenes_seen": [ids]` seeds it. The reach audit cannot read SCENE_SEEN: never gate a way on it without teaching `ReachKit`.

## Added for the flashbacks (STORY-05 step 4, 2026-11-12)

The vocabulary of `architecture/story-05-playable-flashbacks` ("Cutscene vocabulary added"), for `docs/design/drafts/camada_pessoal.md` §2 "Linguagem cinemática"; each step has a `cutscene_steps_test` case.

- **`MemoryBloom`** (`scenes/world/memory/memory_bloom.gd`, Node2D, group `memory_blooms`, `bloom_id`, `feather`, `tint`): owns one `MemorySource` it makes at `_ready`, hidden at rest like a bench's `Bloom`; `bloom_to(shape, rect_size, radius, centre, strength, ring, seconds)` cuts or eases it (node-bound tween: pausable, scaled), `reset()` hides it. The field reads a hidden source as nothing. `MemoryFieldMath` and the shader are unchanged: the dither, its game-pixel grid and its `greyhush_time` animation are the greyhush's own (`memory_bloom_test`).
- **`CutsceneBloom`** (`bloom_id`, `at`, `offset`, `shape`, `rect_size`, `radius`, `strength`, `ring`, `seconds`): the source centres on `point_of(at)` (empty: the bloom's own node) plus `offset`, read when the step begins. `at` is an addition to the plan: the collapse centres on Alba's actor. `restore` hides every bloom the scene touched, a drop and a skip included (`cutscene_director_test`). Centre and size ease on one curve, so a RECT grown from a fixed top keeps that top.
- **`CutsceneFade.colour`** (black by default; white for a crossing): `context.fade(cover, seconds, colour)` calls `Fade.to_colour`.
- **`CutsceneMusicMark`** (`seconds`, `every`): waits for `context.music_position()` (`Music.position()`, negative when nothing plays) to reach `seconds`, or for the music to stop, so a cut lands on the music. With `every` > 0 (STORY-05 step 7) it waits for the next mark `seconds + k * every` at or after the position it began at, or for a looping cue to wrap: flashback 1 cuts on the next bar line (0.555 + k * 1.68 s).
- **`CutsceneMuffle`** (`amount` 0..1, step 7): `context.muffle(amount)` opens `Music.muffle(&"cutscene", amount)`; the scene's end, a drop and a skip lift it (`cutscene_director_test`). `fits(cue)` and `CutsceneCatalog.validate` refuse a mark past the end of a cue its scene asks for; `MusicCue` has no gap field (`systems/music`, "Marks").
- **`CutsceneFray`** (`actor`, `seconds`): agitates every `CorruptionEdge` child of the actor (it enters); the wait's `on_end` calms it, and `land` and `restore` calm it too. `CorruptionEdge` stays the only writer of the edge uniforms. A creature's edge puts its body on the creature pass and writes alpha 253 (the unshielded mark) on every pixel, which would take an actor out of the root canvas and its draw order among the props. So an actor's edge has `creature_pass` false: the actor stays in the root canvas and its sprite carries the edge material only while agitated. At rest it draws as plain art; during the glitch the 253 alpha draws 99 % opaque and no composite reads the mark (`corruption_edge_test`; the plan's "swap the material only for the glitch").
- **`CutsceneTrigger.zone`** (an `Area2D`; its mask must hold Ivo's layer, 256): the armed trigger plays only while `zone.overlaps_body(ivo)`, after its delay and Ivo's gates (`cutscene_trigger_test.test_a_zone_waits_for_ivo_inside_it`).
- **`Letterbox.rest_thickness`** (px, 0..`bar_height`, read-only; set by `rest_at(px, seconds)`, whose one writer is the `FlashbackDirector`, step 5): closed bars slide to it and `close()` returns to it, so the bars stay thin while Ivo walks the past and thicken for talks (`letterbox_test`).

## The visit: FlashbackDirector (STORY-05 step 5, 2026-11-12)

- **`Flashback`** (`resources/story/flashbacks/flashback.gd`): `number`, `crossing`, `opening`, `leaving` (id `flashback_<n>`), `returning`, `music`, `entry_mark` (s into `music`), `entry_fade` (s of white; 0 cuts), `bars_thin` (px), `past_room` (Room id, `garden_past`), `arrival` (feet, px from the past room's top-left) and `arrival_facing`. **`FlashbackCatalog`** (`flashback_catalog.tres`, empty until step 7): `by_crossing(scene)`, `validate(cutscenes)` names a number outside 1..5 or listed twice, a missing scene or one not in the cutscene catalog, a leaving id that is not `flashback_<n>`, a returning scene naming anything but `ivo` (its host is wherever Ivo stood), no music, and an entry mark or an opening/leaving `CutsceneMusicMark` that does not `fit` the music. The director asserts it in debug builds.
- **`FlashbackDirector`** (`World/FlashbackDirector`, INHERIT, group `flashback_directors`; exports `catalog`, `cutscenes`, `dialogue`, `ivo`, `letterbox`). On `scene_ended` of a catalog crossing that is recorded and whose flashback is due, it waits until no talk is open, asks `Music` for the visit's cue (SCENE layer, owned by itself: it covers the past region's silence), waits for `entry_mark`, keeps the return point (room, feet, facing) in memory and emits `travel_requested(point, WHITE, entry_fade)` → `Game.travel`. On `Game.travelled` it sets `Letterbox.rest_at(bars_thin, 0)` and plays `opening` on the past room's contents. The `leaving` scene is started by the past's contents (a beat); its recorded end sets the bars to 0 and travels back under a 0 s black (the scene left the screen black: a recorded end never clears a fade). There it plays `returning` on the return room's contents, and at its end releases the music.
- Hosts are the rooms' contents roots, so ids resolve in the past room (opening) and the return room (returning). `cutscene_rooms_test` counts a flashback's opening and returning as started.
