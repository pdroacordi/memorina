# Guardians: phases, the call, the recall, the AI and staging

Moved verbatim from `CLAUDE.md` ("Guardians (boss fights)") on 2026-10-02.

A guardian encounter is `docs/design/02_mecanicas.md` sections 3 and 4: pressure → lucidity window (call-and-response) → consequence, with the emergency QTE riding on one unavoidable attack, and restoration as the sync that teaches the song. Everything generic lives in `scenes/characters/guardians/`; **a concrete guardian is a scene plus a `GuardianStats`** (`resources/characters/guardians/<name>/`), never a subclass, unless it does something no resource can describe.

- **`GuardianFight` is the phase logic and is pure** (`RefCounted`, tested): `DORMANT → PRESSURE → LUCIDITY → RELAPSE → PRESSURE … → RESTORED`, the hit threshold, the answer window, the relapse clock and the aggression that every failure adds. `Guardian` drives it and does all the sounding, moving and saving; nothing else reads the phase except the resolver. **The recall is a gate**: `set_recall_pending(true)` (the guardian reads the save for it) makes hits saturate at the threshold without opening a window, and `skill_recalled()` is then the blow that opens it - the design's guarantee that no one leaves without the skill. Every answer short of the last passes through `RELAPSE` (`GuardianStats.relapse_time`, `FAILED_RELAPSE_SCALE` of it after a failure): the guardian stands lost, hits do not count, and pressure resumes on its own clock.
- **Hits destabilise, they never wound.** `Guardian._on_hit_received` skips `Health` and knockback and counts the hit into the fight. `Health` on a guardian is inert. **Touching a fighting guardian hurts**: `ContactHitbox` (a `Hitbox` with `continuous = true`, re-applied to whoever stays inside as their i-frames lapse) is phase-owned - `monitoring` is written by the script every frame, on under PRESSURE only, never keyed in a clip. A move with `GuardianAttack.body_contact = false` turns it off from its telegraph to its end (`Guardian.body_hurts()`), so the move's own box is its only hit: the Frost charge, whose 42 px contact capsule hit a frame before the charge box opened in 6 of 9 hits ("Só a investida", user, 2026-10-30; GRD-10; `guardian_contact_test`). Such a move keeps its box open for its whole lunge (`combat_reach_test.test_a_guardian_move_without_body_contact_keeps_its_box_open_while_it_lunges`): the lunge waits `GuardianAttack.lunge_delay` (`GuardianAI.swing_elapsed()`), so the charge stands through the cocked fist (frames 0-6) and runs 455 px/s from 0.5 s, when its box opens, to the clip's end (about 300 px, as the old 260 px/s over the whole swing). Without the delay the golem slid past an Ivo at 62 px before its box opened and the charge never hit. Mashing is answered: every `counter_after_hits` hits between moves, `GuardianAI.provoke()` drops the cooldown and the next move comes at once; and once the hits saturate on a pending recall, `request_recall()` makes the recall move the next one, cadence or not.
- **The call borrows Ivo's instrument.** `Guardian` calls `Player.open_call(song, revealed)`, which sets `MemorinaComponent.call_song`: while it is set the phrase is the only candidate, and matching it emits `call_answered` and leaves the instrument out instead of starting a performance. `Player.close_call(success)` sheathes silently on success and `interrupt()`s otherwise, so a failed answer is the wrong-note vocabulary the player already knows. `PhraseCall` (`scenes/characters/components/`, shared with the teaching creatures since CORE-04) sounds the phrase through its own `MemorinaVoice` on a fixed `note_interval` (0.55 s, a melody rather than six ringing tones) and reports its length; **the answer window is that length plus `GuardianStats.window`**, because the reply cannot be played faster than the call was.
- **The Player↔Guardian contract is small**: the guardian calls `open_call` / `sound_call_note` / `close_call` / `begin_recall` / `learn_song`; it listens to `call_answered`, `sequence_failed`, `skill_recalled`, `skill_recall_missed`. Ivo relays the call to the HUD (`call_opened`, `call_note_sounded`, `call_window_opened`, `call_progress`, `call_answered`, `call_closed`) so the HUD keeps listening to one node.
- **Lucidity begins with a LEAP, not a HUD.** `Guardian._open_lucidity` does not sing where the fighting stopped: it breaks off, throws itself over the top of the frame (`lucidity_leap_height`, 340 px) and comes down `lucidity_leap_distance` (440 px - the camera holds their midpoint, so this is the whole width between them) from Ivo, and only the landing stages the call. The phase change is something you WATCH. **Which way is WORKED OUT, not assumed** (`_leap_target_x`): each direction is measured against the `Arena` the room hands it (`arena_path`, pointed at a sibling `Arena` node on the guardian's instance in the contents scene - level geometry, injected at the composition point; a node-typed export cannot be assigned across a scene-instance boundary, so it is a `NodePath` resolved at `_ready`). Reading `GameCamera.bounds()` for this was the mistake: a RENDERING clamp deciding where a boss comes down, empty in an unbound room and silently moved by any future cutscene camera. Whichever direction ends up further from Ivo wins, so a guardian in a corner goes over his head instead of shuffling deeper into it; a tie goes to the side it already stood on. Taking the side for granted was the bug - a guardian already 250 px away 'leapt' 50 px. **And the leap waits its beat** (`lucidity_delay`, 1 s): a recall is the usual opener and Ivo is in the middle of the move he just remembered, so a guardian that leaps on that frame jumps WITH him, which reads as a coincidence rather than a consequence. While `_leaping` the body is ballistic (the AI is off, `_process_motion` only applies gravity) and **passes through the world above the floor it left** (`collision_mask` 0 until it is descending back past `_leap_floor_y`), because an arena has a ceiling and this arc is meant to leave the frame through it - the Bloom platform swatted the first version down after 34 px. `leapt` / `landed` are wired to a `DustEmitter` in each guardian scene exactly as `ivo.tscn` does, the landing shakes the camera, and `lucidity_leap_timeout` calls from where it stands if the leap never lands.
- **The encounter is staged by `LessonCinematic` and the camera.** `Player.stage_call(caller)` / `unstage_call()` (relayed as `call_staged` / `call_unstaged`) bracket one lucid moment from the window opening to the end of its relapse (or restoration): `GameCamera.frame_pair(other)` (on `call_framed`, which a leap sends at take-off: see the next-but-one bullet) eases the frame to the midpoint of Ivo and the guardian (no zoom - `focus()` while paired only settles `focused`), `LessonCinematic.on_call_staged` dims the world to `call_dim_alpha`, and `MemorinaHud` hides its sheet while the guardian sings. The lesson goes further (`scenes/ui/lesson_cinematic/`, PROCESS_MODE_ALWAYS, sits UNDER the `Creatures` pass in `game.tscn` so the dim spotlights Ivo and the guardian): letterbox bars (since STORY-15 the shared `CanvasLayer/Letterbox`, its `letterbox` export, which cutscenes use too: `systems/cutscenes`), a dimmed world and the piece's title card (`MEMORINA_LEARNED` + `Song.title_key`), which RISES ONLY ONCE THE PHRASE HAS BEEN HEARD: `LessonCinematic.on_note_cue_reached` waits for the song's last cue and then `card_delay` (2.2 s, long enough for the sheet to bow out), so the two never share the frame - hear it, then learn what it is called. It then STAYS for the rest of the track - naming the piece is the point - and leaves with the letterbox; the LESSON owns the layer from its first frame until its frame has closed, so a guardian unstaging its call on `performance_finished` cannot cut that fade short and strand the card, and every way out ends in `_finish()`: hidden AND reset), the camera pushes in slowly for `lesson_push_time` (45 s, longer than any beat so the frame is still closing in at the end of the track), the sheet in the slot bows out once the last note cue has lit (`MemorinaHud._close_lesson_sheet`) because a track runs a minute and the notes are done in six seconds, the guardian's own colour is born at the first cue with a slow, wide pulse (`Guardian.lesson_pulse`, `resources/memory/lesson_pulse_stats.tres`), the REGION lifts its own wells and its own memory to 1.0 across `RegionMemory.lift_time`, and the pair stays framed until the track ends; the guardian answers the finished lesson with its usual pulse. `MemorinaHud` shows only the notes lighting; it no longer carries a banner.
- **A paired frame has NO look-ahead.** `GameCamera` leads the subject's facing by `look_ahead_distance` (96 px), which is right when the frame is about one body and wrong when it is about two: measured, it put a guardian that had just leapt to the far side 5 px off the screen while the camera sat exactly on the pair's midpoint. `_look_ahead_target()` answers 0 while `_pairing`, and the lead eases back when the pair is released.
- **The leap is framed from take-off (GRD-09).** "Câmera na decolagem" (user, 2026-10-30): the camera frames Ivo and the guardian already at the leap's take-off, so the landing, the `leap_land` clip and the bark balloon are in frame. `Guardian._begin_lucidity_leap` calls `_frame()` -> `Player.frame_call(self)` -> `call_framed`, which `game.tscn` wires to `GameCamera.frame_pair` (it was `call_staged`). The call is still staged only at the landing (`_stage()` -> `stage_call`, which calls `frame_call` itself, a no-op when already framed, so a timed-out leap still pairs). The pair blend (`pair_duration` 0.8 s) finishes inside the flight (Bloom and Frost ~1.2-1.7 s), and the midpoint follows the guardian's x, so the frame stops moving on the touchdown frame: 440 px apart, the guardian sits at screen x 100 or 540 with its whole body in view (220 + 60 < 320). `_unstage()` releases on `_framed or _staged`, so a call abandoned mid-leap releases the frame. **The apex is not framed:** the frame blends x only, and fitting 340 px of rise plus a 120-176 px body above Ivo needs a zoom near 0.65, which shows past a one-screen arena's room clamp and draws uneven pixels; the arc leaving through the top stays the design ("throws itself over the top of the frame"). The guardian is horizontally in frame for the whole arc and drops back in from the top. Test: `guardian_leap_framing_test` (framed while leaping, staged when grounded, body inside `view_rect()` at touchdown; without the take-off frame it lands 251 px outside). Evidence: `bugs/the-lucidity-leap-lands-outside-the-frame-so-its-land-clip-is-not-seen`.
- **Hit feedback runs both ways.** `Character.flash(color, seconds)` is the one implementation (a guardian's `hit_flash_color`, its telegraph, its lucid burst, and every body's `hurt_flash_color` on being hit) - script-owned, and safe because nothing keys `Sprite2D:modulate`. `WorldFreeze.hit_stop()` answers `Player.hit_landed` AND `Player.hurt`, a few real milliseconds at near-zero time scale that always return to whatever the recall had set, and both shake the camera (`GameCamera.shake`, bound strengths in `game.tscn`).
- **A hit carries you OUT of what hit you.** `Hitbox` pushes along the direction from its own centre to the target's, which is nearly VERTICAL for a body standing inside it - popped straight up, landing back in the same box, hit again the moment its i-frames lapse. That is how a guardian that lands on Ivo hits him twice. Under `INSIDE_PUSH_X` of sideways the shove becomes decisively sideways instead (`_push_direction`), toward whichever side they already lean or the box's own facing when dead centre; `knockback_lift` is what gets them off the floor. Measured standing dead centre in the Bloom Guardian: two hits in four seconds became one. Ivo's own `knockback_time` / `knockback_damping` (0.3 / 4.0) and his hurtbox's `invulnerability_time` (0.85 s) are the other half of it - a shove that does not clear the bulk is the same bug as no shove at all.
- **An encounter has ONE SLOT, chosen once, and every sheet takes it.** Listen, answer and lesson all happen in the same place: `NoteSheet` is the one place that picks BOTH the x (`encounter_x()`) and the y (`encounter_y()`, `ENCOUNTER_TOP` 40 px) - both HUDs ask it, and two exports holding the same number in two scenes was how the sheet came to jump when the turn passed. It is asked with the side and the HEIGHT the call carried (`Guardian.body_height()`, through `Player.stage_call`). Under `CENTRE_CLEARANCE` (140 px of body) the sheet hangs above the pair with the frame's STAFF - not its box - on the screen's axis (`staff_center()`), because the art's left third is the clef ornament and centring the box leaves the notes, the only part anyone reads, off to the right. Over it, a centred sheet would sit on the guardian's head (the camera frames the pair with their feet near the bottom, so height alone decides: the Bloom tree-stag is 120 px and wears it, the Frost golem is 176 and does not), and the sheet stands aside at `SIDE_MARGIN` on the side away from it. Either way the choice is made when the call opens and HELD for the whole encounter. Inside the frame everything the player reads shares the staff's axis: the message, `CurePips`, the time bar; the `draw_memorina` key hangs just under the frame. A sheet that hops between one beat and the next is what made these moments read as clutter - nothing moves now but the turn. `GuardianCallHud` (`scenes/ui/guardian_call_hud/`) slides down into the slot tinted in its season: "Listen" while the revealed notes light and pop as they sound, `CurePips` under the staff showing `cure_done` of `cure_total` answers - and it slides away when the window opens, leaving `MemorinaHud` to appear in the same place, the sheet the player already knows, pre-filled with the phrase dimmed, a time bar draining DARK across the parchment (gold on gold is invisible) from `GuardianFight.window_fraction()` - **the fight owns the clock and the HUD only draws it** (`call_window_progress`, relayed through Ivo like every other call signal), because a HUD counting its own seconds down is a second truth that agrees with the deadline only by coincidence, and the `draw_memorina` key blinking (`KeyGlyph`) until the instrument is out; `call_progress` lights the phrase back note by note, a failure flashes it, success tints it green. **The instrument stays in while the guardian sings**: `Player.open_call` sheathes it and `_call_listening` refuses the draw until `open_call_window` - a call is heard out before it is answered. `KeyGlyph` (`scenes/ui/key_glyph/`) is the one place that turns an action into a key on screen; both prompts use it.
- **The recall (QTE) is a phase, not a dice roll.** `GuardianAI` schedules the move carrying an `AbilityRecallStats` every `GuardianStats.recall_after_attacks` ordinary moves (its `weight` is 0, so it is never picked at random), and every move first TELEGRAPHS (`GuardianAttack.telegraph` seconds standing still while the sprite pulses `telegraph_color`) before it swings - a swing nobody could read is a sucker punch, not a challenge. The hitbox takes each move's `damage`/`knockback_*` at swing start. **A memory can take more than one press.** A double jump is jump and then jump AGAIN, so asked for with both feet planted it is `AbilityRecallStats.grounded_steps` presses and the moment TEACHES the whole move; caught already airborne it is the single press that is left. `airborne_finish` says the last press only counts off the ground - one given standing is simply not counted, and the ordinary jump it also performs is what earns the step - and `step_window` puts fresh real seconds on the clock for each press but the last. Where the feet are is the BODY's judgement, pushed into `arm()`/`notify()` by `Player`, never read by the component. `RecallPrompt` asks for them ONE AT A TIME in the same place - the key that landed reads as pressed for a beat, then the next is there on its own fresh ring - because two keys side by side looked like a chord to play at once, which is not what a double jump is; `RecallAura` flares on each. This replaced waiting for someone else to put Ivo in the air (`requires_airborne`), which meant the moment could simply never come. A miss costs `miss_damage` straight through `Health` (i-frames must not hide it). **A move reaches inside a BOX**: `GuardianAttack.attack_range` to the side and `attack_height` up or down from the guardian's own feet, never a radius - a swing that landed on someone 60px overhead because the hypotenuse was short is how a boss looks silly, and a player bouncing on its head must not be inside a ground sweep. **A guardian never stands still, and never dithers.** It picks the move that SUITS the moment (`GuardianAI._situational_pick`): one that reaches a player overhead if it owns one, its longest when they are well beyond its spacing, the weighted roll otherwise - and then KEEPS that choice until it is thrown or a new opinion arrives. Re-rolling every frame looks like variety and is the opposite: whichever move happens to be in range wins every race, so the long one is all you ever see. Between swings it holds spacing (`GuardianStats.comfort_distance`): gives ground to a player who closes in, drifts back toward one who backs off, paces when neither, at `pace_speed` of its walk. A player on its head (within `GuardianAI.overhead_band` of its x, out of reach above) is LUNGED out from under (`step_out_speed`) and punished on the landing - the cooldown it was hiding behind is dropped the moment they come down - and it never walks under one it cannot reach until `OVERHEAD_PATIENCE` runs out.

  **Every decision taken from a distance is COMMITTED**, because a player pogoing on a guardian crosses its thresholds three times a second and an AI that re-decides on each crossing turns on the spot: the chase and the facing keep their side inside `TURN_BAND`; "out of reach overhead" is sticky in both axes (`_update_reach`: true the instant they rise past the tallest move, false only after `REACH_RELEASE` of that height for `ESCAPE_RELEASE_TIME`); the escape has a minimum dwell and a wider exit band than its entry; the spacing mode is entered under `give_ground_distance` or past `COMFORT_FAR` x comfort and left only back at the comfort distance. Measured: the same bouncing player took a guardian from 32 facing flips in 6 s to 0. **A move's `attack_range` must not exceed the reach its clip's hitbox keys** - the burst was thrown from 140 px with a 70 px hitbox and the recall never came (`docs/knowledge/bugs/recall-move-range-exceeds-hitbox-reach.md`). `tests/scenes/combat/combat_reach_test.gd` now asserts more than that, on the boxes the clips key through the whole hit window: since a move is thrown at any distance up to `attack_range`, it must touch a grounded Ivo at every distance from its contact box's reach out to `attack_range`. A lunge or leap only has to touch him somewhere.
- **An air jump pressed on the way UP has to add, not assign.** `DoubleJumpComponent` sets `velocity.y` outright, which is right at the apex and wrong mid-rise: measured, a recalled double jump pressed at -184 px/s got a 222 px/s launch, a gain of 38 that nobody can see - and a chained recall FORCES that press, because a world at 0.2 time scale makes waiting for the apex impossible. Rising, the launch is added to whatever the body had (`rising_boost`, 0.5); at or past the apex nothing changes, so ordinary play is untouched.
- **`AbilityRecallComponent`** is the player ability itself, armed with the attack's stats and ticked in **real** seconds (`delta / maxf(Engine.time_scale, 0.001)`; under a menu hold the delta is 0, so it stops rather than dividing by 0). `WorldFreeze` is the only writer of `Engine.time_scale` and `get_tree().paused`: `slow()` / `restore()` (the recall), `hit_stop()`, `freeze()` / `thaw()` (a performance, scale 1) and `hold()` / `release()` (a menu, scale 0). Every change goes through `_running_scale()`, in the order held 0, frozen 1, hit-stop, recall slow, 1, so a thaw or release returns to whatever is still on and a hit-stop ending under a hold leaves 0. The same press that recalls the skill performs it: `PlayerInput.roll_pressed`/`jump_pressed` feed the recall before `_try_roll`/`_try_jump` run, and `SaveSystem.unlock_skill` happens in that handler - which also `clear_knockback()`s and cancels the landing recovery, because the blow that FORCED the memory must not also swallow the move it bought (both gate `_try_jump`/`_try_roll`). A recalled dodge with no direction held goes AWAY from the body that threw the move (`Player._dodge_axis`, `RECALL_ESCAPE_TIME`): Ivo is facing the charge, and a dodge into it is not a dodge. `WorldFreeze.slow()` eases the clock over `slow_ramp` with a tween that ignores the time scale it drives. The recall opens on its CUE, never merely at swing start: `AbilityRecallStats.trigger_distance` waits for the body throwing the move to come that close (`Player.begin_recall` takes the `source`), so the world slows when the blow is about to land and the window is not spent watching a charge cross the arena. Show, not tell: no caption. `RecallAura` (`scenes/characters/ivo/recall_aura/`, a child of Ivo on the creature layer, wired from Ivo's own signals in `ivo.tscn`) draws the colour born around the head - wavy rays trembling in real time, flaring white on a recall, dropping red on a miss - and `RecallPrompt` is only the bound key (`KeyGlyph`) blinking inside a ring that drains with the real-time window, floating above Ivo's head (it samples the canvas transform itself: a prompt that rides a moving body cannot wait for `focused`).
- **`GuardianAnimationResolver` is shared**: `IDLE, WALK, ATTACK_1..3, HURT, LUCID, LUCID_ENTER, PLAY_SONG, RELAPSE, PERFORM, PERFORM_END, RESTORED, LEAP_CROUCH, LEAP_RISE, LEAP_FALL, LEAP_LAND` (all in `CLIPS`; GRD-08 below). `GuardianAttack.clip_index` maps to `attack_clip_for()`, and `Guardian._assert_clip_durations` walks that mapping. Since GRD-07 every move plays its own clip: Frost's charge is `clip_index` 1 (`attack_2`, 1.1667 s like its `duration`); until then it played the swipe. The lucidity leap outranks everything in `resolve()`, then the phase (leap clips: "Our own art (GRD-07)").
- **A guardian's colour is the fight made visible, script-owned, never keyed in a clip** (`Guardian._update_shield`): the rest level is `lerp(corrupted, 1.0, lucidity())`, hits climb a little above it under pressure, each note of the call *breathes* the shield to full and swells its radius (`note_swell`) with the sprite bobbing, the open window trembles slowly (`tremble_hz`, a sine) above a floor that climbs with every note answered, a relapse holds bright then drains to the new rest (or snaps to it with a hard burst after a failure), and restoration keeps it all. The guardian's voice is the same notes an octave down (`Call/Voice/AudioStreamPlayer.pitch_scale = 0.5`) and it groans the mistake sound as it relapses. A shield only lifts memory TOWARD 1, so in a mostly remembered region none of this reads without a well of forgetting under it - and **the well belongs to the REGION, not to the guardian** (below).
- **A PLACE is not the boss's to own.** The wells of forgetting are `MemorySource` children of a `RegionMemory` node in the REGION's own scene (`scenes/world/rooms/region_memory.gd`, authored in `home_village.tscn` / `frost_edge.tscn`), because `MemoryField.sample()` skips a source that is not `is_visible_in_tree()` and `Room.deactivate()` hides a room's contents - so a well authored inside a room, or inside the guardian standing in it, can only ever be felt from that one room, and a region is very often several. Measured: from Downtown, with Bloom Hollow deactivated, the arena's well still reads 0.05 and downtown's own 0.10, exactly as they read from inside the arena. Death marks (design section 4.3) hang here too. `RegionMemory` owns the authored level, the wells and the lift; it never reads `SaveSystem` - whether the guardian is restored is the `Region`'s judgement, pushed in through `restore(seconds)` (at or below 0 it is simply already whole, which is a later visit).
- **A guardian is met when its fight begins**: `_on_player_entered` calls `SaveSystem.meet_guardian(stats.id)`, which makes its notebook entry present (`systems/notebook`). `NotebookWatcher` pulls `Guardian.fight_at` and `Player.is_in_lesson()` to hold the HUD quill until both are over.
- **Restoration is persistent, and the region hears it from the SAVE** (`Enums.Guardian`, `PlayerData.restored_guardians`, `SaveSystem.restore_guardian`). `SaveSystem.guardian_restored` is the channel rather than `Guardian.restored`, because it works whatever order the world loaded in and whether or not the arena's room is still resident; `Region` listens, and `Guardian._restore()` does nothing to the world but record the fact. `Region.memory_changed` re-emits `RegionMemory.changed`, and **`Game` is the ONE writer of `MemoryField.baseline`** - it follows the region it is showing, because a restoration happens while the player is standing in it and not only at the next doorway. A restored guardian starts in `RESTORED` on every later visit and never fights again.
- **A region's memory can also step with the others' restorations** (MEM-13, WORLD-11, 2026-10-13; design 04 section 2: the village falls to 0.6 from the second restored guardian). `RegionMemory.steps` (`Dictionary[int, float]`) maps a restored-guardian count to a baseline; `RegionMemory.level_for(authored, steps, restored)` (static, pure) is the value of the highest key at or below the count, else `authored`. `Region._ready` applies it from `SaveSystem.restored_guardian_count()` after mounting the marks, then the own-guardian check; `Region` listens to `guardian_restored` when its own guardian is unrestored or it has steps, and the handler always re-derives the step after its own-guardian branch. `follow_restorations` sets the level at once, with no tween (the player is in another region when it happens) and emits `changed` only when the level differs, so `RegionPresence` hides the leavers through the existing `memory_changed`. The region's own restoration always wins: `restore()` sets `_whole` before its tween, so a step neither pulls a running lift down nor lowers a whole region. Data: `home_village.tscn` `steps = {2: 0.6}` (architect plan, 2026-10-13). Tests: `region_memory_test`, `region_test`.
- **Three moves each, and one of them is a leap.** `GuardianAttack.leap_impulse` with `lunge_speed` is a jump at the player that lands past them, which is how a light guardian changes sides; a heavy one leaves it at 0 - not every boss should jump. Bloom: lash (close), burst (the launcher carrying the recall), pounce (a leap, 48px up and 192px across). Frost: swipe (close), charge (the lunge carrying the recall), slam (a tall keyed hitbox and `attack_height` 150, so the golem swats a player jumping just above it). Neither answers a pogo on its head: "O pogo é a brecha" (user, 2026-10-29), so bouncing on a guardian is a valid way out of its moves; it steps out from under and goes back to them (below, "Fixes after the GRD-07 playtest"). **A swing keeps the side it started on**: `GuardianAI.facing_intent()` answers 0 while swinging, so a pounce or a charge carries past a player who rolls under it instead of turning in the air to follow him (`bugs/a-guardian-lunge-turned-to-follow-a-player-who-rolled-under-it`). The telegraph still tracks him.

A jump step short of the last is no longer counted on the press but on `JumpComponent.jumped` (`_on_jumped_for_recall`), so a press that some other gate refuses stays buffered and counts when it lifts him; the last step still counts on the press, so its unlock reaches `_try_jump` on the same frame. Test: `player_recall_test`.
- **The pogo is the way out, and the guardian keeps pressing** (`bugs/a-pogo-on-the-frost-golems-head-stops-it-attacking`). The user's decision, 2026-10-29: "O pogo é a brecha". The slam keeps `attack_height` 150 and does not reach a player on the golem's 176 px head. Instead, after `GuardianAI.OVERHEAD_PATIENCE` (1.5 s) of a player out of reach overhead with no move thrown, the guardian ends its escape once the step-out lunge is done and he is outside `overhead_band`, walks back in, and throws its next move where he will come down: `_in_range` then ignores height, but never while he is inside `overhead_band`. Any telegraph resets the patience. Measured in `guardian_ai_test.test_a_pogo_on_its_head_does_not_stop_its_moves` (Frost stats, Ivo bouncing 180-260 px above a fixed x): before, no move in 20 s of play; now a telegraph inside 4 s, from outside the band.
- **"On top" is the guardian's body, not a constant** (GRD-07 review). `OVERHEAD_BAND` (64 px) was sized for the old wide Bloom; with the stag's contact half-width at 16-24 px a jump attack from 24-64 px beside it set off the escape lunge. `GuardianAI.overhead_band` is now `overhead_band_for(body width, Ivo's body width)`, half of both bodies side by side, set by `Guardian._on_player_entered` from `Character.body_rect()`: Bloom 27 px (r16 + r11), Frost 45 px (r34 + r11). Test: `guardian_ai_test.test_only_a_player_over_its_body_is_on_top_of_it`.
- **A flinch faces what hit it** (playtest: the stag flipped `flip_h` mid-flinch 5 times, giving ground with its back to Ivo). A hit outside a move turns the guardian to the source (outside `TURN_BAND`) and holds that facing for the hurt clip's length (`_flinch_left`); a telegraph still squares up to Ivo. Test: `guardian_facing_test.test_a_flinch_faces_what_hit_it_and_keeps_that_side`.
- **A restored guardian watches Ivo** (playtest: the restored golem stood with its back to Ivo entering from the west). In RESTORED it faces `EnemySight.player` whenever he is in sight and outside `TURN_BAND`, so it turns to the side he enters from. Test: `guardian_facing_test.test_a_restored_guardian_turns_to_ivo`.
