# The world layout

**Never break:** a room's bounds are a whole number of 640x384 screens on the world grid, rooms never overlap, and interiors stay in the band at y 12000 and below. A region scene, a contents scene and a `.room` file are written once by the generator; only Room triggers are regenerated.

Design 04 (the approved map, "Approve as proposed", 2026-10-08), WORLD-05.

- **The grid.** Macro cell (c, r) is world x [640c, 640c+640), y [384r, 384r+384): one screen, 20x12 tiles. A surface room's walkable floor is the top of grid row 9 of its bottom screen row (y = 384r + 288). Every region scene sits at (0, 0) in `game.tscn`; its rooms carry their world position. The song trials stay at y 5600..8100 (debug only).
- **Openings.** Rooms share edges. A side opening is air in rows 5..8 of the shared screen row with a floor at row 9; a vertical opening keeps the named columns open. Every other edge is walled: a room's open top with no room above gets a 152 px sky band under an invisible ceiling, and every edge facing the void an invisible wall (WORLD-34, `systems/rooms` "Edges"). A new room needs nothing for this. A room inside rock (`ROCK_SETS`) gets a drawn ceiling instead: the generator closes its first row. An opening drawn onto the void is fixed in the map (the audit's `OPEN_EDGE`). The current room is the one Ivo's feet are in (`systems/rooms`).
- **Interiors** (door rooms, design 04 section 4) live at y 12000 (floor y 12288), x = region index * 12000 + n * 1600, in the generator's region order (home_village, threshold, frost_edge, spring_woods, frozen_lake, wind_orchards, summer_caldera). They never touch another room. A region keeps to its 12000 px x band, less two rings (`RING_GAP`, 320 px: rooms that do not touch are two `RoomRing.WIDTH` apart, `room_ring_test`): a slot whose room would reach past that starts a further row 2000 px lower (`INTERIOR_ROW_SPACING`, past the tallest 4-screen interior), n from 0 again. WORLD-11 (2026-10-13): slot 7 at x 11200 ends 160 px from threshold_farmhouse (x 12000) and slot 8 lies in the threshold's band, so the village's eighth and ninth interiors start row 1: the mill house at (0, 14000), the house of notes at (1600, 14000). A new game starts in `village_house` at (320, 12286).
- **The generator.** `tools/maps/gen_world_shells.py` holds the tables (regions with name key, season, memory, guardian and wall material; rooms with macro rects; side and vertical openings; benches; interiors; doors; barriers; fact grates) and writes Room triggers (always), region scenes, contents scenes and `.room` shells (once). A new shell names its region's tileset (`REGION_TILESETS`, with `ROOM_TILESETS` for the lighthouse, the cistern and the two ash rooms, `systems/rooms`), an interior's shell is planks (`%`), and a new region scene gets its region's backdrop set and horizon (`REGION_BACKDROPS`, `systems/backdrops`). Every scene gets a stable `uid=` from `world_uid_pool.txt` (minted by Godot; uids already in a scene header are skipped), so `SceneKey` save keys survive renames. Every region scene gets its set and one horizon (`REGION_BACKDROPS`) and every contents scene a `Backdrop`: no override outdoors (the region's set), a rock wall inside rock (`ROCK_SETS`), its region's cave set underground (`UNDERGROUND_SETS`), `interior_timber` indoors (WORLD-28, `systems/backdrops`) unless `INTERIOR_SETS` names another (frost_wreck_hold `ship_hold`). A new room's cuts against its neighbours' backdrops must pass `backdrop_seams_test`. To add a room: add its row and openings, run the tool, add the printed contents scenes to `tools/smoke/scenes.txt`, and place it in its region scene by hand (region scenes are not regenerated).
- **The maps are the truth for the tables.** The generator writes a shell once, so a later map edit can leave a table row behind.
