# Sound effects

Updated 2026-11-17. How gameplay, world and menu sounds are described and played. Built by SND-01 (2026-11-16); the levels are a first pass not yet judged by ear (SND-02). The credits-roll role in the plan was dropped: every source is CC0, credited in `CREDITS.md`.

**Never break:** A sound is a `SoundCue`; only `SfxEmitter` makes SFX players, with the bus from the cue. World emitters inherit their owner's pause; `IvoSounds` is explicit PAUSABLE; only `MenuSounds` plays under a hold. `Settings` stays the only writer of bus volume; `SfxMuffle` is the only writer of the `SfxWorld` effect.

## Contract

- **Buses** (`default_bus_layout.tres`, names in `AudioBuses`): `Sfx` sends to `Master`; `SfxWorld` sends to `Sfx`, volume 0 dB, one `AudioEffectLowPassFilter` named "Muffle" (cutoff 20 kHz, off). The Effects slider sets `Sfx`, so it governs `SfxWorld` too; `SfxWorld`'s volume is never written (`audio_buses_test`). `audio_buses_test` checks every scene `AudioStreamPlayer` and `AudioStreamPlayer2D`.
- **`SoundCue`** (`resources/audio/sound_cue.gd`): `stream` (normally an `AudioStreamRandomizer` in no-repeat mode: variants, pitch and volume jitter come from the engine), `volume_db`, `pitch_scale` (the player's base pitch, set when the emitter makes it; the randomizer jitters around it), `max_voices` 1..4 (the player's `max_polyphony`; a new voice steals the oldest), `cooldown` (real seconds between two starts of the cue, across every emitter; 0 never gates), `world` (`bus()` is `SfxWorld` if true, else `Sfx`). `length()` is the longest variant's seconds at `pitch_scale`, the random pitch not counted. Only world-object cues set `world`: doors, pickups, found pieces, seeds, splashes, the bench. Ivo's body, creatures and menus stay on `Sfx`, unmuffled ("Characters never freeze", `systems/greyhush`).
- **`CueGate`** (`resources/audio/cue_gate.gd`, pure): per-cue cooldown keyed by the cue's instance id, on an injected clock (default `CueGate.real_seconds`, wall time, so it runs under a hold). A refused start does not extend the cooldown.
- **`SfxEmitter`** (`scenes/audio/sfx_emitter.gd`, Node2D, group `sfx_emitters`):
  - `play(cue, db_offset)`, `play_at(cue, point, db_offset)`, `stop_all()`, `player_of(cue)`, signal `cue_played(cue)` per started voice.
  - One player per cue, made on its first voice, as its child: `AudioStreamPlayer`, or `AudioStreamPlayer2D` when `positional` (`max_distance` 800 px, `attenuation` 1, `panning_strength` 0.6). `bus` comes from `cue.bus()`, `max_polyphony` from `max_voices`. `play` on a positional emitter sounds at the emitter; `play_at` moves the cue's player to the point.
  - One static `CueGate` for every emitter, so a cue struck by three creatures in one frame sounds once.
  - It starts nothing while `can_process()` is false (paused tree or DISABLED owner): a non-positional player would sound through the pause, and a positional one would start late (`gotchas/an-audio-player-that-cannot-process-still-starts-a-voice`).
  - On `NOTIFICATION_DISABLED` (an idled room) it stops every voice, so nothing comes back with the room. Under a freeze or a hold its voices pause where they are and resume with the world.
- **Ivo** (`scenes/characters/ivo/ivo_sounds.gd`, `IvoSounds`, the last child of Ivo in `ivo.tscn`, explicit PAUSABLE, with a non-positional `SfxEmitter` child):
  - Data: `IvoSoundSet` (`resources/audio/sfx/ivo/ivo_sounds.tres`): `surfaces` (`Dictionary[int, SurfaceSounds]` by `Enums.Surface`, each a `step` and a soft `land` cue), `land_hard`, `jump`, `double_jump`, `climb_grab`, `climb_step`, `swing`, `hurt`, `death`, `rest`, `soft_land_min_speed` (200 px/s), `soft_land_full_speed` (800 px/s, Ivo's `hard_land_speed`). One `SoundCue` `.tres` per sound beside it; variant sets are `AudioStreamRandomizer`s in no-repeat mode with `random_pitch` 1.05 and `random_volume_offset_db` 1.
  - One-shots, wired from Ivo's own signals with `from="."`: `jumped`, `double_jumped` (the jump file at `pitch_scale` 1.25), `landed` (new: `LandingComponent.landed(position, impact_speed)` for every landing that is not hard, re-emitted by `Player`; silent under `soft_land_min_speed`, levelled by `ImpactLevel.db`, the floor's `land` cue), `hard_landed` (`land_hard`), `swung(phase_index)` (new, emitted per attack phase start), `hurt` (silent once dead: a killing hit emits `died` first), `died`, `sat_down` (`rest`, a world cue; an arrival's `sit()` emits nothing). A corpse's landing is silent.
  - Doors and takes (step 6), all world cues on his emitter: `door_entered` plays `door_open[door.look]`; `arrived_through(door)` (new, emitted by `Player.arrive_at` under the fade) plays `door_close[door.look]` of the door he comes out of; `door_knocked(door)` (new, after `door.knock()`) plays `door_knock`; `took(what: Player.Taken)` (new: `ITEM` after `pickup.take()`, `LETTER` or `SONGBOOK` after `found.take()`, `SEED` after `cache.take()`) plays `taken[what]`. `door_open` and `door_close` hold every `Door.LOOKS` key, a null entry silent, and a look is silent both ways or neither (`ivo_sounds_test`).
  - Steps: its `_physics_process` runs after Ivo's and after his `AnimationTree` (a child before it, physics callback). It feeds `Player.animation_state()`, `animation_position()` and `animation_length()` (`AnimationDriver.position()` / `length()`, 0 until the tree has started the driver's clip) to a `StepClock` with `StepContacts.contacts_of(clip)` (`ivo_footfalls.tres`). Each contact crossed plays `climb_step` while `is_climbing()`, else the step cue of `Player.floor_surface()`; NONE is silent. `is_climbing()` turning true plays `climb_grab`.
- **`StepClock`** (`resources/audio/step_clock.gd`, pure): contacts crossed in `(last, now]` of a looping clip. A new clip starts before its first contact (a contact at 0 sounds as the clip starts); a jump back over half the clip is the loop wrap (contacts on both sides counted); a smaller jump back reports nothing. Both bounds carry a 0.00001 s epsilon (`gotchas/a-float32-time-lies-past-the-same-float64-time`).
- **`StepContacts`** (`resources/audio/step_contacts.gd`): `contacts: Dictionary[StringName, PackedFloat32Array]`, clip -> contact seconds. Every key is a `PlayerAnimationResolver` const naming a looping state, every contact inside its clip (`step_contacts_test`). The contacts are the art's planted frames, measured on the sheets (2026-11-16): run frames 0 and 5 land after the airborne frames 7 and 3-4, so the run's rhythm is uneven in the art itself (5 frames, then 3); walk frames 0 and 4 are its two wide stances.
- **A step sounds on its impact** (SND-07, 2026-11-16): "passos não sincronizados com a pisada" (user, 2026-11-16). The contacts were right; the files were late. Cut at -40 dB, the wood, water and stone steps kept 40-75 ms of scuff before the heel. `cut_sfx.py` now cuts steps and landings at their first sample 14 dB under the peak (`STEP_ATTACK_DB`), read from 30 ms before the picked slice (`ATTACK_LEAD`) so a slice that opened inside its impact keeps the start, with at least 4 ms faded in (no file starts on a click, `bugs/eight-step-and-landing-files-start-mid-impact-with-a-click`). Every step and soft landing reaches a quarter of its peak within 50 ms of its start (the slowest: `land_earth_01` 47 ms, `step_water_03` 36 ms).
- **Surfaces.** `Enums.Surface { NONE, EARTH, STONE, SNOW, WOOD, SHALLOW_WATER }`, append only. `BiomeTileset.step_surfaces: PackedByteArray` by `Look`, checked by `problems()`; NONE falls back to the cell's `Enums.Ground`. `StepSurfaces.of(look, ground, biome)` (pure), `RoomMap.look_at(cell)`, `RoomMapNode.surface_at(cell)`.
  - `StepSurfaceProbe` (`scenes/characters/components/`, child of Ivo): `Player._after_move` calls `update()` before the landing check. It reads the first floor collision of the last `move_and_slide()`: a room's `Solid` `TileMapLayer` answers `surface_at(get_coords_for_body_rid(rid))`; any other body its `step_surface` property, else STONE. A frame with no floor contact keeps the last surface.
  - Shallow water wins: `WaterVolume.wade` (true in `water_shallow.tscn`) calls `enter_wading` / `exit_wading` on the body; `Player` delegates to the probe's count, never below 0 (an Area2D reports a disabled or removed body as exited).
- **Water** (`scenes/world/environment/water/splash_sounds.gd`, `SplashSounds`, a Node2D child of `water_pool.tscn` and `water_shallow.tscn` with a positional `SfxEmitter`): `WaterBody.surface_splashed(point, speed)` (new, emitted by `splash()` after its dry and roofed return, `point` on the waterline) plays `SplashSoundSet.cue_for(speed)` (`small` under `large_speed` 500 px/s, else `large`) at the point, levelled by `ImpactLevel.db(speed, quiet_speed 120, full_speed 800)`. Data: `resources/audio/sfx/world/splash_sounds.tres`. A lake has no volume, so no splash.
- **Creatures and guardians** (`scenes/characters/components/body_sounds.gd`, `BodySounds`, a Node2D instanced from `body_sounds.tscn` as the last child of every creature and guardian scene, with a positional `SfxEmitter`): data `BodySoundSet` (`struck`, `blocked`, `death`; null is silent) on `EnemyStats.sounds` and `GuardianStats.sounds`, so a species stays a scene plus stats.
  - `Enemy._apply_stats` pushes `stats.sounds` into it and connects `struck` (new, emitted after `_wound`), `blocked` and `died`. `on_struck` is silent once `on_died` ran: a killing hit emits `died` inside `_wound`, before `struck`.
  - `Guardian._ready` pushes `stats.sounds` and connects `struck` (new, emitted on a PRESSURE hit, the hit that opens lucidity included). A guardian never dies or blocks.
  - Every stats `.tres` has a set with a struck cue, every creature's a death cue no longer than the shortest `CorruptionEdge.vanish_time`, and every creature and guardian scene a `BodySounds` (`body_sound_set_test`).
- **Length rules** (`sound_cue_test`, `body_sound_set_test`): every world cue under `resources/audio/sfx/` lasts at most 1.0 s; the songbook's cue at most `Player.lesson_lead_in` (0.5 s), because the lesson's freeze pauses Ivo's emitter; a creature's death at most the dissolve (0.6 s), because the emitter is freed with the body.
- **Menus** (step 8). `MenuSounds` (`scenes/ui/menu/menu_sounds.{gd,tscn}`, a Node with a non-positional `SfxEmitter` child) is the last child of the `Screens` root and of the `Title` root, exports `sounds` (`MenuSoundSet`, `resources/audio/sfx/menu/menu_sounds.tres`: `move`, `confirm`, `back`, `open`, `close`, `page`), `menu_input` and `scope` (its owner's root). It inherits ALWAYS from `Screens`, so it sounds under a hold; no `CanvasLayer` changes mode.
  - `MenuCueGate` (`resources/audio/menu_cue_gate.gd`, pure): at most one cue per process frame, by priority OPEN = CLOSE > PAGE > BACK > CONFIRM > MOVE (a tie keeps the first asked). A focus change is a MOVE only in a frame that also had a navigation press, in either order, so a focus grab (a screen opening, a step back, a panel) and a press at the end of a list are silent. `MenuSounds._process` flushes it.
  - Navigation presses: `MenuInput.focus_stepped(direction)` (new: the press edge of `ui_up` / `ui_down`, held per direction like `_page_edge`, re-read when a capture ends) and `page_pressed`.
  - MOVE: `Viewport.gui_focus_changed` for a control inside `scope`. CONFIRM: on the first focus of each `BaseButton` in scope, `MenuSounds` connects its `pressed`. The dialogue box is outside both scopes, so its buttons never click.
  - `Screens.screen_changed(from, to)` (new, the end of `_apply`) plays OPEN or CLOSE, silent when either side is MAP. `Screens.stepped_back(kind)` (new, when a back press made `step_back()` true) plays BACK, except for NOTEBOOK: its step back starts the shutting strip, and the CLOSE comes with `screen_changed` as the book lands shut. `Title.stepped_back` (new) plays BACK. `Notebook.page_turned(direction)` (new, as the turn strip starts) plays PAGE; the tab press and the pressed tab's CONFIRM fall in the same frame, so the PAGE wins.
  - The rebind capture is silent past its CONFIRM: `MenuInput` consumes every key and pad event while capturing and emits neither `focus_stepped` nor `back_pressed`, so a captured arrow or Esc plays nothing. A volume step is left or right on a row whose focus neighbours are itself: no focus change, so no MOVE over the Settings preview (`menu_sounds_test`).
- **Notice** (step 8): `NoticeToast.slide_sound` (`resources/audio/sfx/menu/notice.tres`, on `Sfx`) plays through its `SfxEmitter` child in `show_notice`; `withdraw()` stops it, so a notice shown again after a talk slides in with it again. The toast is pausable, so a hold pauses the voice and a show under a pause starts none.
- **Greyhush muffle** (step 9, option (c)). `SfxMuffle` (`scenes/audio/sfx_muffle.gd`, `World/SfxMuffle` in `game.tscn`, PAUSABLE by inheritance, `subject` Ivo) is the only writer of the `SfxWorld` "Muffle" low-pass. Each process frame it samples `MemoryField.sample(subject.global_position)` (the clean CPU field; shields are rendering-only and do not count) and sets the cutoff to `SfxMuffleMath.cutoff(memory)`, the effect on below memory 1 and off at 1. A freeze or a hold keeps the last cutoff, as the paused world voices keep their place. `_exit_tree` leaves it off at 20 kHz, because the bus outlives the `game.tscn` swap and a title has no memory.
  - `SfxMuffleMath` (`resources/audio/sfx_muffle_math.gd`, pure): `cutoff(m) = 20000 * (1200 / 20000) ^ (1 - clamp(m, 0, 1))` Hz, even steps in pitch like `MusicMix.cutoff_hz` (each tenth of memory lowers it about 0.41 octave): 1.0 -> 20 kHz, 0.8 -> 11.4 kHz, 0.5 -> 4.9 kHz, 0.2 -> 2.1 kHz, 0 -> 1.2 kHz. `is_open(m)` is `m >= 1`.
- **Process modes.** An emitter inherits its owner's mode, so it dies with the owner and pauses with it. `IvoSounds` (step 4) is explicit PAUSABLE: a DISABLED Ivo (door, arrival and hazard fades) still sounds, and a freeze or a hold pauses it (`gotchas/an-explicit-process-mode-escapes-a-disabled-ancestor`). `MenuSounds` is ALWAYS by inheritance from the `Screens` root, the only SFX that plays under a hold; audio ignores `Engine.time_scale`, and its cooldowns run on `CueGate.real_seconds`. At the title it inherits the title's PAUSABLE, which never pauses. `NoticeToast`'s emitter and `SfxMuffle` inherit PAUSABLE.

## Decisions

- **Contacts** (`ivo_footfalls.tres`), from the drawn feet, measured as the lowest opaque rows of each 128 px frame (ground row 95): `run` (8 frames, 0.667 s) frames 0 and 5, where a foot comes down on the ground row after the airborne frames 7 and 4 (0, 0.417 s); `walk` (8, 0.8 s) frames 0 and 4, the wide double supports (0, 0.4 s); `brace` (10, 1.0 s) frames 0, 5, 7 and 9, where a foot is planted after a lifted frame (0, 0.5, 0.7, 0.9 s); `climb` and `climb_back` (4, 0.6 s) frames 1 and 3, the hand at its highest reach (0.15, 0.45 s).
- **Biome surfaces** (Look EARTH, STONE, WOOD, WALL): every biome EARTH, STONE, WOOD, NONE, except `frozen_lake` (earth is SNOW) and `summer_caldera_ash` (its STONE look is loose ash scree, so EARTH: a soft crunch, not a hard click). WALL is a back wall and never collides, so NONE.
- **Landings**: no landing files exist for stone, snow or wood, so their soft landing is the surface's own step set 4 dB over the step (-4 dB against -8); earth uses `land_earth`; shallow water uses `land_mud`. `step_mud` is unused until a surface needs it. Ivo's hurt is `hurt_low` (an old man's voice). The double jump reuses `jump` at `pitch_scale` 1.25.
- **Doors by look**: `door` (a wooden building door) opens with `door_open` and shuts with `door_close`, the old wooden door; `hatch_up` and `hatch_down` (a ladder hatch) with `door_open_alt` and `door_close_alt`, lighter; `passage`, `stairwell` and `breach` (an opening with nothing to swing) are silent. The plan had only an open table; `door_close` on arrival uses the two close files.
- **Block**: no blocked file was cut, so `blocked` is the three struck variants at `pitch_scale` 0.75 and -6 dB. Guardians use `guardian_struck` only. Struck, blocked, guardian struck and the splashes have a 0.05 s cooldown, so one swing through several bodies sounds once.
- **Levels** (2026-11-17). A level is the loudest momentary loudness (EBU R128, 400 ms) in LUFS, before bus volume, spatial falloff and `ImpactLevel`. `cut_sfx.py` gains every file to -24 LUFS; the files of one cue share one gain, held lower when a peak would pass -1 dBFS (steps on stone -27, `move` -29, `death` -26.5), so a cue's variants play alike. Each cue's `volume_db` is then `SFX_TARGETS` in `tools/audio/measure_levels.py` minus its files' level, written by `--apply`, and never above 0 (`sound_cue_test`). The targets, by tier: steps -37, climb step -35; soft landings -32, jump, double jump and climb grab -31; swing and rest -29; menu move -40, confirm and back -33, open, close and page -31, notice -29; doors and knock -27, pickup -26, letter, seeds and songbook -28, splash small -29, large -25; struck and creature death -26, blocked -28, guardian struck -24, `land_hard` -25, hurt -23, death -24 (reaches -26.5). Before, door open played at -14 and pickup at -17, 20 dB over the steps; the steps on snow and water at -31. The story stand-ins (`synth_story_placeholders.py`, `LEVELS`) are leveled the same way: the guitar -28 beside the Memorina's notes (-26 to -34, not leveled), the hums -26, `gate_bolt` -27, the room sounds -29 to -32; they were -8 to -12. "Os sons de passos também parecem meio altos demais [...] Tente fazer uma normalização em músicas e sfx para nenhum ser muito alto" (user, 2026-11-17). Re-run `measure_levels.py` after a file or cue changes.
- Menu: move has random pitch 1.05 and a 0.03 s cooldown, so a held stick's repeats do not stack. Open and close are the bookOpen and bookClose files, shared by the pause and the notebook.
- The muffle's curve is the Music bus's (even pitch steps), with the plan's ends: 20 kHz at memory 1, 1.2 kHz at 0, to be set by ear in the playtest (step 11). It has no smoothing: memory moves continuously with Ivo and the pulses; a room change can step it once.
- Soft landings take `ImpactLevel` (-12..0 dB) over their cue's level.
