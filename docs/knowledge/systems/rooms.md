# Rooms: text maps, the importer and the legend

Moved verbatim from `CLAUDE.md` ("Project structure") on 2026-10-02. The file format itself is `docs/maps/README.md`.

**Rooms are text** (`docs/maps/README.md`, the `room-map` skill). A `.room` file names its biome (`tileset = <id>`, required: `resources/world/tiles/biomes/<id>.tres`, a `BiomeTileset`) and is an ASCII grid - one character per 32 px cell, `#` earth, `S` stone, `%` wood, `=` / `-` one-way earth / plank ledges, entity letters - plus an optional `[back]` grid of walls behind the room (ground characters and `h`; no collision, no ground) and an optional `[water]` layer (water can lie OVER ground; `r` and `u` are reaches the parser joins to the water under them - the rain's and a shell's - `systems/water`) and `[entities]` params by grid column,row. `addons/room_maps` imports it ONCE (bump `room_map_importer.gd`'s `FORMAT_VERSION` whenever the parser, `GroundAutotile` or `RoomMap` change, or checkouts keep stale imports) into a `RoomMap` resource with every display tile already resolved (`RoomTileResolver`, `GroundAutotile`), so a room's load is one `set_cell` per display cell; `RoomMapNode` builds, as SIBLINGS, `Back_<look>` (z -20) and `Ground_<look>` dual-grid layers per look in Look order, `Ledges`, the hidden `Solid` collision layer, one `WaterLayer` per kind of water and the entities (a lake parented under the ground's TileMapLayer stopped drawing in front of it); the entities share one `Entities` node filled outside the tree and added whole, so a link resolves whatever order the file lists them in. Never paint ground in the editor: the `RoomMap` node's children are a preview and are never saved. `resources/world/maps/room_legend.tres` is the only place a character means anything; `EntityParams` applies params by property name (a bare `id` in a NodePath param links to that sibling). The ground is drawn on a dual grid from the biome's look sheets (one 128x960 corner-Wang sheet per look, three season bands; `architecture/biome-tilesets`). The fill and the four straight edges take variants, so a street one row deep does not repeat every 32 px ("Variants on top tiles (Recommended)", user, 2026-10-10); a character fixes the gameplay `Enums.Ground` and a `BiomeTileset.Look`, the biome only draws it, and a look the biome lacks is an import error (the Caldera and the Frost Edge have no earth: "No earth there", user, 2026-10-08). **Grass** (WORLD-23, `systems/grass`): `RoomMapNode` adds a runtime `Grass` (`GrassField`) after `Ledges` on every exposed top whose look grows grass in the room's biome; nothing in the `.room` names it. **Secrets** (WORLD-21, `architecture/secret-false-walls`; "False wall that fades (Recommended)", user, 2026-10-10): an optional `[secret]` grid of ground characters and `k` (cracked stone, the hint) over air. `RoomTileResolver` resolves the room with those cells filled in and keeps the display tiles a secret owns (`RoomMap.secret_layers`), so the closed copy meets the real ground exactly; `RoomMapNode` builds one `SecretWall` per 8-connected group (`Secret_x_y`, after `Water_*`): a world copy at z 40 that water reflects, a `ForegroundPass` copy at z -1 over the creatures, crack decals from `BiomeTileset.crack` (atlas source 16), and a trigger on the Player body (layer 9). It fades open over 0.4 scaled s for good and records `found_secrets` (live data: a bench keeps it, a death forgets it). A secret never collides; a secret cell may neither hold water nor sit under it (water would draw over the closed wall, or its lip spill into the water); a secret's id comes from its first cell, so reshaping a released secret closes it again in old saves; `room_files_test` wants a crack or a `d` draft by every secret. Collision never comes from the art: the hidden, cell-aligned `Solid` layer (`GroundCollision`: one full square per `Enums.Ground`, alternative 1 one-way, `ground` custom data) is the only collision, and `RoomMapNode.ground_at()` answers what the ground is made of. Every room is `tileset = legacy` (the pack sheet converted by `tools/art/assemble_tile_sheet.gd --from-blob`) until its biome's art lands. The importer runs after textures (`_get_import_order` 50) because a map loads its biome's sheets; a failed import is cached as `valid=false` at the current format and is not retried until the source or `importer_version` changes. The guide's legend, entity params and Ivo's reach (`JumpReach`) are GENERATED (`tools/maps/gen_map_docs.tscn`) and `map_guide_test.gd` fails when they drift; `room_files_test.gd` validates every map against the current legend. Tools that load the legend's entity scenes run as scenes (`res://tools/maps/*.tscn`), not `-s` scripts, because only a running scene has the autoloads those scenes reference.

## Regions, benches and room entry

- `Region.name_key` is the translation key of the region's name (`REGION_HOME_VILLAGE`, `REGION_FROST_EDGE`, `REGION_TRIALS` on the five trials). A rest saves it so the title can name the slot. Every new region sets it.
- A bench's `bench_id` names it on the title through `"BENCH_" + bench_id` in capitals. `room_files_test` fails when a bench in any `.room` has no such key with every language filled.
- **`Game.room_changed(room)`** fires once the camera frames the new current room: after `_enter_room` on a walk-in, after a hazard respawn that changes the room, and after `_arrive`'s seat and `snap()` (never from `_enter_room` itself, `bugs/a-debug-boot-reveals-map-cells-ivo-never-saw`). `MapRevealer` and `RegionWeather` listen to it.

## Doors and interiors (WORLD-09, design 04 §4, `architecture/doors-and-interiors`)

- A door is the legend entity `E` (`scenes/world/interactables/door/`). Its `link` is shared by exactly two doors across the world; its `to_room` is the partner's room id, the Room scene's file name (`Room.id()`), in the same region. `requires` (a `SaveFact` `.tres` path) seals it; `arrive_facing` turns Ivo on arrival there. `room_files_test` checks every pair (`DoorLinks`).
- Up in reach (or reached within `Player.DOOR_BUFFER`, 0.1 s; "Buffer it ~0.1 s", user, 2026-10-08): `Player._try_door` drops every other buffered press and emits `door_entered(door)`; `Game` disables Ivo DEFERRED (the press arrives inside his physics step, `gotchas/disabling-a-body-inside-its-own-physics-step-errors-its-move`), fades out, and lands him through `_land_in(room, place)` - the same landing `_arrive` uses (enter, wait for the contents, place, `snap`, `room_changed`). A sealed door knocks.
- `Room.interior`: never revealed on the map, no weather; the map opens on the building's door instead (`Screens.set_map_anchor`, cleared by any non-interior room).
- Authoring an interior: below its region's rooms, outside every `RegionMemory` source's reach (it then shares the region's baseline, season and death marks); bounds at least 640x360, filled; never touching another room's bounds. The test bed is `trials_solstice/door_trial` and `door_trial_inside` (F10, x 18560).
- **The map is keyed by the room's `SceneKey`**, with cells counted from the top-left of its bounds, so a region that moves keeps its map. Bounds that are not a multiple of 64 px round the grid up (a 602 px room has 10 rows). Two rooms instanced from one scene would share a map; today every room is its own scene (`systems/map`).
- **When Ivo has left the current room is `RoomExit.has_left`** (2026-10-10, WORLD-05): sideways once his whole body (`Player.body_rect()`) is out of the room's bounds; up or down only once it is `SEAM_MARGIN` (64 px) past the edge, or he stands. `Game._physics_process` then enters the room under the body's centre. One point (the feet) swapped rooms back and forth for a body astride a seam (`bugs/walking-across-a-room-seam-fades-back-and-forth`); the centre, then the whole body without a margin, flipped twice on every hop from a floor just below a seam (`bugs/a-hop-below-a-seam-flips-the-room-twice`, `bugs/a-hop-from-a-floor-just-below-a-seam-swaps-the-room-up-and-back`). Out of every room and with no room in his centre's column below his head (`RoomExit.is_lost`), `Player.fall_out_of_world()` holds him like a sink and the hazard beat puts him back on firm ground (`_lost`); above an open top it waits, since he falls back (`bugs/the-out-of-room-safety-net-fires-on-a-double-jump-above-an-open-room-top`). Meanwhile `GameCamera._keep_subject_in_view` lets the room clamp yield so his body (plus `subject_margin`, 8 px) stays in frame over a sliver of the running neighbour, never past `Game._reach_for` (the room plus the rooms his body reaches into, set every step with `set_reach`), so no void shows over an open top; the yield never pulls a glide back (`bugs/crossing-a-seam-upward-takes-ivo-out-of-the-frame`). `body_entered` only seeds the first room (`Game._on_room_entered`, at the body's centre, when no bench placed Ivo). Every non-motion move goes through `Character.teleport` (`systems/life-benches-death`, `gotchas/a-teleported-kinematic-body-overlaps-from-its-old-place-for-one-step`).
- **Walking across a seam neither fades nor stops Ivo** (2026-10-09): `_enter_room(room, true)` swaps the room inside `Game._physics_process`, `GameCamera.set_bounds(bounds, true)` glides the frame from where it stood to the new clamp (`bounds_glide_time`, 0.45 s, cubic ease-out so it moves at once; `snap()` ends a glide; `_framed_position()` includes a framed pair) and the memory baseline eases over `baseline_glide_time` (0.45 s). Disabling Ivo for a fade cut every jump that crossed a horizontal seam (`bugs/a-jump-across-a-horizontal-seam-loses-its-height-in-the-fade`). Only between touching rooms of one region: a region seam or a teleport cuts (`_enter_room(room, false)` + `snap()` on one frame, Ivo keeps moving), because a glide would show two regions' memory, season and weather mixed (`bugs/a-neighbour-room-in-another-region-runs-on-the-current-regions-memory`). Doors, benches, hazard respawns and death still fade.
- **The current room's neighbours run** (`RoomResidency`, 2026-10-09): `settle` activates the current room and every room whose bounds share an edge or a corner with it, so the ground across a seam exists (`bugs/a-neighbour-room-has-no-ground-until-ivo-enters-it`); rooms out of reach idle (hidden, DISABLED) and beyond `MAX_IDLE_ROOMS` (2) are evicted. `wake` (after a rest) evicts every other room, expires the current one and settles again; an expired room still in reach keeps its contents and rebuilds when it next comes back into reach. A neighbour's enemies and props run off screen, on the current region's memory even when the neighbour is in another region (accepted). The current room is pushed to the front last, so a door trip idles the room Ivo left instead of evicting it. A guardian ends its call when Ivo leaves its `Arena` (`systems/guardians`): its room keeps running after a walk out.

- **Format 3** (2026-10-07, PZL-09): `RoomMap.reach`. **Format 4** (2026-10-08, PZL-06): `RoomMap.shell_reach`. A format bump does not reimport on a headless `--import`: delete `.godot/imported/*.room-*` and import again, and commit the `.room.import` files (`gotchas/import-plugin-output-is-stale-when-its-logic-changes`).
