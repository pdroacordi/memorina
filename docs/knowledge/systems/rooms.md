# Rooms: text maps, the importer and the legend

Moved verbatim from `CLAUDE.md` ("Project structure") on 2026-10-02. The file format itself is `docs/maps/README.md`.

**Rooms are text** (`docs/maps/README.md`, the `room-map` skill). A `.room` file names its biome (`tileset = <id>`, required: `resources/world/tiles/biomes/<id>.tres`, a `BiomeTileset`) and is an ASCII grid - one character per 32 px cell, `#` earth, `S` stone, `%` wood, `=` / `-` one-way earth / plank ledges, entity letters - plus an optional `[back]` grid of walls behind the room (ground characters and `h`; no collision, no ground; it also hides a hard cut between two rooms' backdrops, WORLD-28, `systems/backdrops`) and an optional `[water]` layer (a lake `w` lies only over air, each row running off a room side or ending against solid ground, format 11, WORLD-29; `r` and `u` are reaches the parser joins to the water under them - the rain's and a shell's - `systems/water`) and `[entities]` params by grid column,row. `addons/room_maps` imports it ONCE (bump `room_map_importer.gd`'s `FORMAT_VERSION` whenever the parser, `GroundAutotile` or `RoomMap` change, or checkouts keep stale imports) into a `RoomMap` resource with every display tile already resolved (`RoomTileResolver`, `GroundAutotile`), so a room's load is one `set_cell` per display cell; `RoomMapNode` builds, as SIBLINGS, `Back_<look>` (z -20) and `Ground_<look>` dual-grid layers per look in Look order, `Ledges`, the hidden `Solid` collision layer, one `WaterLayer` per kind of water and the entities (a lake parented under the ground's TileMapLayer stopped drawing in front of it); the entities share one `Entities` node filled outside the tree and added whole, so a link resolves whatever order the file lists them in. Never paint ground in the editor: the `RoomMap` node's children are a preview and are never saved. `resources/world/maps/room_legend.tres` is the only place a character means anything; `EntityParams` applies params by property name (a bare `id` in a NodePath param links to that sibling). The ground is drawn on a dual grid from the biome's look sheets (one 128x960 corner-Wang sheet per look, three season bands; `architecture/biome-tilesets`). The fill and the four straight edges take variants, so a street one row deep does not repeat every 32 px ("Variants on top tiles (Recommended)", user, 2026-10-10); a character fixes the gameplay `Enums.Ground` and a `BiomeTileset.Look`, the biome only draws it, and a look the biome lacks is an import error (the Caldera and the Frost Edge have no earth: "No earth there", user, 2026-10-08). **Grass** (WORLD-23, `systems/grass`): `RoomMapNode` adds a runtime `Grass` (`GrassField`) after `Ledges` on every exposed top whose look grows grass in the room's biome; nothing in the `.room` names it. **Secrets** (WORLD-21, `architecture/secret-false-walls`; "False wall that fades (Recommended)", user, 2026-10-10): an optional `[secret]` grid of ground characters and `k` (cracked stone, the hint) over air. `RoomTileResolver` resolves the room with those cells filled in and keeps the display tiles a secret owns (`RoomMap.secret_layers`), so the closed copy meets the real ground exactly; `RoomMapNode` builds one `SecretWall` per 8-connected group (`Secret_x_y`, after `Water_*`): a world copy at z 40 that water reflects, a `ForegroundPass` copy at z -1 over the creatures, crack decals from `BiomeTileset.crack` (atlas source 16), and a trigger on the Player body (layer 9). It fades open over 0.4 scaled s for good and records `found_secrets` (live data: a bench keeps it, a death forgets it). A secret never collides; a secret cell may neither hold water nor sit under it (water would draw over the closed wall, or its lip spill into the water); a secret's id comes from its first cell, so reshaping a released secret closes it again in old saves; `room_files_test` wants a crack or a `d` draft by every secret. Collision never comes from the art: the hidden, cell-aligned `Solid` layer (`GroundCollision`: one full square per `Enums.Ground`, alternative 1 one-way, `ground` custom data) is the only collision, and `RoomMapNode.ground_at()` answers what the ground is made of. **Tilesets per region** (WORLD-07, 2026-10-13; "We should only our set of tiles from now on and not mix em.", tiles "Own tiles per region (Recommended)", user, 2026-10-13): every room names its region's biome, and no room, trial or generator default names `legacy` (the pack sheet, kept only for parser test fixtures; `biome_tileset_test`). home_village `village`; threshold `threshold`; frost_edge `frost_edge`, but lighthouse_base, lighthouse_top and lighthouse_interior `lighthouse` ("Lighthouse masonry tiles"); spring_woods `spring_woods`; frozen_lake `frozen_lake`; wind_orchards `wind_orchards`; summer_caldera `summer_caldera` (basalt), except caldera_dry_moat and caldera_open_crater on `summer_caldera_ash` ("Caldera ash as scree": the dry moat is the bed of the lake that was not, design 02 section 8.4, and the open crater is the one room under the sky the ash falls into; every other caldera room is a basalt cave) and caldera_cistern on `village` (the village's own cistern under the square's well, drawn in the square's cobbles with `village_strata` behind). The trials borrow their region's (`board_world`): winter `frozen_lake`, spring `spring_woods`, autumn `wind_orchards`, the solstice trials `threshold`, the_catch, door_trial and door_trial_inside `frozen_lake`, and summer `threshold` (its floor is earth, which the caldera biomes lack). The four region interiors (threshold_farmhouse, woods_hut, lake_fisher_hut, orchard_inn) are built of planks (`%`, `-`) like the village houses, so no earth turf lip shows indoors, and an interior draws band 0 in every season, so no snow or leaf lip either (`systems/seasonal-art`); the farmhouse's and the inn's hearths stay stone. `gen_world_shells.py` writes a new room's tileset from its region (`REGION_TILESETS`, `ROOM_TILESETS`) and a new interior's shell in planks. The importer runs after textures (`_get_import_order` 50) because a map loads its biome's sheets; a failed import is cached as `valid=false` at the current format and is not retried until the source or `importer_version` changes. The guide's legend, entity params and Ivo's reach (`JumpReach`) are GENERATED (`tools/maps/gen_map_docs.tscn`) and `map_guide_test.gd` fails when they drift; `room_files_test.gd` validates every map against the current legend. Tools that load the legend's entity scenes run as scenes (`res://tools/maps/*.tscn`), not `-s` scripts, because only a running scene has the autoloads those scenes reference.

## Regions, benches and room entry

- `Region.name_key` is the translation key of the region's name (`REGION_HOME_VILLAGE`, `REGION_FROST_EDGE`, `REGION_TRIALS` on the five trials). A rest saves it so the title can name the slot. Every new region sets it.
- `Region.epithet_key` (`REGION_<ID>_EPITHET`) and `Region.shows_title` (false on the village and the five trials) drive the first-visit region title (`systems/region-title`). Every new region sets an epithet key; `region_title_fit_test` fails on a titled region without one.
- A bench's `bench_id` names it on the title through `"BENCH_" + bench_id` in capitals. `room_files_test` fails when a bench in any `.room` has no such key with every language filled.
- **`Game.room_changed(room)`** fires once the camera frames the new current room: after `_enter_room` on a walk-in, after a hazard respawn that changes the room, and after `_arrive`'s seat and `snap()` (never from `_enter_room` itself, `bugs/a-debug-boot-reveals-map-cells-ivo-never-saw`). `MapRevealer`, `RegionWeather` and `RegionTitleWatcher` listen to it.

## Edges (WORLD-34, `architecture/world-edges-from-the-room-list`)

User, 2026-10-14: "there are some places that should have invisible walls or something like that. Look at this, for ex. the characther will be able to escape pretty much every place in the game." Choice "Closed edges + ceiling (Recommended)": "Every room edge with no room beyond gets an invisible wall. Over open-sky tops an invisible ceiling sits about one jump above the top: Ivo can jump into the sky but never land on anything outside. Caves and other enclosed rooms open to the sky get a drawn rock ceiling. A test fails if any surface outside a room can be reached." On A-Q1, 2026-10-14: "Drawn rock ceiling, all five (Recommended)".

- **Ivo never stands outside a room.** The allowed space is every room plus a **sky band** over each run of first-row columns that are open (not solid, one-way, roof or void; air, water and a secret are open) with no room at (column centre, top - 1). The band is `RoomEdges.CEILING_RISE` (152 px) tall.
- **`RoomEdges`** (pure) holds the rule: `closed_top(map)`, `bands(rooms, closed)`, `walls(rooms, bands)` (16 px slabs over every point outside the allowed space within 16 px of it, corners included, merged) and `allows`. A seam, a corner contact or a gap of 32 px or less gets no slab inside a room.
- **`WorldEdges`** (`World/WorldEdges`, a StaticBody2D on layer 5 "Invisible Walls", mask 0) holds one shape per slab, named after the room it borders. `Game._ready` builds it from every Room after `DebugTrials` mounted the trials. It dies with `game.tscn`.
- **Only Ivo** masks layer 5 (`collision_mask` 27). Guardians, enemies, rain, the shell and the camera's ground ray stay off it.
- **An edge is never grippable** (`WallMobilityComponent`: a slide collision on layer 5 is no wall) **and never safe ground** (`SafeGroundTracker` probes without layer 5).
- **152 px** is `RoomRing.WIDTH` less `GameCamera.subject_margin`, so the frame holds every jump over an open top. A full jump from `RoomRing.FLOOR_ROWS` under an open top clears it by 19 px (`room_edges_test`). It is an orchestrator call, not a user decision.
- **A first-row seam pillar is never crossed through the sky.** That would mean standing on it, which is outside. Seams are crossed through their openings. An orchestrator call.
- **A room inside rock gets a drawn ceiling, never sky.** frost_cliffs, lake_waterfall_path, lake_high_wall (cols 1-14), lake_chasm (cols 23-38) and orchard_counterweights have a stone row 0 ("Drawn rock ceiling, all five (Recommended)", user, 2026-10-14). `gen_world_shells.py` closes the first row of every new `ROCK_SETS` shell. The one exception is lighthouse_top (`ROCK_SETS`): its row 0 stays open over a solid row 1, so its sky band is unreachable.
- **An opening drawn onto the void is fixed in the map.** The audit's `OPEN_EDGE` (below) lists it. An allowlist entry needs a reason the map cannot change. An orchestrator call.
- **The trials are walled like every room.** Their sides are open in rows 0-8 above their walls, so a walk or jump off a trial's end stops at its bounds.
- **Safety net:** `RoomExit.is_lost` still holds Ivo and puts him back, and now `push_warning`s "an edge leak". That warning in a playtest log is a hole in the walls.

## Doors and interiors (WORLD-09, design 04 §4, `architecture/doors-and-interiors`)

- A door is the legend entity `E` (`scenes/world/interactables/door/`). Its `link` is shared by exactly two doors across the world; its `to_room` is the partner's room id, the Room scene's file name (`Room.id()`), in the same region. `requires` (a `SaveFact` `.tres` path) seals it; `arrive_facing` turns Ivo on arrival there. `room_files_test` checks every pair (`DoorLinks`).
- **A doorway fits its place** (WORLD-24; "The door in the cave makes no sense. Why is there a wood door in a cave? There are still doors in the middle of nowhere", user, 2026-10-10; board "Approve (Recommended)", user, 2026-10-10). A door's `look` picks its art (`Door.LOOKS`, two frames each, open and sealed): `door` (wood, inside a building's facade), `passage` (an arch cut in stone), `stairwell` (steps down through a stone floor; its art sinks `Door.SINKS` px below the floor line), `hatch_up` (a ladder to a ceiling trapdoor; the ceiling sits 64 px over the landing) and `hatch_down` (a trapdoor in the floor). `facade_doors_test`: every door outside an interior stands in a facade unless it is a passage, stairwell or hatch, and every look is known. The trials are test beds and exempt. A door inside a `[secret]` hides its art until the secret is found (`Door.hide_until`, set by `RoomMapNode`), so a stairwell's sunk steps never show through the closed stone (`bugs/the-cistern-stairwell-shows-through-the-closed-secret-wall`).
- **Roofs** (`^`, legend kind `ROOF`, format 8): a one-way ledge with no tile art, drawn by the building's facade behind it; ground `WOOD`, so nothing roots on it, and grass never grows under it (`GrassPlacement` reads a platform above as cover, though a roof has no look). `facade_doors_test` holds each run of roof cells over a facade with both ends on its top edge (8 px), so a roof is never a floating plank.
- Up in reach (or reached within `Player.DOOR_BUFFER`, 0.1 s; "Buffer it ~0.1 s", user, 2026-10-08): `Player._try_door` drops every other buffered press and emits `door_entered(door)`; `Game` disables Ivo DEFERRED (the press arrives inside his physics step, `gotchas/disabling-a-body-inside-its-own-physics-step-errors-its-move`), fades out, and lands him through `_land_in(room, place)` - the same landing `_arrive` uses (enter, wait for the contents, place, `snap`, `room_changed`). A sealed door knocks. The Up prompt stands above the art, or beside it at its middle when above would leave the door's room (`Door.prompt_position`; `door_prompts_test`, `bugs/the-lake-well-hatch-prompt-is-cut-by-the-top-of-the-frame`).
- `Room.interior`: never revealed on the map, no weather; the map opens on the building's door instead (`Screens.set_map_anchor`, cleared by any non-interior room).
- Authoring an interior: below its region's rooms, outside every `RegionMemory` source's reach (it then shares the region's baseline, season and death marks); bounds at least 640x360, filled; never touching another room's bounds. The test bed is `trials_solstice/door_trial` and `door_trial_inside` (F10, x 18560).
- **The map is keyed by the room's `SceneKey`**, with cells counted from the top-left of its bounds, so a region that moves keeps its map. Bounds that are not a multiple of 64 px round the grid up (a 602 px room has 10 rows). Two rooms instanced from one scene would share a map; today every room is its own scene (`systems/map`).
- **When Ivo has left the current room is `RoomExit.has_left`** (2026-10-10, WORLD-05): sideways or down once his whole body (`Player.body_rect()`) is out of the room's bounds; up only once it is `SEAM_MARGIN` (64 px) past the edge, or he stands. Down needs no margin (2026-10-10, CORE-07): the up margin is the hysteresis, and a fall must swap before its landing is due. `Game._physics_process` then enters the room under the body's centre. One point (the feet) swapped rooms back and forth for a body astride a seam (`bugs/walking-across-a-room-seam-fades-back-and-forth`); the centre, then the whole body without a margin, flipped twice on every hop from a floor just below a seam (`bugs/a-hop-below-a-seam-flips-the-room-twice`, `bugs/a-hop-from-a-floor-just-below-a-seam-swaps-the-room-up-and-back`). Out of every room and with no room in his centre's column below his head (`RoomExit.is_lost`), `Player.fall_out_of_world()` holds him like a sink and the hazard beat puts him back on firm ground (`_lost`); above an open top it waits, since he falls back (`bugs/the-out-of-room-safety-net-fires-on-a-double-jump-above-an-open-room-top`). Since WORLD-34 the edges stop him first, and the net also warns of an edge leak ("Edges"). Meanwhile `GameCamera._keep_subject_in_view` lets the room clamp yield so his body (plus `subject_margin`, 8 px) stays in frame over a sliver of the running neighbour, never past `RoomRing.reach` (the room's bounds grown by `RoomRing.WIDTH`, 160 px, on every side; set with `set_bounds` and again by `Game._set_camera_reach` every step and after a room swap, `bugs/a-region-cut-frames-one-step-without-the-open-sky`). Every pixel of that ring is drawn by a resident room (`systems/backdrops`), so **the camera never shows the void** without any correction step (`bugs/the-drawn-frame-correction-flips-between-candidates-and-drops-ivo-out-of-frame`); the yield never pulls a glide back (`bugs/crossing-a-seam-upward-takes-ivo-out-of-the-frame`).
- **Room ring** (2026-10-11, `RoomRing`; `bugs/the-woods-pond-shelf-jump-takes-ivo-out-of-the-top-of-the-frame`, `bugs/the-drawn-frame-correction-flips-between-candidates-and-drops-ivo-out-of-frame`): the void within 160 px (`RoomRing.WIDTH`, square-cornered) of a room is drawn by the nearest room's backdrop (no dither since WORLD-28: inside the rooms a seam is a hard cut, and where two pictures meet in the ring `backdrop_seams_test` lists the cut with its reason), and the camera's keep-in-view yield may reach that far past the current room on any side, never further. Resting framing still clamps at the room. 160 px holds Ivo's 54 px body and the 8 px `subject_margin` at the apex of a full jump (143 px) from a floor `RoomRing.FLOOR_ROWS` (2) rows under an open top, and is under half the 384 px between rooms that do not touch, so the nearest room to any pixel of the ring is the current room or one touching it: resident. `room_ring_test` pins both, sweeps Ivo's body 2 px at a time around every room (the frame moves no more than the body, holds it wherever it is inside the ring, and every sampled pixel has exactly one resident owner), and checks every glide between rooms of one region. During a glide the left room's neighbours keep running (`RoomResidency.settle(current, rooms, keep)`; `Game._glide_keep` adds the neighbours of every room a retargeted glide left and settles again on `GameCamera.glide_finished`, `bugs/a-stale-glide-timer-idles-the-left-rooms-neighbours-mid-glide`): the glide still frames the left room's ring, which they may draw (`bugs/a-ring-corner-diagonal-shows-a-rock-wall-in-the-mills-sky-on-a-jump`). Over an open top the sky band's ceiling (152 px, `RoomEdges`) holds every jump inside the ring, so the frame always holds Ivo (WORLD-34). **Glides** run only between rooms that share an edge (`RoomResidency.share_an_edge`; a corner is cut like a region seam), and ease both axes together or one after the other through a corner frame, whichever keeps every frame inside the two rooms' rings (`RoomRing.glide_order`, `bugs/a-glide-skips-the-drawn-frame-correction-and-snaps-when-it-ends`). `_glide_weight` runs linear in time and `RoomRing.glide_offset` eases each leg out over its own share (the whole glide, or half each). **The look-ahead is bounded by the room clamp, never the ring** (`bugs/a-facing-turn-in-a-one-screen-wide-room-slides-the-frame-past-its-bounds`). The ring test includes the song trials DebugTrials mounts. They are exempt only from the two-rings-apart premise: their regions sit 2400 px apart, leaving 32 px and 256 px between some rooms. Each room judges ownership among the rooms it touches, and the sweeps over the trials still find one resident owner per pixel. **Authoring:** a first-row pillar is never crossed through the sky. A seam is crossed through its opening, and a pillar the sky must pass over is lowered one row. The reach audit catches a required take-off the ceiling clips. The `NEAR_TOP` list (row-1 floors under an open top) was retired by WORLD-34 on 2026-10-14. Replaced the same day: an 80 px `OpenSky` per room plus a drawn-frame correction, which flipped between candidate frames at the void corners its box reach held.
- **A fall shows its landing** (CORE-07; "when we fall into a new room, the camera takes too long to follow and that is really bad for the player to track where he is going to fall.", user, 2026-10-10). In the air the camera keeps a lead below the feet (`GameCamera.fall_lead()`): at least `fall_view` (176 px) x `air_axis`, and never less than the unclamped follow would show, so the lead takes over from the follow at a seam without a stall. It grows at `fall_lead_rise` (720 px/s, so a walk-off leans in), moves the frame down at most `fall_lead_follow` (1200 px/s from last step's centre, faster than the 1000 px/s fall, so a room swap or glide shows no jump), eases back over `vertical_smooth_time` after a landing and drops to 0 below half a pixel. It stops at the ground below plus `LANDING_GROUND` (32 px), by a ray at each side of the body from 2 px above the feet: descending, ground counts only under both sides; standing or rising, the nearer hit counts, so neither a drift off an edge nor a stance at one dips the frame. It yields only into the **fall reach** (`set_reach(body_reach, fall_reach)`, `Game._fall_reach_for`, measured from `steady_view_rect()` so a shake never closes it): the current room plus, under its floor seam, rooms of the same region that together span the frame's width (`FallReach.of`). A region seam still cuts (`snap(true)` keeps the lead across it), so another region's look never shows before the cut, and a narrower room below (a well shaft) shows no void beside it. Measured (`playtests/2026-10-10-camera-on-falls`, revision 6): same-region drops show the landing 0.20-0.29 s before touchdown, against 0-0.11 s before; a frame that holds still while the landing is already in view is accepted (engineering judgement, not a user decision). Open: `bugs/the-frame-jumps-up-when-the-fall-reach-closes-under-a-yielded-frame` (sideways drift).
- **Walking across a seam neither fades nor stops Ivo** (2026-10-09): `_enter_room(room, true)` swaps the room inside `Game._physics_process`, `GameCamera.set_bounds(bounds, true)` glides the frame from where it stood to the new clamp (`bounds_glide_time`, 0.45 s, cubic ease-out so it moves at once; `snap()` ends a glide; `_framed_position()` includes a framed pair) and the memory baseline eases over `baseline_glide_time` (0.45 s). Disabling Ivo for a fade cut every jump that crossed a horizontal seam (`bugs/a-jump-across-a-horizontal-seam-loses-its-height-in-the-fade`). Only between touching rooms of one region: a region seam or a teleport cuts (`_enter_room(room, false)` + `snap()` on one frame, Ivo keeps moving), because a glide would show two regions' memory, season and weather mixed (`bugs/a-neighbour-room-in-another-region-runs-on-the-current-regions-memory`). Doors, benches, hazard respawns and death still fade.
- **The current room's neighbours run** (`RoomResidency`, 2026-10-09): `settle` activates the current room and every room whose bounds share an edge or a corner with it, so the ground across a seam exists (`bugs/a-neighbour-room-has-no-ground-until-ivo-enters-it`); rooms out of reach idle (hidden, DISABLED) and beyond `MAX_IDLE_ROOMS` (2) are evicted. `wake` (after a rest) evicts every other room, expires the current one and settles again; an expired room still in reach keeps its contents and rebuilds when it next comes back into reach. A neighbour's enemies and props run off screen, on the current region's memory even when the neighbour is in another region (accepted). The current room is pushed to the front last, so a door trip idles the room Ivo left instead of evicting it. A guardian ends its call when Ivo leaves its `Arena` (`systems/guardians`): its room keeps running after a walk out.

- **Helix stairs** (`@`, WORLD-26, `systems/helix-stair`): an entity on the bottom floor whose cell's bottom-left corner is the stair's axis; params `shape` (a `HelixStairShape` .tres path), `look` (a `HelixLook`) and `hand`. `room_files_test` runs `HelixStair.check` on every one: a clear column up the axis (one-ways pass), a plank gallery at the head, the shell's inner faces at the wall's silhouette. Reach role STAIR: the audit joins its foot and head both ways (`HelixStair.reach_ends`).
- **Void** (`_`, kind VOID, `architecture/room-void-outside-the-shell`; "It's okay to leave a black space or something like that", user, 2026-10-11; wall edge "Straight cut (Recommended)", user, 2026-10-11): outside a room's shell. Solid stone to gameplay (the `Solid` layer, `ground_at()` STONE, roots never grow, the audit blocks on it), no look. Every one of a void cell's 8 neighbours is ground (not a ledge), void or off the map, so a drawn wall always stands between the room's air and the void; never under water, a reach or a secret (parser errors). `RoomTileResolver` gives each void cell its nearest wall's look and clears every display tile of four void corners, so the wall's tiles toward the void are fill; `RoomMapNode` adds `Void` (`RoomVoid`, z 60), one black rect per merged run, which cuts them on the cell line. Interiors only for now (`room_files_test`, with door art kept off void); the nine building interiors, the bell tower and `lighthouse_interior` use it, not the cellar. `gen_world_shells.py` writes a new interior's shell as one cell of wall and void beyond.

- **Format 11** (2026-10-13, WORLD-29): the lake rules (`RoomMapParser._check_lakes`, `systems/water`). - **Format 10** (2026-10-11): VOID cells, `RoomMap.void_cells` and the section cut. **Format 9** (2026-10-11, WORLD-26): the `@` legend entry. - **Format 8** (2026-10-10, WORLD-24): ROOF cells. **Format 3** (2026-10-07, PZL-09): `RoomMap.reach`. **Format 4** (2026-10-08, PZL-06): `RoomMap.shell_reach`. A format bump does not reimport on a headless `--import`: delete `.godot/imported/*.room-*` and import again, and commit the `.room.import` files (`gotchas/import-plugin-output-is-stale-when-its-logic-changes`).

## Reachability audit (TECH-05, `architecture/room-reachability-audit`)

User, 2026-10-10: "There are plenty of weird places that lead nowhere and random platforms that lead nowhere too." Choice: "Audit tool + rework (Recommended)".

- **What it checks.** `room_reach_test` stitches every room `game.tscn` places (`ReachWorldLoader`, `.room` text parsed fresh, each map the size of its room's bounds) into one `ReachGrid` and runs `ReachAudit`. Errors: UNREACHABLE (a platform the start cannot reach with the full kit), LEADS_NOWHERE (a reached platform or shelf that holds no anchor and that no way to an anchor or back to the start needs, in any tier), NO_WAY_BACK (a reached surface that tier cannot return to the start from). UNPAIRED_DOOR (a door whose `link` no other door of its region shares). OUTSIDE_ROOM (WORLD-34): a footprint standing with its feet in a sky band, reached or not, e.g. a song floor in an open first row or a mover's floor rounded above it (a band cell keeps its grid flags), and any grid flag in no room and no band (a mover or current authored past its room; `bugs/outside-room-misses-a-song-floor-above-a-rooms-first-row`). It is never allowlisted; `room_reach_test` fails on it and on any allowlist entry for it. OPEN_EDGE (WORLD-34): a run of side or bottom edge cells Ivo's body can fill (not solid, not a hazard) whose outside neighbour is in no room and no band, i.e. an opening drawn onto an invisible wall. Hazard cells are skipped (an orchestrator call): the three dead lakes on a bottom row (village_mill, frost_shore, lake_fishing_village) lie under a solid or one-way floor, so Ivo never enters them, and HAZARD comes only from the lake `w` (`takes_reach = false`), which no song raises or holds back. Warnings, never failing: unreached ground, REDUNDANT_ROUTE (the last of parallel routes, kept), DEAD_END_POCKET (the ground and roofs of a group that holds and serves nothing; its platforms and shelves are still LEADS_NOWHERE; promoting pockets is the user's call).
- **Anchors**: the new-game start (the Player's place in `game.tscn`), doors (a pair is a two-way edge), entities whose legend `reach_role` is CONTENT, and surfaces touching a secret.
- **A platform** is held only by one-way ledges or islands (solid groups touching no room edge). A roof (`^`) moves Ivo like a one-way and counts as ground. A surface only on a song's floor, or in water a Redoma shell holds back, is a song's and never flagged.
- **A shelf** (TECH-06) is ground judged as a platform for LEADS_NOWHERE: a run of at most 6 cells (`ReachAudit.SHELF_CELLS`) with a drop at one end (no wall, and no floor within 3 rows of the feet beside it) where most cells holding it have air within 2 rows below (`SHELF_DEPTH`): a ledge out of a wall or off a post. So is any narrow run beside a drop, indoors or out: the tavern's 6-cell east upper floor and orchard_windy_gap's 1-cell cocoon stop are shelves, allowlisted with their reasons; expect the same of an upper-floor section or a block of 6 cells or fewer next to a drop. A floor, a terrace solid to the ground, a block on a thin floor (its ends step down), a notch between two walls and a roof stay ground and land in DEAD_END_POCKET. An unreached shelf is still a warning.
- **Roots go with their ledge.** `ReachGrid.roots` keeps each span's earth ends; a platform or shelf grows a transit node only when every root in that node has an end in its island or slab and no current runs through it (transit cells of several spans merge into one node). A ledge growing a node that something needs is needed, and the redundancy check removes a ledge together with the nodes it grows. A ledge whose roots share a node with other spans is credited with nothing, so it can be a LEADS_NOWHERE error but is never skipped (`reach_audit_test`; `bugs/a-ledge-is-credited-with-a-whole-merged-root-node-and-its-dead-end-goes-unreported`).
- **Movement** (`ReachLattice`): Ivo's footprint is his capsule in whole cells (`MapGuide.ivo_body`, 1x2), so CORE-08's redraw changes only data. One step is the time he runs one cell; the rise or fall comes from `JumpReach.heights`. One-ways pass upward and land downward, with no drop-through (WORLD-19). Outside the rooms only the sky bands' whole cells are open (`ReachGrid.bands`, from `RoomEdges.band_cells`; 4 rows, so the audit is up to 24 px stricter than physics over a top, never looser). An edge is never stood on or slid on. The 10-row sky and the one-column side poke were retired by WORLD-34. Tiers: BASE, +DOUBLE_JUMP (the air jump at any step), +WALL_CLIMB (a wall the body's height is a take-off). Pinned in `reach_lattice_test`: 4 cells up and 8 across, 8 up with the double jump; a late air jump clears about 15 cells, more than the guide's 13 (apex press).
- **Songs, all at once**: root spans at the wet length (a bridge is an optional floor, a shaft or pillar is climbed), ice on the top of ICE water, pools passable, the lake (HAZARD) not, gates, barriers, covers and sealed doors open, a current (CURRENT) takes Ivo in anywhere in its box and sets him down on its downwind edge, falling with the air jump kept (never a ground jump from mid-air), and each MOVER's root script answers `reach_cells(map, at)` from its authored values and its placement `at`, never its live position: the floor cells it passes (`MapCells`). A current also answers `reach_exit_cells`. A new mover implements them (`room_reach_test` checks).

- **Boards**: `"<godot>" --headless --path . res://tools/maps/reach_boards.tscn -- --out <dir>` (default `user://reach_boards`) writes `<room>.txt` (marks on the feet row, never a legend symbol: `!` unreachable, `*` leads nowhere, `+` redundant route, `:` dead-end pocket, `&` no way back, `?` unpaired door, `x` outside a room (not `$`, which WORLD-35 reserves for reward slots), `|` open edge) and `<room>.png` (8 px per cell) and a summary.
