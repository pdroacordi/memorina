# Backdrops

**Never break:** a layer is placed only by `BackdropMath`: the region's horizon on an axis where the art has an edge, world 0 on an axis where it repeats. A region has one set and one horizon; a room's `Backdrop` only overrides the set (caves, interiors, rooms inside rock). A backdrop pixel is drawn by exactly one room: the one whose rect holds it, or, in the void, the room `void_owner` picks among the rooms within `RoomRing.WIDTH`: the highest hold (over its top, beside it, under its floor, past a corner), then the nearest, so every dividing line is straight. A seam is a hard cut: where two rooms draw different pictures (`BackdropMath.same_picture`) the cut touches ground, void, a `[back]` wall or a lake (`backdrop_seams_test`; every exception in its `ALLOWED` with a reason). A fill past the strip reads the sheet's own edge row, never a colour. `art_row`/`art_col` and `pixel_centre`/`keeps`/`void_owner`/`rect_distance` in `BackdropMath` and `backdrop_layer.gdshader` are one mapping in two languages: change both. Every room has a backdrop whose first layer is opaque; no scene draws the pack's backgrounds (`backdrop_set_test`).

Design 04 section 6 ("Fundos verticais"), WORLD-06, which also fixes WORLD-04. Plan and options: `architecture/vertical-backdrops`.

- **Draw order.** `Backdrop.Z` is -30: behind WORLD-07's `[back]` walls (-20) and props (-10) (`systems/props`).
- **Over its top, the region's sky** (2026-10-13, `bugs/a-wall-climb-over-the-seam-pillar-shows-the-wall-up-cut-in-the-sky`). A `Backdrop` that overrides its region's set builds the override with `Backdrop.Part.UNDER_TOP` and the region's set with `OVER_TOP` (`Sky0..`); the shader's `part` discards by `BackdropMath.over_top`, the pixel centre above the room's top edge (one test in both languages). Rock fills only the room and the void beside or under it; over its wall tops the sky goes on, so two rooms of one region meet in the same pixels there.
- **Authoring** (WORLD-28, 2026-10-13). A region scene sets `Region.backdrop_set` and `Region.horizon_y` (the region's one horizon, px below the region node). A room's contents scene has one `Backdrop` node (first child of `Contents`) whose `backdrop_set` is null to show the region's set, or a set that overrides it; the anchor is the node's x and the region's horizon y (`Backdrop.shown_set`). A region's set repeats across on every layer, and a set with an edge on y needs a horizon (`backdrop_set_test`). The editor preview of a contents scene opened alone has no Region, so a room without an override shows no backdrop there (accepted). A set is data: its layers far to near (`BackdropLayer`: stacked sheet, `band_count`, `band_of_season`, `scroll_scale` per axis, `horizon_row`, `fill_above`/`fill_below` NONE/EDGE/REPEAT, `repeat_x`, haze `distance`) and `horizon_screen_y`, the eye level in game px.
- **The model, per axis.** At the reference camera (`BackdropMath.reference_centre`: the camera that shows the anchor's horizon at `horizon_screen_y`) every layer stands where it was authored. Away from it a layer's anchor moves by `(camera - reference) * (1 - scroll)`. So a far layer (`scroll` near 0) keeps its horizon at eye level whatever the camera's height, and a layer at 1 is pinned to the world.
- **Built nodes.** `Backdrop.refresh()` builds `LayerN` (`Parallax2D`, `scroll_scale` from the layer, `scroll_offset` from `BackdropMath.scroll_offset` against the room's global position) holding `Art`, a `MeshInstance2D` `QuadMesh` sized to `BackdropMath.cover_rect` (what the room's camera can show at zoom 1 or more, plus 32 px) and clipped by `clip_to_fills`. The built nodes are unowned and never saved. Without a `Room` above it (the editor, a contents scene opened alone) it uses a fixed preview rect.
- **Clipped to its room** (2026-10-09): the current room's neighbours run and draw (`systems/rooms`), and a parallax layer slides past its room's edge, so `Backdrop` sets `room_rect` (the room's world bounds) and the shader discards every pixel drawn outside it (`MODEL_MATRIX * VERTEX` carries the `Parallax2D` scroll). Without it a neighbour's layers drew over the current view and popped at every crossing (`bugs/a-running-neighbour-rooms-backdrop-draws-over-the-current-room`). No `Room` (editor preview): no clip.
- **Room ring** (2026-10-11, `systems/rooms`, `bugs/the-drawn-frame-correction-flips-between-candidates-and-drops-ivo-out-of-frame`, `bugs/the-ring-tie-wedge-draws-a-hard-diagonal-between-two-backdrops`, `bugs/the-ring-dividing-line-leaves-a-one-pixel-diagonal-hole-above-a-seam`): the shader judges a pixel by its centre, `floor(world_pos) + 0.5` (`BackdropMath.pixel_centre`), so two quads never disagree on it (`gotchas/two-quads-disagree-on-a-pixels-world-position`). A pixel in no room goes to `void_owner`, if within `ring` (`RoomRing.WIDTH`, 160 px, by `rect_distance`, the farther-axis distance, so the ring is the square `RoomRing.reach`). Among the rooms within `ring` of the pixel (2026-10-13, `bugs/a-ring-corner-diagonal-shows-a-rock-wall-in-the-mills-sky-on-a-jump`), `void_owner` takes the highest hold, then the smallest gap, then the lower x, then the lower y:
  - ABOVE (3): the room's columns hold the pixel over its top; gap in y. The sky over a room is that room's.
  - BESIDE (2): its rows hold the pixel; gap in x.
  - BELOW (1): its columns hold the pixel under its floor; gap in y. A deeper room beside a floor goes on down under it.
  - CORNER (0): any other room; `rect_distance`.
  The sky band over an open top (WORLD-34, `systems/rooms` "Edges") lies inside its room's ring, so it needs no new art.
  Every dividing line is straight: a room edge continued, or a ring limit. The 2026-10-11 nearest-gap rule left a 45-degree line at every L-corner, which the dither hid until WORLD-28. Each room judges among the rooms it touches. Each layer's cover is `RoomRing.reach(bounds)`; the ring reads each layer's `fill_above` and `fill_below` (EDGE on the surface sets, REPEAT on the cave and interior sets). Replaced the same day: per-room 80 px sky rects, then a nearest-room rule with 45-degree seams.
