# Backdrops

**Never break:** a layer is placed only by `BackdropMath`: the room's horizon anchor on an axis where the art has an edge, world 0 on an axis where it repeats. A backdrop pixel is drawn by exactly one room, the one holding the pixel moved by the seam jitter. A fill past the strip reads the sheet's own edge row, never a colour. `art_row`/`art_col` and `seam_jitter`/`keeps`/`BAYER` in `BackdropMath` and `backdrop_layer.gdshader` are one mapping in two languages: change both. Every room has a backdrop whose first layer is opaque.

Design 04 section 6 ("Fundos verticais"), WORLD-06, which also fixes WORLD-04. Plan and options: `architecture/vertical-backdrops`.

- **Draw order.** `Backdrop.Z` is -30: behind WORLD-07's `[back]` walls (-20) and props (-10) (`systems/props`).
- **Authoring.** A room's contents scene has one `Backdrop` node (first child of `Contents`) with a `BackdropSet` and `horizon_y`: the room's horizon, px below the node. A set is data: its layers far to near (`BackdropLayer`: stacked sheet, `band_count`, `band_of_season`, `scroll_scale` per axis, `horizon_row`, `fill_above`/`fill_below` NONE/EDGE/REPEAT, `repeat_x`, haze `distance`) and `horizon_screen_y`, the eye level in game px.
- **The model, per axis.** At the reference camera (`BackdropMath.reference_centre`: the camera that shows the anchor's horizon at `horizon_screen_y`) every layer stands where it was authored. Away from it a layer's anchor moves by `(camera - reference) * (1 - scroll)`. So a far layer (`scroll` near 0) keeps its horizon at eye level whatever the camera's height, and a layer at 1 is pinned to the world.
- **Built nodes.** `Backdrop.refresh()` builds `LayerN` (`Parallax2D`, `scroll_scale` from the layer, `scroll_offset` from `BackdropMath.scroll_offset` against the room's global position) holding `Art`, a `MeshInstance2D` `QuadMesh` sized to `BackdropMath.cover_rect` (what the room's camera can show at zoom 1 or more, plus 32 px, and half the seam band under a Room) and clipped by `clip_to_fills`. The built nodes are unowned and never saved. Without a `Room` above it (the editor, a contents scene opened alone) it uses a fixed preview rect.
- **Clipped to its room** (2026-10-09): the current room's neighbours run and draw (`systems/rooms`), and a parallax layer slides past its room's edge, so `Backdrop` sets `room_rect` (the room's world bounds) and the shader discards every pixel drawn outside it (`MODEL_MATRIX * VERTEX` carries the `Parallax2D` scroll). Without it a neighbour's layers drew over the current view and popped at every crossing (`bugs/a-running-neighbour-rooms-backdrop-draws-over-the-current-room`). No `Room` (editor preview): no clip.
- **Seams blend** (WORLD-22, `architecture/backdrop-seams`; the user, 2026-10-10: "The transition in kind of the same room when a part of the room is grey and the other isn't is not that good, its just a line, could have more of a gradient or something like that."): a pixel near a room's edge is drawn by the room whose rect holds the pixel moved by `BackdropMath.seam_jitter` (16 Bayer levels in game pixels, within `SEAM_BAND`/2, 64 px), or by its own room when that point is in no room. `Backdrop` passes its touching rooms' bounds (`RoomResidency.touching`, at most 8) and the band to the shader and grows each layer's cover by half the band. Every seam, vertical, horizontal or across a region, becomes a dithered band. A repeating axis is phased on world 0 (`BackdropMath.layer_anchor`), so rooms of one set draw the same pixels there.
- **The shader** picks each pixel's texel from its layer-local position (`VERTEX + quad_position`) with `texelFetch`: EDGE repeats the strip's first or last row, REPEAT wraps, NONE is transparent. Season from the mask (`gh_season_at`, `gh_band_for` in `seasonal_common.gdshaderinc`, shared with `seasonal_art.gdshader`), haze from `distance` exactly as `systems/seasonal-art`. No `TIME`, no `FRAGCOORD`.
- **Measured.** `backdrop_test` drives a real `Camera2D`: each `Parallax2D` lands where the model says at zoom 1 and at 1.5 (the Memorina draw), and a room at (12160, 6000) keeps the vertical pose of one at the origin. `backdrop_math_test` checks one owner per seam pixel for side-by-side rooms, a four-room corner and a room against the void.
- **Art contract** (every biome set): `architecture/vertical-backdrops`, "Art contract". Every EDGE row is one colour per band (`backdrop_set_test`). `background_layer_3`'s bottom row was flattened to its dominant colour for this (2026-10-08); its season sources too, so `tools/stack_seasonal_sheets.gd` keeps it.
- **The village valley** (WORLD-11; "They're perfect. It just doesn't have a lot of contrast with the character. Not talking about color itself, because soon I might redraw Ivo (I will, actually). Is there a way to improve that without changing much of the scenario?", user, 2026-10-10). `village_valley.tres` on the four village street rooms: a sky drawn by `tools/art/cut_backdrop.py` (stepped bands, flat clouds) and three Codex layers, snowy mountains (s 0.12, 0.07), hills with fields (0.3, 0.18) and a near tree line (0.55, 0.32), all `horizon_row` 229 and 2x density. Autumn and winter are recolours of the spring drawing. The contrast answer is **atmospheric depth baked into the art**: each band is mixed toward its season's horizon sky by the layer's depth (`AERIAL`: mountains 0.5, hills 0.42, trees 0.32), so the backdrop is lighter and lower in contrast than the play plane whatever Ivo's colours are; the runtime `distance` haze still dissolves it in the grey. The board compared 0, 45/35/22 and 50/42/32 percent; the strongest was kept. The hills' Codex strip ends in a sea cliff with the lighthouse, which cannot wrap, so the tile is cut left of it.
- **Today's sets.** `pine_woods.tres`, the pack's forest, is on the eight trial rooms (`horizon_y` -53: 53 px above their floor) and, as a placeholder, on the 27 other new-world surface rooms (`horizon_y` 53 px above the room's bottom floor). Underground rooms and interiors have one-layer sets that repeat on both axes and have one band (no seasons underground or indoors): `village_strata` (caldera_cistern), `earth_caves`, `ice_caves`, `basalt_caves` (every other caldera room, the open crater included for now), `interior_timber` and `interior_stone`. Their sheets are placeholders drawn by `tools/art/draw_backdrop_placeholder.gd` until the Codex art (`tools/art/prompts/backdrop_*.md`). The room generator writes a `Backdrop` into every new shell. The title keeps its own autoscrolling `Parallax2D`.
