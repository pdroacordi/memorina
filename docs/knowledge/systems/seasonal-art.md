# Seasonal art: stacked sheets, the season mask and regional weather

Moved verbatim from `CLAUDE.md` ("Seasonal art") on 2026-10-02.

A pulse does not tint the world into its season; it **redraws** it. Every seasonal drawable is a **stacked sheet**: the same drawing repeated in equal vertical bands, one per season, and the scene always references **band 0** (a biome look sheet's rows 0–5, `architecture/biome-tilesets`; a background `region_rect` inside the top 346 px). Which band is which season is declared on the material, not by a global rule — the look sheets and the backgrounds are both spring / autumn / winter top to bottom, so both materials carry `band_of_season = (2, 0, 1, 0)` in `Enums.Season` order. Summer has no art and points at spring's band; a summer pulse is spring art plus summer's tint.

- **`SeasonMask` (`scenes/world/memory/seasonal/`) evaluates the field once per frame** into a 640×360 texture: one byte per game pixel saying which pulse season owns it, dithered across the feather with the same Bayer cell as the greyhush. `GreyhushRenderer` feeds it the same uniforms as the two colour passes and publishes its texture as the `greyhush_season_mask` global, plus `MemoryField.season` (the region's native season, from `Region.season`) as `greyhush_region_season`. Giving a sprite or `TileMapLayer` seasons is assigning `seasonal_art.gdshader` in a material with `band_count` and `band_of_season` — no script, no registration.
- **Back walls draw darker through the same shader** (WORLD-07): `seasonal_art.gdshader`'s `shade` (an RGB multiplier, 0..1, applied before the haze) is 0.55 on `back_tiles_seasonal_material.tres`, with `distance` 0.2 so a forgotten room's back wall dissolves before its floor (starting values). Built ground gets the seasonal top like earth ("Seasonal top", user, 2026-10-08); built walls stay one band.
- **`gh_source_influence()` in the include is the only place a source's geometry is evaluated.** The mask and the colour passes call it, so the snow cannot stop a pixel short of the colour.
- **In a canvas_item fragment `COLOR` already holds `texture(TEXTURE, UV) * modulate`.** Any shader that samples the texture elsewhere must carry modulate as a varying from `vertex()`, or it multiplies its sample by the band-0 texel.
- Backgrounds are authored one season per file under `assets/sprites/world/background/<season>/` and stacked by `tools/stack_seasonal_sheets.gd` (`"<godot>" --headless --path . -s res://tools/stack_seasonal_sheets.gd`) into `background/seasonal/`, which is what the scenes reference. Rerun it after touching a source PNG; a missing season stands in with spring. Tiles are stacked by hand in the sheet itself.
- **The grey is empty, not dark.** Two things sell it, both static: `black_lift` (the faded print — forgotten blacks rise toward a cold tone, white stays white) in the greyhush pass, and `distance` on each seasonal material (the sky is 1, the ground 0), which dissolves far layers into the haze by how forgotten the pixel is, so a dead place loses its horizon before its floor. The mask's G channel carries the SMOOTH memory for that — never the dithered value, or the world material and the screen pass dither the same pixels twice and every rounded-up pixel pops out as a bright square; `greyhush_haze_color` / `greyhush_distance_fade` are globals pushed by the renderer so one knob rules every layer. A backdrop layer's `distance` lives on its `BackdropLayer` (`systems/backdrops`), read by `backdrop_layer.gdshader` with the same haze term.
- The pulse's leading ring (`PulseTimeline.ring()`, packed into `tints[i].a`) and the slow per-sector re-roll of the ragged edge (`edge_reroll_period`) are `GreyhushRenderer` exports; at 0 the frame is what it was before them. **Do not add high-frequency texture to the grey**: a per-pixel "deserting pixels" speckle was tried and cut — at any density that registers it reads as static and is tiring to look at. Emptiness is low-frequency.
- A season's particles are a `CPUParticles2D` scene on `SeasonPalette.pulse_particles`, mounted under the pulse, wearing `seasonal_particles.gdshader` so they are clipped to the mask: they exist exactly where the art has swapped, and nowhere else.
- **A region wears its season in proportion to its memory** (`docs/design/03_mundo_e_ambiente.md` section 5.2). `Game` hands `MemoryField` the region's `SeasonPalette` (`SongCatalog.palette_for(season)`) beside its season and baseline. `GreyhushRenderer` pushes the palette's tint as `region_tint` at `region_tint_amount = baseline`, applied in `gh_shade` wherever no source tints the pixel - so a restored region keeps its season's colour cast and a pulse of another season still overrides it. `RegionWeather` (`scenes/world/environment/region_weather/`, in `game.tscn` under `World`, PROCESS_MODE_ALWAYS) mounts the same particle scene a pulse would, with the shader's `ambient` uniform set so it lives wherever NO pulse has swapped the art (and inside pulses of its own season), follows the camera, and reads `MemoryField.sample()` AT THE CAMERA - not the bare baseline - so a well of forgetting stills the sky above it and a pulse makes the flakes fall again inside itself ("dentro do pulso os flocos voltam a cair"). **The grey stays empty**: that one number drives `speed_scale` (the fall stops), `modulate.a` (`quiet_alpha`) and the emission box, which is SPREAD wider than the screen as memory drops (`quiet_spread`) so the same flakes scatter thin and most fall out of frame - a forgotten sky is not a sky full of stopped specks. None of the three restarts the emitter (CPUParticles2D has no `amount_ratio` in 4.7, and writing `amount` respawns the whole sky mid-lesson). Restoring a guardian is what wakes it.
- **Indoors there is no season** (2026-10-13, WORLD-30; user, 2026-10-13: "No season indoors (Recommended)": "Indoors always looks the same: no snow on floors, and an indoor pulse doesn't redraw the tiles (its petals or snow particles also stop falling indoors, for consistency)."). In a `Room` with `interior` set, `RoomMapNode._clipped` sets `band_of_season` (0, 0, 0, 0) on its copies of the floor material (ground, ledges, cracks, secret walls) and of `back_tiles_seasonal_material` (the `[back]` layers, shade 0.55), so every season draws band 0, as indoor backdrops have one band. A pulse played in an interior mounts no `pulse_particles` (`ColorPulse._indoors`, through `Room.at`); its memory, tint and song effect are unchanged. Region weather was already hidden indoors (`RegionWeather`). Tests: `room_map_node_test.test_an_interior_draws_one_season`, `color_pulse_indoors_test`.
- **The title's valley** (`scenes/ui/title/title.tscn`) references band 0 of the village valley's stacked layers directly (`region_rect` 0, 0, 1024, 384) with no seasonal material: they wear `title_grey_material`, every pixel forgotten, so the season never shows. Colour on the title lives only in the logo's flute and its pulse (`systems/screens`).
- **Generated grass** (WORLD-23, `systems/grass`) picks its band per game pixel exactly as the tiles do, so a blade and the turf under it swap on the same pixels; winter has no blades (the snow lip shows).

- **No falling weather under rock** (MEM-05; user, 2026-11-11: "Yes, every region (Recommended)"): `RegionWeather` fades out over 0.5 s in any room where `Room.has_sky()` is false (interiors and generator-marked `covered` rooms), in every region and season; the first room of a world build shows at once. It multiplies every layer's alpha by `Settings.weather_intensity()` and never writes `amount` (that respawns the particles). A region with a `WeatherProfile.gust_particles` gets a layer driven sideways by `RegionWind.drive()` at z -10 (`RegionWeather.GUST_Z`), behind the ground and in front of back walls and backdrops (MEM-16) (`systems/air`, Regional weather).

## The year (STORY-07, 2026-11-16)

"Pelo relógio (Recommended)" (user, 2026-11-16): at 1.0 the village passes through its four seasons, about 4 minutes each while Ivo is outdoors there, with a 12 s dithered turn (`architecture/story-07-epilogue`, "The year").

- **Data:** `YearStats` (`resources/world/year/year_stats.gd`; `village_year.tres`: order SPRING, SUMMER, AUTUMN, WINTER, `season_seconds` 240, `turn_seconds` 12, `when` `world_restored.tres`). `YearMath.at(elapsed, stats, start)` (pure) gives `{season, next, turn}`: a season holds `season_seconds - turn_seconds`, then `turn` rises 0..1 (smoothstep) and the next season commits.
- **`RegionYear`** (`scenes/world/rooms/year/region_year.gd`, the `Year` child of `home_village.tscn`, `Region.year`): the only owner of the elapsed time, in memory only (a world build starts at `Region.season`, turn 0). It advances in `_physics_process` (pausable, scaled) only while `MemoryField.year` is itself (Game follows Ivo's region), its `when` holds, Ivo's room is in its region and `has_sky()`, he is not `is_in_story()`, and no visible source with negative strength reaches the view (`forgetting_in_view`), so no grey pixel on screen ever turns. Otherwise it holds, mid-turn included. `season_changed(season)` at each commit.
- **One writer each:** `Game` stays the only writer of `MemoryField.season`, `palette`, and the new `year` and `next_palette`: at a room change it follows the region's `year` and writes `Region.current_season()`; on `season_changed` while it follows that year it writes the new season.
- **Renderer:** the globals `greyhush_region_season_next` and `greyhush_season_turn` (0 without a year), and the region tint lerped from `palette` to `next_palette` by the turn. The pulses' tints are untouched.
- **Mask:** `season_mask.gdshader` writes `next + 1` into R where no pulse swapped the pixel and `greyhush_season_turn > BAYER[...]` (strict greater, as the pulse test). The Bayer cell is read at `floor(px - turn_origin)`, `px = UV * game_size` and `turn_origin` the canvas origin rounded, so the turn's cells stay on the world's game pixels as the camera moves instead of crawling over the screen. No `TIME`, no `FRAGCOORD`; G is unchanged. Every consumer already decodes R, so no consumer shader changed.
- **Weather:** `RegionWeather` is unchanged: turned pixels read as swapped, so the current season's flakes or petals leave them; at the commit it mounts the new season's particles.
- Interiors never turn (band 0, no weather) and hold the year; the rest of the world keeps its own season.
- **Band audit (2026-11-16):** every seasonal drawable the village shows outdoors has its own picture in each band: the ground tiles (`village/earth.png`, `stone.png`, `wood.png`, every cell drawn in all three bands), the generated grass (`village_earth_grass`, `village_stone_grass`: no blades in winter), every layer of `village_valley` and of the five `waterfall_garden_*` sets, and the props on `prop_seasonal_material` (`bush`, `flower_pot_a/b/c`, `garden_bed_planted`, `village_tree`, the garden's `flower_bed_{mixed,white,yellow}` and `old_tree`). The drawables that never change with the season: the built wall `wall.png` and `crack.png` (one picture in every band; built walls stay one band, above), `village_strata` (one band, the interiors' rock), and everything without a seasonal material: the facades, the other props (barrels, carts, fences, lanterns, the well head, the market stalls, the swing, the flat stone, the falls and their cliff and foam), the villagers, the hens and the cat. New seasonal art for any of them is a separate roadmap item.
- Tests: `year_math_test`, `region_year_test`, `game_season_test`, `greyhush_renderer_season_test` (headless renders nothing, so the mask's turn is held to its source).
