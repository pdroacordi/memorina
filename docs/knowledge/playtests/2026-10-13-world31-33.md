## What was tested

Windowed `tools/playtest` timelines (scratchpad, not kept): standing and walking in woods_deep's pocket (-3664, 280) and at its shaft's foot (-3104, 60); walking and jumping on the open crater's main path (-1500, 1795); a wall-jump attempt up orchard_chasm_bridge's east wall toward the cairn (-3248, -2016).

## Findings

- The pocket reads as a hollow in the root mound: the darker earth back wall fills it under both stone ceilings (`pocket_west.png`), and at the shaft's foot the woods' sky stops at the stone ceiling's line (`shaft_foot.png`). Both stills predate the row-31 fix (`bugs/woods-deep-pocket-back-wall-stops-short-of-the-east-wall-at-row-31`).
- The crater's inner wall ends in a ragged ridge of basalt column tops against the landscape's floor; no straight line shows (`crater_path.png`, `crater_jump.png`). A first rim strip ending exactly on the wall's top row left a 1 px line of the near layer's floor between the two quads; the strip now runs 8 px on over the wall in the wall's pixels.
- The cairn was not seen in play: placing Ivo above lake_chasm's top takes him out of every room, and the wall-jump timeline went through the passage under the lake's stone overhang instead of onto the wall top. The cover is checked numerically (`backdrop_seams_test.test_a_region_tops_cut_shows_only_the_shared_sky`: the seam column is opaque from 88 px over the tops, past the 53 px the lake's peaks reach).
- With the sky at vertical scroll 0, the far layers move against it by their own scroll (0.07 for the peaks) instead of 0.04; in the crater, where the camera can sit about 560 px from the reference, a little more of the sky's lower bands shows under the peaks. Nothing opens a gap; a still cannot judge whether the drift reads.

## Ratings rationale

Aesthetics 4: both visible fixes remove a straight line and fit the regions' art. Fun and fluidity 3: nothing about play changed. Not judged: the parallax drift in motion and the cairn in a real climb.
