## What was tested

Every timeline is in `tools/playtest/scripts/world29_*.json`. All runs used `"locale": "en"` and a redirected `APPDATA`. Seam timelines were also run on a HEAD export.

- `world29_fv_walk_east`: walk from the landing (700, -1632) to x 2612 in lake_waterfall_path. Log every 0.25 s, a frame every 0.6 s. Six stills at x 760..2520.
- `world29_fv_drop` (double jump and roll unlocked) tries every way down through the deck: look_down held, look_down + jump, a straight double jump (apex -1878, lands at 675 px/s), the same with look_down held through the fall, a roll, a ground attack with down held and an air down-slash.
- `world29_fv_jump_run`: eight running double jumps east to west along the whole deck.
- `world29_fv_stairwell`: walk to the stairwell, Up, then a per-frame trace for 6 s on lake_well's catch. Up through the hatch, then walk back onto the deck.
- `world29_well_bank_to_catch`: the base-kit way back to the catch from lake_well's bank. `world29_well_catch_jump_and_off`: a jump on the catch, then a walk off its east end.
- Seams: `world29_seam_fv_west`, `world29_seam_fv_east` and `world29_seam_wp_to_fv` (fishing village <-> lake_shore and <-> lake_waterfall_path), `world29_fs_walk_west_seam` (frost_shore -> threshold_wayshrine) and `world29_seam_fs_east` (frost_shore -> frost_cliffs). Logs every 0.05 s, frames every 0.1 s, on both trees.
- `world29_mill_walk`: across the plank bridge, then a double jump with look_down held, landing back on the planks. Three stills.

## Findings

Ranked. None blocks a commit.

1. **New, medium-low (art): the village tileset meets legacy stone at both seams.** Since WORLD-29, lake_fishing_village uses `tileset = village`; it was `legacy`. Two different stone materials now meet at x 640 and x 2560:
   - lake_shore's legacy brick floor runs into the village cobble landing.
   - The hanging stubs (col 0 and col 59, rows 0-4) are cobble beside the neighbour's brick.
   - Seen in `seam_fv_west` at t 1.8 and `seam_wp_to_fv` at t 2.0 (`seam_lake_shore_legacy_vs_village.png`, `seam_waterfall_path_legacy_vs_village.png`).
   - Inside the room the cobble landing and the grey plank read fine against the snow backdrop. The clash shows only at the seams, in the frames where both rooms are on screen: the clamp at each side, and the glides.
   - Switching this room to `frozen_lake` later will not remove the cut while lake_shore and lake_waterfall_path stay `legacy`.
2. **New, low (art): the fisher hut's stone plinth now stands on the 6 px plank deck.** It has the same 80 px post spacing as open deck (`deck_middle_x1500.png`, stills x 1500/1900). A heavy stone course on one thin plank over open water reads slightly off. Denser posts under the hut, or a wooden sill, would settle it.
3. **New, low (judgement): the post feet.** At play scale (2x game px) they read right: the post meets the water at a pale ripple, then a 12 px submerged part pulled toward the lake blue, then the reflection (`deck_middle_x1500.png`). Two things only show when zoomed in (`post_foot_zoom_8x.png`):
   - The reflection under the foot is darker and more saturated than the submerged part (game rows 308-317: (77, 51, 31) against (93, 80, 77) above). It reads as a dark base on the post more than as a reflection. The row-band slide also shifts it ~1-2 px sideways.
   - Inside a remembered patch of water (still x 1900, window x 102), the post above the water is grey (100, 97, 99) but its reflection under the foot is brown (74, 50, 31). The water's memory column is lit there and the post's top is not.
   Neither is wrong for the greyhush model, and neither reads as broken in motion.
4. **New, low (feel): the catch is a crawlspace.** Row 0 is closed, so the catch is two rows tall, with 10 px between Ivo's head and the ceiling. A jump there is an 8.6 px hop into the ceiling (`well_catch_jump_and_off`, t 1.3). Before, the mouth above cols 8-11 let a jump rise. Nothing requires a jump there.
5. **Pre-existing, low (unchanged from HEAD; 16 of 17,920 band pixels differ): the glide into lake_waterfall_path shows a cave strip under the lake.** For ~0.3 s (`seam_fv_east` t 1.75-2.05, cam y -1697 to -1686) the frame is ~20 px below the fishing village. The lake's bottom edge then ends in a hard line against lake_waterfall_path's cave backdrop, drawn in the ring (`east_glide_cave_under_lake.png`). The reverse glide does the same. The continuous lake makes the line more visible. It may be the intended ring owner here: the pixel is also in lake_waterfall_path's west side band. Not filed.
6. **Pre-existing (known since `playtests/2026-09-23-lake-and-water-hazard`): the grey lake's reflection band is checkerboarded by the ramp dither.** It shows over the whole grey half of the deck (stills x 760, 1100).

What passes:

- **The fishing village reads as one lake under a pier, not broken rectangles.** Compare `before_head_x1680.png` (lake drawn over stone, cut by the well mouth) with `deck_middle_x1500.png` and `landing_stairwell_x760.png`. One flat waterline crosses the room from the landing to the east seam. The 22 posts stand in it and reflect.
  - The frame clamps at the room's bottom (cam y -1716, frame bottom -1536), so the lake always fills the bottom 64 game px.
  - The lake ends flush against the landing's stone on the west and against lake_waterfall_path's stone column on the east.
- **The deck cannot be dropped through.**
  - Walk: x 700 to 2612 at a constant 192 px/s, y -1632.0 at every log, `floor=true` throughout. The room changes to lake_waterfall_path at x ~2577.
  - Every down input in `fv_drop` left Ivo on the deck. A landing at 675 px/s with look_down held is caught. The roll stays at y -1632.1.
  - The deepest y in the eight-jump run is -1632.0.
  - No way into the lake was found.
- **Stairwell and hatch.**
  - The stairwell's Up prompt is in reach at x 760. Up fades out over 0.5 s and places Ivo at (1584, -1440) on the catch, facing right.
  - Control returns 1.1 s after the press (`pmode` 0 at t 3.6).
  - All 360 traced frames over the next 6 s are pos (1584.00, -1440.00), vel 0: no blow-off at the squall's top edge.
  - The hatch prompt stands beside the hatch at head height and is uncut (`well_catch_under_hatch.png`).
  - Up returns Ivo to (752, -1632) on the landing, with the prompt showing. He walks straight out onto the deck.
- **Back to the catch with the base kit.** From the bank (1776, -1346): the step, then one jump west. The squall carries him at up to 612 px/s, and he lands on the catch at x 1429.7 and stays. A walk off the catch's east end lands on the step, and the squall returns him to the bank (1543, -1344), as designed.
- **frost_shore.** The lake now runs the full width under the beach. Before, it was a rectangle starting mid-screen over stone (`frost_shore_x9100.png`).
  - The beach row's underside is a thin frost fringe with the waterline right under it. It reads as a stone ledge touching water, not odd edge art (`frost_shore_beach_underside_4x.png`).
  - At memory 0.05 the lake is a flat dark gradient, with no reflection.
  - The west side is a region cut, so the lake's end at x 8320 is never on screen. The east glide into frost_cliffs shows the lake ending cleanly against the cliffs' stone.
- **Mill bridge.** The water sits between the earth banks with no earth band over it, and the wheel dips into it (`mill_bridge_x2150.png`). Walking the planks keeps y at 287.9. A double jump with look_down held lands back on the planks.
- **No new engine errors in any run** (the runner's two `get_space` door errors are filtered).

## Ratings rationale

- **Fun 3:** inferred. The change is mostly visual, plus one route: the stairwell trip is quick (1.1 s press to control) and the catch arrival is calm. The cramped catch is the only rough spot.
- **Fluidity 3:** walking and jumping on the deck are flat and constant by the numbers (no stall in the logged runs). Glides at both fishing village seams and into frost_cliffs match HEAD: the logged camera centres are identical, apart from rows where the input landed one physics frame apart (3.2 px of walk). Screenshots cannot show input latency, camera smoothing feel, or how the door fade feels.
- **Aesthetics 3:** a clear step up from HEAD. The pier reads as a pier, the lakes as lakes, and the frost shore's lake no longer starts mid-screen. Held back by the brick/cobble seams, the grey reflection checkerboard and the hut plinth on a plank.
- **Not judged:** the reflection's ripple and band motion, the glints in motion and audio. Whether the colour lake reads at full memory was not judged either: only a small remembered patch near the hut was coloured in these runs, and the frost shore was fully grey. A human look at a remembered fishing village is still needed.
