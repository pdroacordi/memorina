# CORE-13 round 1: the boar and the scorpion

**Ratings (low confidence, from frames and logged timing; a human must confirm):**
fun 3/5, fluidity 3/5, aesthetics 3/5.

## Summary

The working tree was tested windowed, uncommitted, on top of `f59bfb4`. Results:
- Both creatures gather out of the grey when Ivo comes near.
- The fray reads in grey rooms and in the lit summer trial.
- The boar's tell, charge and ledge daze, and the scorpion's tell, strike and stuck, all
  happen in the order and at the times the data gives.
- Contact and the attacks each cost 1 life.
- A pogo on the scorpion's front lands, and one over its raised tail hurts Ivo.
- The death dissolve takes 0.6 s.
- The fray keeps re-rolling while a Congelar performance freezes the world.
- The summer trial lure still works.

Two genuine bugs were filed:
- Ivo's own `GreyhushShield` puts colour back on any creature within sword reach.
- A boar charge that hits Ivo by the bridge ledge dazes on top of him, and contact takes a
  second life after his 0.85 s invulnerability ends.

Four old timelines had lost their creature, three of them since the world rebuild. Their
start positions were fixed.

## Build and method

- **Build:** working tree, `--import` at 11:29:23. At 11:45:33 a sibling changed four files:
  `boar_charge.tres` and `scorpion_strike*.tres` (move `reach` cut to y -16..16 and y -21..8:
  `bugs/a-scorpion-strikes-at-an-ivo-on-a-one-cell-ledge-it-cannot-touch`), and
  `corruption_edge.gdshaderinc` (a latent precision fix).
  - Runs before 11:45: the boar vault (`boar2`, `boar3`), the scorpion fight and pogo runs,
    `world35s7`, and the unedited bridge timeline.
  - The decisive fight and pogo runs were re-run on the new code (boar vault, scorpion
    step-back punish, the three pogo positions). Every run from the map timelines on used
    the new code.
  - The new code changed one result: the front pogo at 21 px (below).
- **Harness:** `tools/playtest/playtest_runner.tscn`, with a fresh `APPDATA` per run, checked
  by a headless `OS.get_user_data_dir()` probe before each run. Facts come from the runner's
  `trace` foes field (`hp`, phase, `knocked`), measured in physics frames. Frames were read
  as world-anchored crops (cam_centre from `log`).
- Timelines were generated by script in the session scratchpad. The repo's `core13_*` drafts
  start in the wrong places (below) and were not run as shipped.

## Findings

### 1. The gather (dissolve reversed): pass

- **Boar.** It wakes when Ivo's feet come within 207 px (`trace`: dormant → `-` at t 1.461
  with dx 207.5). It forms over ~0.3 s (`boar_gathers.png`):
  - +0.04 s: a sparse dithered core;
  - +0.14 s: a denser core;
  - +0.24 s: whole, with bristle strays.
- **Scorpion** (caldera_bait): the same, pale grey on dark rock, from 2.01 s
  (`scorpion_gathers_and_tells.png`, frames 1-3).
- **Gathers off frame.** With the wake radius 200, the gather can happen beside the frame's
  edge, and at the bridge it was partly hidden by the camera's look-ahead as Ivo turned. A
  player walking in at speed may see a whole boar rather than the gathering.
- **Spawning inside the radius:** a spawn inside the wake radius gathers during the load, so
  the creature is already whole at t 1.0 (`map_*` runs).

### 2. The fray reads in grey: pass, with a caveat at low hp

- **At rest:** a ragged 1-art-px edge and up-drifting strays on the boar (bristles), and
  diagonal strays on the scorpion. It reads against the threshold's light grey and the
  caldera's dark grey. In the summer trial under Sombra's pulse the scorpion is full orange
  and still frays.
- **Hp fray:** it rises with damage (`boar_hurt_fray_and_death_dissolve.png`: hp 4, then 1).
  At 1 hp the boar is a loose cloud. That reads as "nearly dead", but its tell pose is then
  hard to see under the fray.

### 3. Tells and latency

**Boar** (`boar_walk_tell_charge.png`):
- The tell is 0.60 s (2.947 → 3.549 by trace, every run). The head drops and the agitation
  visibly scatters the back and the bristles. The charge frame is calmer and longer.
- From the tell to contact with a standing Ivo at ~190 px is ~1.2 s (charge 300 px/s).
- **Feel (inference):** generous. A jump pressed 0.1 s into the charge clears it every time.
  A jump pressed at the start of the tell (0.6 s early) lands in its path (`boar2`: hit at
  4.147).

**Scorpion bait:**
- The tell is 0.55 s (3.461 → 4.011) and the strike is 0.20 s, so a standing Ivo is hit at
  the end of the tell.
- **Feel (inference):** a fair but quick read for the "faster" variant.
- **The tail is too thin for the agitation.** It is 1-2 art px wide, so every pixel is an edge
  pixel. Under agitation it dissolves into a dotted line (tell frames 3.6-4.0 in the session).
  The tell reads as "the tail dissolves", not "the tail cocks". The raised silhouette, the
  intended cue, is the part that disappears. Worth a look when tuning `agitation` or the edge
  weights.

### 4. The weak clips with their timing

- **Boar daze: pass, at the ledge.** The charge ends on the ledge probe with the snout at the
  lip. The boar's feet stop at x 7159-7171 (the deck starts at 7136) every run. The daze lasts
  1.00 s (`boar_dazed_at_the_ledge.png`). The daze pose differs from idle only slightly at
  game scale; the stop and the 1 s of stillness carry it.
- **Scorpion stuck: pass.** The tail drops and the claws stay out for 0.6 s. That window is
  punishable: step back 0.3 s during the tell, step in 0.22 s, slash and chop. That hit at
  4.611 and 4.961 (8 → 7 → 5), both inside the window.
  - A **roll** at the tell carries Ivo ~150 px. From there the 0.6 s stuck window cannot be
    reached (`scor3`).
  - **Feel (inference):** the dodge that punishes is a short step, not the roll a player
    reaches for.
- **Strike:** the 4-frame strike is a blur at 20 fps. It reads only through Ivo's hurt flash.
- **Boar vault and punish:**
  - A jump straight up 0.12 s into the charge lands Ivo 46 px behind the dazed boar after
    ~1.2 s in the air, about 0.45 s into the daze. One or two swings fit.
  - With a steer over the boar, Ivo lands 55-62 px away, out of reach, and the daze runs out
    before he swings (`boar3`: first hit 0.4 s after the daze).
  - **Feel (inference):** the punish window after a vault is short. Whether it feels earned
    or stingy needs a human.

### 5. Damage: pass, with one bug

- Every charge, strike and contact took exactly 1 life (3 → 2 → 1 → 0, with no double count
  in one frame). The boar is armoured in its charge: Ivo killed it mid-charge at 6.814 with no
  knockback on it.
- **Bug:** `bugs/a-boar-charge-that-hits-ivo-by-a-ledge-dazes-inside-him-and-contact-takes-a-second-life`.
  This happened in 4 of 4 runs where Ivo stood in the charge by the bridge ledge. The charge
  knocks him ~70 px west, the boar dazes 0-15 px from his feet, and its contact lands again
  0.84-0.85 s later, the frame his invulnerability ends.

### 6. Pogo: pass, narrow window

The test needed open sky: both lure tunnels have a 96 px ceiling, so Ivo rises only 42 px and
can never get above a scorpion there. The bait scorpion was led out to caldera_bait cols 9-10.
Ivo double-jumped over it during its tell and pogoed on the way down
(`scorpion_pogo_front_trade_tail.png`):

| Ivo's x relative to its feet (it faces him) | Result |
|---|---|
| 28 px ahead | misses, lands beside it |
| 21 px ahead | old code: pogo and contact in the same frame (both lose 1); new code (20.9 px): clean pogo |
| 17.7-18 px ahead | clean pogo, bounce, scorpion 8 → 7 (both builds) |
| 12 px ahead | tail guard hurts Ivo |
| 7 px behind (over the tail) | tail guard hurts Ivo at 46 px above its feet; scorpion unhurt |

The front pogo works, but the window is about 9 px wide. On one side is the 24 px `Guard`
plus Ivo's 22 px body; on the other side the front falls away. The scorpion's front is only
24 px tall, and its contact box top sits 4 px under its hurtbox top, so a late pogo trades.
**Feel (inference):** a player will often get hurt "pogoing the front". A tuning note, not
filed as a bug (the trade did not reproduce on the new build).

### 7. Death dissolve: pass

At hp 0 the body keeps its momentum (it slid ~40 px from the charge) and dissolves from the
edge inward. It becomes a checkered cloud with brown strays, then nothing, in 0.60 s (6.814 →
7.414/7.447). Contact was off while it slid past Ivo's feet (no hurt).

### 8. The fray during a song's freeze: pass

Ivo stood on the step east of the bridge deck, the woken boar idle on the deck. He played
Congelar (`clock: real`). From the first paused frame (paused=true at ~8.4 s real), the snow
in a control region stopped (0 changed pixels from 9.0 s). The boar's region changed 900-2484
pixels every second or third 0.1 s frame (a 0.25 s re-roll) through 13.0 s, while its
position stayed at dx -233.1 throughout (`boar_fray_rerolls_under_freeze.png`,
`freeze_context_f09.20.png`). This matches user decision 4.

### 9. The summer trial lure: still works

`song_shadow_lure` was run unchanged: Sombra at col 45, onto the roof at ~7.5 s.
- The scorpion wakes as Ivo passes (~6.9 s), walks to the shadow, telegraphs at 12.0 and
  strikes it at 12.5.
- It walks home from 13.0 s. That is the old report's "breaks it".
- Ivo drops down the shaft at 16.0 (timeline). By then the scorpion is back. The chamber
  behind it (cols 56-57) lies inside its 320 px leash, so it reaches Ivo there and strikes him
  at ~19.9 (hp 2).
- **Feel (inference):** the slip window is ~8.5-13.5 s. The timeline's 16 s drop is late, and
  the "safe behind it" chamber is not safe.

### 10. Ivo's shield colours creatures in melee: bug

`bugs/ivos-greyhush-shield-colours-a-creature-in-melee-range`. In grey rooms, every creature
pixel within ~38 px of Ivo's chest draws in its art colour:
- the scorpion's claws turn orange and red during its tell;
- the boar's snout turns brown;
- the stinger turns orange during a pogo.

The creature is cut by a soft circle. See `scorpion_coloured_inside_ivos_shield.png` and
`boar_snout_coloured_inside_ivos_shield.png`, each with a control frame.

## Other observations

- **Knocked off the ledge.** Ivo's slash and chop knock a dazed boar back. In the edited
  `traverse_opening_e_bridge_gap` (new code) two hits pushed it off the ledge into the gap
  pit (y 256), where it wanders at 1 hp below the bridge for the rest of the run. Its ledge
  probe guards only its own steps. Not filed; a design question: should knockback respect
  ledges?
- **Stalemate below the deck.** A boar on the bridge deck and Ivo on the lower step east of
  it (dy 64) is a stalemate. The charge reach (y -16..16 on the new code) never holds, so it
  stands at the ledge facing him. That is expected.
- `world35s7_b2_bait_past_the_scorpion` still shows what it meant: one bait hit (hp 2), then
  past it to the dry moat, as with the brute. `b22_over_the_scorpion` (t 5.5) catches Ivo 430
  px west of the scorpion, not over it; the name came from the brute version.

## What this cannot judge

- **Input latency and weight.** Whether the tell-to-charge 0.6 s and the 0.55 s strike tell
  *feel* fair. The numbers above are measured; fairness is inferred.
- **Readability at speed.** Whether a player notices the boar gathering at the edge of a
  moving frame. Frames are still.
- **Audio.** No creature has sound yet. The tells rely on the fray alone.
- **Daze punish feel.** Whether ~0.45 s after a vault feels rewarding.
- **Fray readability in colour.** How the fray reads in a coloured region over time. Seen
  only in the summer trial's lit pulse.
