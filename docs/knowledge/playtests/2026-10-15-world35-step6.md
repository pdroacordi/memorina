# WORLD-35 step 6: the wreck and its hold

Windowed debug runs of `tools/playtest/scripts/world35s6_*.json` (three timelines) with a per-frame trace. Positions are
Ivo's feet in world px. The game frame is 640x360. The runner never writes a player's save (`BootPolicy`). Base kit.

## What was checked

| Timeline | Result |
|---|---|
| `a_shore_to_the_letter_and_back` | Up at the breach (8912, 288) fades into the hold at its door (25712, 12288), facing east. Running jumps take Ivo up the first floor step, the middle plank and the bow plank to the slot (col 17). Walking west, he drops off the planks and down both floor steps to the west wall, steps back onto the door, and Up fades him out at the breach (8912, 288), facing east. Five runs, each ending at the letter and back on the shore. |
| `b_shore_approach` | From x 8600, east of the rock spur, Ivo walks along the wreck to the breach (8925), where the prompt shows, and on to the breaker's foot (9237). The flat run stays flat: the facade never collides. |
| `c_middle_plank_to_the_bow` | Standing on the middle plank (25960, 12160), one running jump lands on the bow plank (26109 and 26096 in two runs). |

## Findings

1. **The wreck reads as a ship on the beach** (b00, a00). Ivo stands in the breach. The door's open frame is clear, so
   the hole is the facade's own (b02). The bow lifts beside the shifted breaker (b03). No walkable roof: the gunwale
   slopes about 60 px and its snapped ribs break it.
2. **The hold reads as a ship's hold**: the `ship_hold` wall's ribs and planks stand behind the listing deck (a01, c00).
   The frost edge's memory, 0.05, greys it all, as in lighthouse_interior. The wooden `door` inside works as a bulkhead.
3. **The hop to the bow plank was 10 px short under the first deck.** Under the row-1 deck a jump's feet top out at
   y 12118, 10 px over the bow plank (12128), so the hop landed only when Ivo was already under the bow's higher deck at
   his apex. Two of the first runs fell to the bow floor. The deck over the middle plank's east end (cols 11-12) now
   starts at row 0: the hop clears the plank by 42 px and lands in every run of timeline c.
4. **Fixed press times in timeline a land at different moments from run to run.** The runner counts `t` against
   variable frame times, so the jump arcs start a few px apart. Every run reached the letter, but some through the bow
   floor instead of the middle plank. Timeline c shows the plank hop from a standing start.

## What this cannot judge

- Whether the letter on the bow plank is worth the stop before STORY-06 writes it.
- Feel. The hold is one screen with three jumps.
