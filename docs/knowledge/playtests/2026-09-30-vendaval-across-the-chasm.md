## What was run

`tools/playtest/scripts/song_gale_chasm.json`: Ivo three cells back from the chasm edge in
the autumn trial, plays GALE, runs and jumps into the natural current.

## What the frames show

- First run: the fully radial gale launched the jumping Ivo far above the tree line - lift
  fed itself as he rose. Fixed (`GaleShape.VERTICAL_SHARE`, `wind_lift` halved); the arc
  is now a long, flat glide, landing on the far bank of the 16-cell chasm.
- The pulse's colour spreads from the play spot; the far bank sits just inside the disc's
  edge, so the landing happens as the colour thins - a good read of "the song got me here".
- The current's streaks and leaves read as weather; the gale's radial streaks are faint at
  this zoom. Fun 3, fluidity 3, aesthetics 3.

## What stills cannot tell

Whether the glide feels controllable, whether the eye's stillness is noticeable before the
run, and whether 17 cells against a 16-cell chasm leaves enough slack for a human's timing.
