## What was tested

Four timelines, run during the implementation itself (not by the playtester agent):

- `water_hazard_fall.json` (existing): one fall into the Downtown pool, watching the HUD.
- `death_rewind.json`: three falls into the same pool, so the third kills; then the death beat
  and the reload with no bench saved.
- `bench_sit_and_stand.json`: walk up to the summer trial's bench, sit with down, a jump press,
  sit again, walk off.
- `bench_rest_and_death.json`: sit on the Downtown bench, then walk right through the brute and
  into the pool until dead; the world comes back.

## Findings

- **The lost note forgets on the Bayer cell** (`life_note_fade.png`, rows top to bottom over
  ~1 s): gold, a stippled sweep, then grey in the faded print. The row's sway is out of phase
  note to note, and the HUD stays drawn over the fade.
- **Death beat** (`death_rewind.png`): the corpse is seen sinking, the screen darkens, and the
  world is rebuilt at the authored start with three coloured notes. No errors in the log: the
  self-swap leaves no stranded coroutine or group behind.
- **Sitting** (`bench_sit_and_stand.png`): the `Down` key shows only in reach, sitting hides it,
  the crouch loop plays, a jump press stands him up without jumping (he is still on the floor
  in frame 03), and walking gets him off.
- **Rest then death** (`bench_rest_and_death.png`): he comes back on the Downtown bench with full
  life (frame 08, then up at once because the timeline still holds right), and the brute stands
  in its place again (frame 10).
- **Harness note, not a bug**: Ivo's authored start is in Bloom Hollow, so a `player_position`
  in Downtown costs a room transition (about 1 s with input dropped) - the first press of a
  timeline there must wait for it. The first run of `bench_rest_and_death.json` sat at t 1.5,
  lost the press, and so "died back" to the authored start; the timeline now waits.
- **Not seen**: a death mark. The Downtown deaths fell inside the Downtown well, where memory is
  already near 0, so a small negative source changes nothing visible there - which is the design
  (marks only read where the world is remembered). A mark is covered by `region_memory_test.gd`.

## Ratings rationale

- **Fun 0**: not judged - these are system checks, not play.
- **Fluidity 3**: the beats run without hitches or stuck states in the frames, but the death
  hold (0.6 s) and the arrival fade are untuned numbers, and input latency cannot be judged from
  stills.
- **Aesthetics 3**: the note HUD reads well and belongs to the greyhush; the bench is still a
  flat placeholder box, and sitting is the template's crouch, not a seated pose.
