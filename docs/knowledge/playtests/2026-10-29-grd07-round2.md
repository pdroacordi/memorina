## What was tested

This session checks the fixes for the user's three complaints, after this morning's playtest
(`playtests/2026-10-29-grd07-guardian-art`).

- **Full fights:** `guardian_fight_bot_driver.gd` with `guardian_bot_bloom.json` and
  `guardian_bot_frost.json` (`BOT_GOD=1`). A scratch copy of the driver took a screenshot every
  0.1 s through lucidity, instead of every 0.25 s. Bloom reached RESTORED at 40 s and Frost at
  61 s, with both recalls learned.
- **Frost swipe distance probe:** a new `-s` driver (scratch, not committed). On every swipe
  telegraph it walks Ivo, closed-loop, until his feet are TARGET px from the golem at the swing.
  It never attacks. It screenshots every frame the hitbox is live and logs dx, the sprite frame
  and hp. Runs at TARGET 90, 110 and 130, 40 s each, plus 110 again on the 20:30 code.
- **Bloom standing:** `BOT_PASSIVE=1 BOT_KEEP=88`. Ivo stands about where the stag paces and never attacks.
- **Pogo:** `BOT_BOUNCE=1` on both guardians, 60 s each.
- **Sheets:** every frame of `frost_guardian_attack_2.png` and both `*_leap.png` sheets, in
  numbered grids.

## 1. Leap clips (crouch, rise, fall, land): clips pass; the landing is mostly unseen

- **The clips play in order on all 5 leaps** (`[spr]` log). Bloom: `leap` fr 0-1 (crouch) for
  0.3 s before take-off, fr 2-3 while rising, fr 4-5 after the apex, fr 6-7 on touchdown, then
  `lucid`. Frost: fr 0-2, 3-4, 5, 6-7. The 20:30 code gives the same sequence to within 0.05 s.
- **The airborne pose holds for the whole flight.** Bloom leap 1: rise frame 3 held 10.02-10.63 s,
  fall frame 5 held 10.83-11.54 s, until touchdown. Frost leap 1: fr 4 held 10.97-11.57 s, fr 5
  held 11.57-12.49 s. No frame of `lucid` or `walk` appears mid-air.
- **The crouch reads.** The stag drops into a low lean and springs up onto its toes
  (`bloom_leap_crouch_spring.png`). The golem raises both fists, then tilts forward as it lifts
  off (`frost_leap_air_and_land.png`, top row). Both face Ivo before the leap and after it
  (snout and fists toward him).
- **What the player actually sees of the flight:** the crouch, the first ~0.3 s of the rise,
  and then nothing. The body is above the top of the frame for ~1 s (Frost feet at screen y
  -60..0 from 11.4 to 11.9 s).
- **Low bug: the land clip plays mostly off screen.** It is unseen on 2 of 5 landings and
  half-seen for its last ~0.2 s on the other 3. The guardian comes down 440 px from Ivo, at
  screen x 882 or -42, before the camera pairs (`bloom_leap_lands_off_frame.png`: sheet up, Ivo
  alone, the stag walking in from the edge in lucid). Filed as
  `bugs/the-lucidity-leap-lands-outside-the-frame-so-its-land-clip-is-not-seen`. The
  touchdown dust is also off screen.

## 2. Frost swipe hits only where the fist is drawn: pass

The hit window is frames 7-8 and the box is x 40..116. Results by Ivo's distance at the swing
(feet to feet). The dx logged on the hurt frame is already after one frame of knockback.

| Target | Swipes | Hits | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| ~90 px (88.6-89.3) | 5 | 5, all on frame 7 | `frost_swipe_90px.png` |
| ~110 px (111.2-111.8) | 4 + 4 (re-run) | 8, frame 7 | `frost_swipe_110px.png` |
| ~130 px (131.3-132.0) | 6 | 0 | `frost_swipe_130px.png` |

- **Every hit has visual contact.** On the hurt frame the low fist overlaps Ivo's body at 90
  and at 110 px. At 130 px the fist on frames 7-8 stops ~10 game px short of him, and nothing
  hits. The complaint "acerta mesmo o sprite estando bem longe" is not reproducible.
- **The reverse case, contact without a hit (cosmetic only):** on frame 6 (outside the window)
  the raised fist passes over Ivo's head and overlaps his hair at 90-110 px, with no damage. It
  reads as a near miss, then the low frame-7 fist hits. It does not need fixing.

## 3. Frost charge more animated: pass

- The `attack_2` strip (`frost_charge_strip.png`, 14 frames): a crouch (1), a forward lean with
  one fist cocked (2-6), then alternating running strides (7-13). This morning's strip was
  almost idle, with only the shoulder changing.
- In game the stride shows as the golem slides through (`frost_charge_in_game.png`, frame 021,
  knee up and fist forward).
- Still true from this morning, low: most charge hits land through the contact capsule at
  41-42 px a frame before the charge box opens (6 of 9 hits across runs; the other 3 on the
  box, frames 4-6). The damage is the same either way.

## Also checked

- **Bloom lash from where it paces: readable and fair (inferred).** Standing at 74-77 px, Ivo is
  lashed every ~10 s. It is telegraphed 0.36 s with the red tint and a rearing pose, swung
  0.3 s, and then hits on frame 4. Frame 3 draws the vine as a thin line above his head with no
  hit, and frame 4 brings it to chest height on him (`bloom_lash_from_comfort.png`). That is
  ~0.66 s from telegraph to hit, and he stands inside his own 77.5 px slash reach when it comes.
  The vine is 1 art px thick and close to the background in value, so it is easy to lose in the
  grey. This morning's flag "lash weak" is improved but not gone. Whether the warning is long
  enough by hand needs a human.
- **Bloom burst, the shock removed (reach 70): readable.** The stag drops to all fours and rams
  upward with its head, and Ivo is lifted where the skull meets him (`bloom_burst_ram.png`).
  The launch now has a visible cause, so "stamp weak" is resolved. The recall still opens and
  is recalled (7.0 s, both runs).
- **Frost slam, fists against the hit: pass.** Frame 5 (box raised, y -192..-112) does not hurt a
  grounded Ivo. The hit lands on frame 6 as the arm comes down on him
  (`frost_slam_fists_hit.png`), 4 of 4 hits at 71-74 px. This morning's "hit by the wind-up"
  is fixed.
- **Frost's enlarged shield: pass.** On each call note the whole golem saturates, fists and outer
  crystals included, and between notes it drops back to uniform grey (`frost_shield_call.png`).
  This morning's trailing fist and outer crystals stayed grey; now they colour too.
- **Pogo as the opening ("O pogo é a brecha"): works.** The bounce bot filled Bloom's bar to 0.43
  in 30 s and Frost's to 0.75 in 21 s from pogoes alone. Both guardians kept throwing moves
  under it: Bloom pounce, burst and lash; Frost swipe, 4 slams and a charge. The bot also took
  contact damage every 1-3 s when it came down on a head without a pogo. Its policy presses
  attack once per fall at 150 px up, which is under Frost's 176 px head. Treat that as a bot
  artefact, not a finding. A human run is needed for how forgiving the pogo is.
- No new errors in any run (the exit-time "4 resources still in use" only). One "Playback can only
  happen when a node is inside the scene tree" in the Bloom bounce run, at an unknown point.

## What screenshots cannot show

- How the leap feels in motion. The guardian is invisible for ~1 s at the apex, and the landing
  happens off screen.
- Audio, input latency, camera smoothing, and the weight of the golem's charge.
- Whether 0.66 s of lash warning is enough for a person. The bot reacts to state, not to pixels.

## Ratings rationale

- **Fun 3** (inferred). The same loop as this morning, with fewer "what hit me" moments.
- **Fluidity 3.** No stuck states and no facing flips seen in the leap. Docked for the landing
  that happens off frame.
- **Aesthetics 4.** The charge, burst, slam and shield all improved. Docked for the thin lash
  vine and the unseen landing frames.
