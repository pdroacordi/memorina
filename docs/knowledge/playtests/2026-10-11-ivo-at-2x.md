## What was tested

Two timelines, run windowed with `tools/playtest` (memory-only save):

- `tools/playtest/scripts/ivo_2x_moves.json`: skills double jump, wall climb and roll. Spawn at x 0, which falls into Ivo's house at y 12288. Steps: run right, jump and fall, a two-swing ground combo, a jump with an air slash, a roll, a double jump, a running slash to the left, idle, then a double jump into the house's east wall held to the right.
- `tools/playtest/scripts/ivo_2x_climb.json`: the root shaft timeline (`song_root_shaft_climb`) with its own screenshots. It plays Enraizar, then holds up and jumps onto the roots and climbs.

The logged states matched each screenshot: `run`, `jump_start`, `fall_start`, `attack_idle_2`, `attack_jump`, `roll`, `air_spin`, `attack_run_1`, `idle`, `wall_landing`, `wall_slide`, `climb`.

## Findings

- **Density.** Ivo now reads at the world's density. His 2x2 pixels match the furniture, beds and timber (`01_idle`, `02_run`). He is 58 px tall beside 32 px furniture, about the villagers' scale.
- **Facing.** Facing is right both ways. The art faces east and flips cleanly when he walks left (`01_idle` faces left after the running slash). Wall clips face the wall, with the hands on its surface (`07_wall_slide`).
- **Floor contact.** No pose sank into the floor or floated above it while grounded (`01`, `02`, `04`, `06`). `ivo_sheet_test` holds this.
- **Readable clips.** The ground finisher's arc (`04_attack_ground`), the air slash's trail under him (`05_attack_air`) and the somersault roll (`06_roll`) read as what they are. The climb reads as hand over hand on the roots (`08_climb`).
- **Feel: the first ground swing is a quick forward jab with a thin blade.** At 1x scale it is hard to see. The finisher carries the swing.
- **Gameplay: the swings are short.** By the user's "Shrink to the art", the hitboxes now reach 31-53 px forward (was 52-75; first run 27-49, before the attack sheets moved onto the flip axis). Enemies and guardians have not been retuned; that is CORE-09. No enemy was fought in this run.
- **Run.** The first run was closer to a brisk stride than a sprint; the redo runs (see the note above).
- **Hurt.** The hurt flinch, which turns him three-quarters to the camera, was not captured here.
- **Hair and scarf.** The scarf ends trail in the run, jump and air slash frames. Whether the hair's motion reads at speed cannot be judged from stills.
- **Scene quirk (not CORE-08).** Most actions ran against the house's east wall, because the house is only one room wide. The roll therefore played in place against the wall (`06_roll`).

## Ratings rationale

- **Aesthetics 4.** One density, one palette, a consistent face and scarf across clips. Minus one for the thin first jab and a few frames where PixelLab's sword is a 1 art px line.
- **Fluidity 3.** Every clip kept its length and keys, so timing is unchanged by construction. Stills cannot show how the loop seams (run 8 to 1, the brace ping-pong) look in motion, so this score is not confident.
- **Fun 3.** The movement is unchanged. Combat is untested against enemies at the shorter reach; until CORE-09 the shorter reach probably makes fights harder.
