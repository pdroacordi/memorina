## What was tested

This pass is independent of the implementer's `2026-11-19-grd16-sun-fight`. The runs went one window at a time. Before
each run, the run script refused to start if any Godot process was running. Each run's `APPDATA` pointed at its own
scratch folder, checked first with a headless `OS.get_user_data_dir()` probe. Every run's median frame time was 5-6 ms
(`[frame]`), and no run logged a key press from outside the timeline. Spawn: (3226, 1818), `"clock": "real"`.

Driver: a session copy of `guardian_fight_player_driver.gd` (scratchpad `feel16/drv.gd`) with these changes:
- The `[st]` line adds the guardian's screen x and facing, Ivo's screen x and hp.
- The driver quits after a no-god death. The shipped one waits until `max_duration`.
- Pinned roar answers per kit: roll through with the Lake kit.
- `PL_CHARGE=roll` rolls toward the lion 0.12 s before the box arrives. The shipped one rolled away at the jump lead and
  was run down twice in a discarded run.
- `PL_BOXED=1` answers a roar thrown into an ember gap as pinned.
- `PL_PJ=1` jumps out of a pounce shadow over a burning patch.

| run | kit (`skills`) | policy | result |
|---|---|---|---|
| `o1` | Orchard `[0, 1]` | shipped policy (back off, jump the charge) | died 73.8 s in stage 1. 3 hits: roar with Ivo in an ember gap, pounce after a spit, sun shaft 1 at the swing stand-off |
| `l1` | Lake `[2]` | roll through the charge | died 102.9 s in stage 1. 3 hits: roar in an ember gap (x2), pounce after a spit |

## Frantic: tempo per stage (dodging runs)

| stage | busy T+S | busy T+S+R | idle gap mean / max | pressure time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 51-53 % | 70-73 % | 0.67-0.79 / 0.98-1.52 s | 23-25 s (Orchard), 45-46 s (Lake) |
| 1 | 62-66 % | 79-81 % | 0.53-0.59 / 0.67-1.33 s | 28-62 s |

The tempo matches the implementer's and is the busiest of the four guardians: Leaf stage 1 is 55-62 % T+S, and the Sun
reaches 62-66 % at its last stage. A still Ivo dies in 7.4 s; the Frost took 9.97 s. Stage 1 threw all six of its first moves in three of four runs, and
no string was over a third of a stage. Three things thin it for a dodging player:

- **The claw string never chains.** Across 21 claws in four dodging runs, there were 0 second claws and 0 bite lunges,
  because backing off or jumping ends the string. The implementer's final dodging runs (`pl7`, `pl10`, `pl11`) show the same. Only the bot, which
  stands and swings, got them (6 second claws and 2 bites). Two of the six new moves are invisible to a player who
  dodges. The Frost swipe string has the same issue.
- **A stage-0 loop for the roller.** In `l2`, stage 0 settled into ember spit, charge, claw (7 three-string repeats in
  19 strings). After the spit, Ivo is beyond the claw and roar ranges, so the charge is the only far pick. A roll leaves
  him far, so the claw (spit and charge are recent) follows. The Orchard runs had 0 repeats.
- **The Lake kit never sees the return** (bug below).

## Readable

Wind-up start to the first live box (scaled s, all runs):

| move | first live box | n |
|---|---|---|
| claw | 0.60-0.67 | 21 |
| roar (the rear's high box) | 0.82-0.96 | 19 |
| charge | 1.05-1.09 | 30 |
| return | 0.99-1.02 | 4 |
| pounce slam | 1.24-1.29 | 8 |
| ember spit | patches burn 1.65-1.73 after the wind-up starts; each spot is marked as the ember leaves the mouth | 25 |
| sun shafts | first lit at 0.63-0.69, burns 0.6 s later | 13 |

The timing is generous everywhere. The poses mostly are not
(`screenshots/2026-11-19-grd16-sun-feel/windup_crops_claw_charge_spit_roar.png`, crops at game scale round the lion):

- **The claw, charge, ember spit and roar wind-ups barely differ from the idle stance.** The claw's "paw raised" is a few
  dark pixels at the muzzle. The charge's "leaning in" and the spit's "head drawn back" read as standing. The roar's
  crouch shows mostly through the red telegraph pulse. A player tells these four apart by distance and by what follows,
  not by the pose. This is placeholder art (GRD-18), but it is the main readability gap: these are the stage-0 moves.
- **The sun shafts' rear and the pounce's crouch-spring read well**, and so does the charge's run. The two rears look
  alike, but their spawns tell them apart within 0.15 s: the shafts' first column, or the pounce shadow at the strike's
  start.
- **Sun shafts** (`sun_shafts_second_burns_third_lit.png`): a thin vertical line, its fill rising, and a comb on the
  floor while lit, then a wide pale column while burning. The order reads clearly: one burns while the next is lit. The
  lit line is much thinner than the 48 px it will burn, so only the floor comb shows the width. 1 of 50 shafts hit, and
  it was the driver's late step (it swings from 96 px, which is inside shaft 1).
- **Ember spit**: each landing spot shows a bar and a comb from launch, and the burning patch has orange tips on the
  comb. The pods are small light squares. Against the grey caldera stone all of these read.
- **Pounce shadow** (`pounce_shadow_over_burning_patches.png`): a bright outlined bar with a comb, unmissable. When it
  covers ember patches, its white comb and the patches' combs mix into one row of spikes. Which parts are burning is
  hard to tell at a glance, and in that situation it matters (bug below).
- **Off screen**: the lion's centre was on screen at all 119 wind-up starts. Its tail tip was off screen at 16 of 29
  charges (up to 71 px) and 3 of 4 returns (up to 83 px, with the rear of the body off too:
  `return_windup_rear_off_screen.png`). The camera's look-ahead puts Ivo 96 px off centre, away from the lion, whenever
  he has just moved away from it. That is exactly when the far moves are thrown. `test_every_move_is_thrown_from_on_screen`
  assumes Ivo at the view's centre. The head is always visible, so this is cosmetic.

## Dodgeable with each kit

- **Orchard kit** (walk, single jump; the double jump was used only in the forced roar probe): `o2` restored with 1 hit.
  The charges and returns were jumped 14 times with 0 hits, and the return followed 4 of 4 stage-1 charges.
- **Lake kit** (walk, single jump, roll): `l2` restored with 0 hits. Rolling toward the lion through the charge was
  clean 19 of 19 times. One roll through a boxed roar was clean. The return never came (bug below).
- **Punish windows**: the driver's hits all landed in the idle between moves (a hurt clip plays only from idle, so hits
  inside a recovery do not show in `[spr]`). With either kit, stage 0 ended after 23-46 s of pressure.

## Fairness

- **Every move after an ember spit is thrown while its patches burn round Ivo**
  (`bugs/a-pounce-slam-thrown-after-an-ember-spit-cannot-be-walked-out-of`, medium). Of the 44 strings that followed a
  spit (four runs here plus the implementer's three), 43 began their wind-up with Ivo boxed between two patches
  (`ember_gap_then_roar_hit.png`). What follows determines the risk:
  - Charge (13): still a jump or a roll.
  - Claw (3): jump it.
  - Roar (6): the back-off left inside the gap is a few px (the slam reaches 90 px from the lion, and the patch behind
    starts about 100 px out). A jump over the lion or a roll through it works. All four of the driver's roar hits were
    here.
  - Pounce slam (2): the shadow covers both neighbouring patches, so the documented walk-out is impossible. A forced
    probe (`pt1`, 20 pairs) jumped out over the patch at swept times. Presses at 0.0, 0.3 and 0.4 s into the strike were
    never hit (0 of 10). Presses at 0.12-0.22 s were hit by the landing-ember runner as Ivo came down (4 of 4 away from a
    wall). A press at 0.5 s was hit by the descending body (2 of 2 away from a wall). The answer exists, but it is a
    timing a human has no tell for, and it killed both no-god runs that met it.
- **The pinned roar is fixed in play.** `rt1` forced the roar with Ivo teleported to the arena wall and jumped over the
  lion with the direction held from the press. Presses 0.0-0.45 s into the 0.6 s wind-up all cleared (7 of 7, landing
  134-160 px behind it). A press at 0.5 s was hit by the rear's high box. This matches the kit test's ≥0.25 s window
  (`bugs/a-pinned-ivo-jumps-the-roaring-lion-only-in-a-frame-tight-window`). The lion did not turn to an Ivo passing
  over it during the wind-up (facing at strike start = facing at wind-up start, 8 of 8).
- **The return landing on a jumped Ivo**: 0 hits in 4 Orchard-kit returns and 9 of the implementer's.
- **Body contact from the 132x72 body**: 0 hits in the dodging runs. The driver swings from 96 px. The only body hits
  were the descending pounce body above.
- **Two notes from one move or string**: none. All hits were more than 2.5 s apart.

## The return after a charge

- With a jump (Orchard kit), it followed every stage-1 charge (4 of 4; the implementer's 9 of 9). The turn holds still
  for 0.5 s and the box arrives 0.99-1.02 s after the turn starts. Readable and fair.
- **With a roll through the charge (Lake kit) it never followed** (0 of 8;
  `bugs/a-roll-through-the-sun-charge-always-skips-its-stage-1-return`, medium). The charge ends 278-381 px from a
  rolled Ivo, past the return's 208 px range. Once, it ended with the lion's centre 130 px off screen. This contradicts
  "Turn after passing Ivo (Recommended)", the user's 2026-11-19 decision, for the kit that reaches the Caldera from the
  Lake.

## Bugs and checks

- Floating: none. At every wind-up, idle and recovery the lion stood at y 1823.9-1824.0.
- Stuck: none. The longest idle gap was 1.52 s.
- Every clip played: the strips seen were `attack_1`-`attack_3`, `leap`, `walk`, `idle`, `hurt`, `lucid`, `perform` and
  `play_song`.
- Sliding: the known 120 px/s walk on an 84 px/s clip (GRD-18).
  - After Ivo's death the move is cancelled to idle at once and does nothing more.
  - A charge cut this way slides 68 px in the idle pose to a stop (`p1`). Cosmetic.
  - A pounce cut mid-air drops to the floor in idle (`l1`).
- Spawns are cleared at lucidity: every phase change logged `spawns_alive=0`.

## Ratings (inferred from logs and frames, not felt)

- **Fun 3/5.** This is the busiest and most varied stage 1 of the four guardians: six moves, 0.53-0.59 s gaps, and spawns
  that make Ivo move. Against it: the claw string's new moves never appear to a dodger, the roller gets a three-move
  stage-0 loop and no return, and the spit-then-move overlap caused 6 of the 7 hits in the dodging fight runs.
- **Fluidity 3/5.** There are no stalls, and wind-ups chain into strikes and spawns cleanly. The placeholder wind-ups
  look like the idle, so moves appear to start without a lead-in, and the walk slides. Both are GRD-18 art.
- **Difficulty 3/5** for the scripted dodger, probably 4 for a human. Both kits restored with 0-1 hits once the driver
  answered the ember-gap compounds. Without that, both died to them. A still Ivo dies in 7.4 s.
- **Readability 2/5.** The timings are generous (0.6-1.7 s to the first hit), and the spawn warnings read on dark stone.
  But four of the seven wind-ups (claw, charge, spit, roar) do not read as poses, and the pounce shadow over ember patches
  mixes two warnings into one row of spikes.

## What screenshots cannot show

Input latency, the weight of the charge and the pounce landing, how the rear's sustained pose feels for 2.85 s, and
audio. Every "dodgeable" above comes from a scripted policy with perfect reaction and exact timing. A human playtest
should confirm three things: whether the 0.3-0.4 s jump-out of a pounce after a spit can be found without being told,
whether the roar can be told from the claw and the charge in its wind-up, and how the roller's stage-0 loop feels.
