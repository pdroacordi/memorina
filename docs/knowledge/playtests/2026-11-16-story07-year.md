# STORY-07: the village's year

## What ran

`story07_year.json`: Ivo idle in the square with the world restored, the year at 20 s a season and a 6 s turn (the shipped pace is 240 s and 12 s). The runner's `log` prints `season=` and `year=<season>><next> turn= advancing=`.

| t | logged | seen |
|---|---|---|
| 0.3 | SPRING>SUMMER turn 0.00, advancing | green valley, grass lip, petals |
| 16.4 | SPRING>SUMMER turn 0.35 | almost no change: summer is spring's art with summer's tint, so the turn is the tint lerp |
| 21.4 | SUMMER>AUTUMN 0.00 | summer commit |
| 41.4 | AUTUMN>WINTER 0.00 | golden hills, leaf lip, falling leaves |
| 56.0 | AUTUMN>WINTER 0.26 | the winter band dithered in over the backdrop hills and the turf lip |
| 58.0 / 65.0 | 0.37, advancing=false (village_house) | the year holds indoors, mid-turn |
| 67.0 | 0.74, advancing | back out, the turn resumes from where it held |
| 72.9 | WINTER>SPRING 0.00 | snowy hills, white lip, flakes |
| 92.9 | SPRING>SUMMER 0.00 | back in spring after four commits |

## Findings

- Every commit lands on the season the log names, four commits return to spring, and the interior holds the turn exactly (0.37 at 58 s and at 65 s).
- The turn reads as a season moving in, not a cut: at turn 0.26 the dither is sparse and even over the backdrop and the turf lip, and the facades, the villagers and Ivo do not change (no seasonal material). At 100 % zoom the dither is on game pixels; the playtest cannot judge whether the cells crawl while the camera moves (Ivo stood still), which the world-anchored Bayer cell is there to prevent.
- Spring to summer shows little: summer has no art of its own (`systems/seasonal-art`).
- Weather follows the commit: leaves until winter's commit, then flakes.
- The death-mark hold is covered by `region_year_test` (`forgetting_in_view`), not by this run.

## What this cannot judge

The pace at 240 s a season, which the plan says is tuned in play; whether a 12 s turn is noticed while the player runs through the village.
