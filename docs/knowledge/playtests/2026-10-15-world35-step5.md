# WORLD-35 step 5: the sun stair, the street grate and the chimney base bench

Windowed debug runs of `tools/playtest/scripts/world35s5_*.json` (six timelines) with a per-frame trace. Positions are
Ivo's feet in world px. The game frame is 640x360. The runner never writes a player's save (`BootPolicy`). The layout is
the second one: a stone spiral, the shrine on the floor, and the grate at cols 29-32 (orchestrator calls, 2026-10-15).

## What was checked

| Timeline | Kit | Result |
|---|---|---|
| `a_base_climb_to_the_grate` | the lake kit (roll, Congelar, Redoma; no double jump, no wall climb) | From the arena (3700, 1824) up the spiral: wall step, wide shelf, wall step, wide shelf. 15 single-jump hops, each on the first try, reach the plank (4294, 384) under the closed grate. A jump there stops at the grate. Back down, Ivo walks off each shelf toward the next: 15 drops of 3 rows at 408 px/s, no hard landing, to the arena doorway (3825, 1824). |
| `b_shrine_by_sombra` | Sombra | Sombra played on the floor's plate (4144, 1824): the gate rises, and Ivo walks into the alcove past the slot (4437, 1824). |
| `c_open_grate_hole_and_back` | summer restored | Walking east along the road into the open hole drops him 3 rows onto the plank (4159, 384). One running jump west puts him back on the road (4024, 288). |
| `d_open_grate_climb_into_the_street` | summer restored | From the top shelf up through the plank, then one jump up through the hole onto the road (4060, 288). |
| `f_vault_west_edge` | base | Walking west on the plank stops at the vault's west wall (4139, 384), facing west. |
| `e_chimney_base_bench` | double jump, wall climb | Two steps east from the chimney's foot and down: `sit=true` on `caldera_chimney_base_bench` (-10032, 1824). |

## Findings

1. **The first layout's zigzag needed an overhang fix.** Shelves on alternating walls put the shelf on the same wall 6
   rows up over the take-off edge, and a jump 30 px back from the edge hit it. Plank lips cured it, but they were village
   oak on basalt. The spiral replaces both. A wall step (3 cells) and a wide shelf (12 cells) alternate on each side, so
   the shelf above a take-off column is 9 rows up or more. Every hop here landed on the first try.
2. **Falls are short.** Every drop in the spiral is 3 or 6 rows, so there is no straight gap and no need for a soft landing.
3. **No frame from the vault reaches the ring cut west of the stair.** In all six runs, no frame that spans the road line
   (y 384) has its left edge west of x 3723. The ring line is at x 3680. The vault's west wall holds Ivo at x 4139, so even
   facing west the look-ahead stops 43 px short (f01). In the first layout a 20 px strip showed it.
4. **Look.**
   - The spiral reads as basalt shelves on the cave wall (a02).
   - The grate is iron bars between two village stone blocks, transparent over the stone-lined vault (c00, f01).
   - The shrine's carved gate stands by the arena doorway, so the first thing a player sees on entering is the puzzle (b03).

## What this cannot judge

- Whether 15 alike hops over four screens feel long.
- Whether players read the closed grate as a door home before the summer guardian exists (GRD-04).
- Not driven: a Redoma shell or the double jump in the stair. They only shorten a climb that is already two-way.
