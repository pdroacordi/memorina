## What was tested

Six timelines in `tools/playtest/scripts/`, run windowed with `"trace": true`. The runner's `log` and `trace` lines now also print every enemy and guardian within 600 px: its offset from Ivo, its hp or fight phase and hit progress, and whether it is winding up, swinging or knocked back. Each verdict below rests on those traces, not only on the frames.

- `core09_brute_dodge_and_punish`: Ivo stands 132 px from the brute. He steps back when it winds up, walks in, does a standing combo, then walks in again and does a second combo.
- `core09_brute_running_opener`: Ivo runs at the brute from 190 px, then a running slash and the backhand.
- `core09_bloom_exchange` (skills double jump and roll, so no recall): walk in, roll under the pounce, step in and combo, step out, roll under the second pounce.
- `core09_frost_exchange` (same skills): walk in until the swipe telegraphs, step back, punish with a combo, a third swing, step out of the counter.
- `core09_brute_jump_in_place` and `core09_frost_close`, added after the CORE-09 review: Ivo jumps in place where the brute parks, and stands 47 px from the golem when its swipe comes.

Before these, passive runs (Ivo standing still) gave the baselines. The brute swings from 89 px and its spikes hit him there. The Frost swipe now hits a grounded Ivo at 74 and 103 px, and the charge at 44. Ivo died to every guardian when he did not dodge.

## Findings

- **Brute: the standing combo lands both swings.** The slash hit at 47.5 px (hp 5 -> 4) and the chop at 55.3 px (4 -> 2), then the finisher's 300 px/s shove sent the brute to 103 px for its 1 s stun (`a3_slash`, `a4_chop`). The second combo interrupted its next wind-up at 38.7 px (hp 2 -> 1 -> 0, `a6_second_combo`). Ivo took no damage.
- **Brute: the swing is avoidable.** It winds up at 89.3 px and its spikes come out 0.5 s later. Stepping back for 0.22 s, 0.14 s into the wind-up, put Ivo at 126 px, and the spikes ended short of him (`a1_windup_step_back`, `a2_spikes_miss`). The wind-up is a held crouch on a black body, so it reads by the brute stopping, not by a flash: `Character.flash` multiplies a black sprite and shows nothing.
- **Brute: a running slash opens.** The brute began its wind-up at 90 px while Ivo ran in. The running slash hit at 53 px and cancelled the wind-up (hp 5 -> 4), and the backhand hit at 38 px (4 -> 2). Ivo stopped 37 px from it, clear of its 26 px contact box (`b1_running_slash`, `b2_backhand`).
- **Bloom: the pounce can be rolled under, and the guardian holds its distance just outside Ivo's reach.** The first fix run found the pounce turning in mid-air to follow the roll (`bugs/a-guardian-lunge-turned-to-follow-a-player-who-rolled-under-it`). With the fix it lands 155 px past him, and the second pounce is rolled under too (`c2_roll_under_pounce`, `c6_second_pounce_rolled`). During its cooldown it paces at 100-128 px, against Ivo's reach of 84-98. A step in to about 85 px whiffed as it paced back; a deeper step landed one slash at 69 px. Ivo ended at full hp.
- **Frost: dodge, then punish.** The swipe telegraphs at 103 px. Stepping back to 129 px leaves its box short of him: it reaches 120.5 px, 10.5 past its 110 range (`d2_swipe_misses`). A step in after the swipe landed the slash at 61-64.5 px; the chop landed at 72.5 px in one run and whiffed in the next, as the golem gave ground at 35 px/s (`d3_punish_slash`, `d4_punish_chop`). A third swing at 74-78 px landed, and in the first run it provoked the counter, which stepping out to 140 px dodged. Hits 2-3 of 8, Ivo at full hp. From the edge of reach (70 px) the chop whiffs as the golem backs off; the combo needs a deeper step.
- **Attacking inside a Frost telegraph does not leave time to escape the swipe.** A standing slash locks Ivo for 0.42 s, so from 63 px he reached only 107 px by the time the swipe's box opened (0.9 s after the telegraph began), and it hit him. Inside a Frost telegraph the choice is dodge or roll, not one more hit. That seems right for a boss whose swipe reaches 10.5 px past where it is thrown, but it is a feel call for the user.
- **Frost: no safe pocket by the golem's legs** (review, `bugs/the-frost-swipe-passes-over-ivo-standing-close-to-the-golem`). The first retune left the swipe touching Ivo only at 63.5-120.5 px. Now it sweeps from the body front out to the fist, and Ivo standing at 47.3 px took it 0.5 s into the swing (`f2_close_swipe`).
- **Brute: a jump from where it parks does not draw it under Ivo** (review, `bugs/the-brute-walks-under-ivo-when-he-jumps-from-where-it-parks`). It parked at 47.5 px and stayed there for his whole jump in the 96 px tunnel, where his head meets the ceiling at about 41 px. He landed clear of its contact box. His slash then cancelled the swing it had started as its cooldown ended, and the chop followed (hp 5 -> 4 -> 2, `e1_parked`, `e2_jump_apex`, `e3_landed_slash`). After the brute fixes, the other brute runs repeat their earlier results to within a few px.

## Ratings rationale

- **Fun 3.** Each fight now has a readable loop: the brute pokes from far and parks in reach, Bloom pounces and paces, Frost telegraphs and punishes standing still. Each loop lands hits at the short sword's reach. The timelines are scripted and cannot react, so they hit the gaps the timing allowed; whether the step-in feels tense or fiddly needs a human. Bloom's pacing in and out of the 84-98 px band will decide it.
- **Fluidity 3.** No stuck states or flips. The brute's longer wind-up and the committed lunges read cleanly in the traces. Latency and the feel of the step-in cannot be judged from frames.
- **Aesthetics 4.** Unchanged art. The Frost swipe now hits where its punch passes just over Ivo's head; a human should check that this reads as fair.
