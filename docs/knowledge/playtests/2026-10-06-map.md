## What was tested

- **Setup.** Godot 4.7.2, windowed. Every run had `APPDATA` redirected to a fresh scratch folder,
  and the real `save_debug.tres` md5 was `990dc788…` before and after all 19 runs.
- **Timelines** in `tools/playtest/scripts/map_*.json`.
  - From the implementer: `explore_pan_zoom`, `refused_mid_air`, `closed_by_hit`.
  - Added this session: `pad_toggle_zoom_pan`, `blocks_ivo`, `memorina_drawn`, `esc_and_pause`,
    `three_rooms` (double jump and wall climb unlocked to clear the Woods pillar at x ~800),
    `bench_rewind`, `open_at_death`, `refused_climbing`, `refused_climb_foot`,
    `opened_while_walking`.
- **Runner change.** `log` now also prints hp, attack/climb/drawn, `PlayerInput.blocked`, the
  open screen, the map centre and zoom, and the seen-cell count per room key.
- **Sources of evidence.** Every claim about state comes from those log lines. Frames are for
  the look.
- **Units.** At 1280x720, 1 pack px = 4 screenshot px, and a 64 px cell at the opening zoom is
  16 screenshot px.

## Findings

The user's decisions, pass or fail:

1. **M / pad LB toggle: pass.** M opens and closes it. LB (button 9) opens, closes and reopens it,
   with `screen=MAP` / `NONE` and `blocked` following.
2. **Esc / B close: pass.** Esc closes the map without opening the pause (`paused=false`). B
   closes it with `roll=false`, and roll is unlocked: an unpaused B in the same run rolls at
   345.9 px/s.
3. **Opens only on the ground: pass.**
   - Refused mid-air (`jump=true`, `screen=NONE`), then opens once he lands
     (`open_on_ground_two_rooms.png`, first pair).
   - Refused while climbing the root web and while hanging still on it (`climb=true`,
     `screen=NONE`).
   - Climbing ends the moment his feet touch the floor (`climb=false`, `floor=true` at the
     foot), so on this web "climbing on the ground" cannot occur.
   - `stands_on_ground()` does not test `is_climbing()`; it relies on the two being exclusive.
     Wall climb (`WallMobility`) was not probed.
   - It opens while sitting on a bench, which the decisions do not forbid.
4. **Any hit closes it: pass** (`hit_closes_map.png`). Three separate brute hits each logged
   `screen=NONE, blocked=false` at the hurt frame.
5. **Death closes it: pass.** In `open_at_death` the map was reopened at hp 1 and was open at
   t 7.10. The fatal blow at ~7.15 left `screen=NONE, blocked=false, state=death` at 7.20.
6. **World keeps running: pass.** `paused=false, scale=1.00` throughout. The brute walked up and
   hit Ivo 0.3 s after the map opened. A roll started just before the open finished under the
   map (80 px). Cells kept being revealed while it was open (120 → 132) and appear the next
   time it opens, as the plan says.
7. **Ivo cannot act: pass.**
   - Keyboard: Right held (`vel 0`), Z and X (zoom only), Shift, C, and Up with the Memorina
     drawn (pans; no note on the sheet).
   - Pad: A and X zoom only.
   - After the close everything acts again: Z/A jump, X attacks (`attack_idle_1`), Shift/B
     roll, C draws, Up plays a note.
   - A direction still held at the close resumes walking at once (192 px/s).
8. **Opens centred on Ivo, no marker: pass only with no direction held.**
   - Every open with the stick and arrows at rest logged a centre equal to Ivo's position
     (2563, 1383, 1500, -300, -1104, 7984).
   - The zoom resets to 4 px on every open.
   - No marker is drawn. Ivo's own sprite stays visible under the dim, at his camera position,
     which is not where the map puts him.
   - **Fail:** a walk direction held at the open pans the map off Ivo within 0.02 s and to
     the clamp by 0.5 s (`open_while_walking_pans_away.png`). See
     `bugs/a-direction-held-when-the-map-opens-pans-it-off-ivo`.
9. **Pan: pass.** Left stick (axis 0) and arrows pan. The clamp holds at the seen area's edges:
   the left edge (785 in Woods, -84 in Downtown), the room top (-538), and 102 at the bottom.
10. **Zoom Z/X and pad A/X: pass.** The steps are 1/2/4/8 px per cell, opening at 4.
11. **Reveal: pass.** One still view marks 60 cells (10x6, the centre rule). A jump that lifts
    the camera adds rows (80), and the double-jump arcs left notches in the top edge. Each room
    is its own outlined box (`zoom_steps_three_rooms.png`). The seen area follows the camera,
    not Ivo.
12. **Rewind on death: pass.** With no bench, a death forgot all of Downtown; only the respawn
    room (Bloom Hollow, 60) remained.
13. **Survives a bench rest: pass** (`bench_rest_survives_death.png`).
    - Ivo arrived east of the bench, walked to it (96 cells), rested, walked on (120) and died.
    - After the rebuild the map holds 96 cells, the identical 16x6 box. That is more than the
      60 the bench's own view re-reveals, so the rest committed the pre-rest cells and the
      death forgot the 24 seen after it.

Probes:

- **Esc and pause.**
  - Esc with the map open closes only the map. A second Esc opens the pause (focus Resume).
  - M under the pause is ignored, and Esc resumes.
  - Start (button 6) with the map open closes the map without pausing.
- **Memorina drawn** (`map_over_memorina_sheet.png`).
  - The map opens over the drawn sheet, and notes are blocked.
  - The sheet stays on screen under the 65 % dim. The seen box is drawn on top of it, and the
    faint cream fill over the parchment is hard to read.
  - Cosmetic. Hiding the sheet, or opening the map farther from it, would avoid it.
- **Three rooms.**
  - Downtown, Woods and Bloom Hollow (80/294/80 cells) read as three boxes. The gaps are the
    real 32-36 world px between rooms: about 2 pack px at 4 px per cell, still visible at
    1 px per cell.
  - At the opening zoom the three rooms (90 cells) do not fit on the screen (320 pack px is
    80 cells). At 2 px they do.
- **Readability by zoom** (`zoom_steps_three_rooms.png`).
  - 8 px: large boxes, with the forest showing through the 0.27 fill.
  - 4 px: a single screen's view is a 40x24 pack px box.
  - 2 px: the whole explored world in a strip.
  - 1 px: shapes only. Most of a two-cell-tall run is ink, so it reads as outlines.
  - All four are legible against the dimmed forest and the grey Downtown.
- **Pan clamp** (`pan_clamps_at_screen_centre.png`). The clamp stops the centre at the seen
  area's edge, so at any edge half the screen is empty dimmed world. Whether the edge should
  stop at the screen edge instead is a design call; the decision only says "clamped".
- **Partial last row.** A 602 px tall room has 10 rows. Its 10th row is drawn whole, so every
  seen area reaches 38 px below the room (down clamp 102 against the room bottom at 64).
  Harmless while rooms sit side by side. Rooms stacked vertically would overlap by that much.
- **HUD under the map.** The life notes are dimmed with the world: their brightest pixel drops
  from 245 to 86, the 0.65 dim. With enemies free to hit him, the threat and his life both sit
  at 35 % brightness. A hit closes the map anyway.
- **Debug-boot leak** (`bugs/a-debug-boot-reveals-map-cells-ivo-never-saw`). It does not
  occur in the runner: the fresh save has no bench, and the first log already shows only the
  teleport room.
- **Outside the map.** On the bench route, Ivo stopping at x -441, pressed
  against the `BruteShadow`, was never attacked: 20 s with the map open, and 8 s without it in
  a control run. Stopping at -518 he is hit at once. Not caused by the map: the brute never
  turned to a target already in reach behind it, fixed in
  `bugs/the-brute-swings-away-from-a-target-already-in-reach`.

Pan speed, measured:
- At 8 px per cell the centre moved 299-341 world px per 0.25 s of log time, which is
  ~1300 world px/s ≈ 162 pack px/s. That matches the 160 pack px/s spec, with frame-time
  jitter.
- The speed is full at once, with no ramp. A 0.1 s arrow tap moves 16 pack px (4 cells at the
  opening zoom). A 0.5 s stick tilt crossed all of Woods' seen area to the clamp.
- The pan is the same in pack px at every zoom, so at 1 px per cell it is 160 cells/s.

What frames cannot show:
- How the pan feels.
- Whether rounding the canvas to whole pack px judders. Inference: at 60 fps, 160 px/s is
  2.67 px a frame, so the steps alternate 2 and 3 px. That may read as uneven, and it needs a
  human eye.
- Any latency between M and the open (the log saw it within 0.02 s).
- Audio.

## Ratings rationale

- **Fun 0**: a map screen is not play (the convention of the pause and title sessions).
- **Fluidity 3**: every toggle, close and zoom landed on the first press. No zoom leaked a jump
  or an attack, and hit and death close it cleanly. It is held to 3 because the most natural
  way to open a map, while walking with the stick or arrows held, throws the view off Ivo
  before the player has read it. Fixing the bug would make this a 4; a human pass on the pan
  judder would decide 4 or 5.
- **Aesthetics 3**, low confidence and not compared side by side with the approved mockup:
  - The "C ink overlay" reads: cream ink and a faint fill over a 65 % dim, crisp on the 2x grid.
  - Against it: the seen box is small at the opening zoom, the forest shows busily through the
    fill at 8 px, the drawn Memorina sheet clashes with the map, and half the screen is empty at
    every pan clamp.
