# WORLD-35 step 4: the crag, the ridge, the summit and the mill town bench

Windowed debug runs of `tools/playtest/scripts/world35s4_*.json` (nine timelines) with a per-frame trace. Positions
are Ivo's feet in world px. The runner never writes a player's save (`BootPolicy`), so the bench saved in memory only.
The layout is the final one: the arena's lowered east lintel, the stone spire, and the leaf piles under both drops
(orchestrator calls, 2026-10-15).

## Findings

1. **The climb is now found by a natural move.** The east doorway's lintel reaches row 5, so the climbable face starts
   96 px over the floor. A single jump while walking into the doorway catches it. Before the change, walking east carried
   Ivo through the doorway, and only a deliberate jump at one spot found the face.
2. **Both drops back are soft; the ridge's is one-way, the summit's not for a Redoma kit.**
   - The leaf piles (`leaf_pile.tscn` over a SoftLanding zone) turn landings at 929 and 980 px/s into plain landings.
   - The ridge's drop is one-way for every kit without wall climb. A double jump inside its shaft peaks 120 px under
     its 512 px lips, and the ridge covers the leaf path except at the shaft.
   - The summit's west lip is 480 px over the terraces' row-10 terrace and 448 px over their west hill. That is under
     a Redoma shell plus a double jump (462), so a Redoma kit may climb in.
   - Accepted (orchestrator call, 2026-10-15): no trap, and the woods kit already walks in from orchard_chasm_bridge.
3. **Logico 1 works in the world room.** The earlier report of a stall 86 px up was a misread still: at t 7.6 the lift
   had left the frame through its top. The logged state shows the full 448 px rise in 3.5 s
   (`bugs/the-orchard-counterweights-lift-stops-partway-up`, not a bug). The rider must board as it starts, from the east
   floor, because playing from the lift itself drops both loads and jams it, as designed.
4. **Jump ceilings.** The crest ends at y -1632 over an earth `[back]` wall, and the overhang ends at y -1632 to -1568, so
   the leaf path's and arena's base jumps are free (a full double jump's head, y -1572, touches the overhang's
   row-22 cells: harmless). From the leaf path the `[back]` reads as a dark overhang once the
   camera rises (g01). The first drafts capped these jumps at the frame's edge.
5. **Look.**
   - The spire is the orchards' dry-stone (`S`) now (b02). It reads as built stone more than as a limestone needle;
     the region has no natural-rock look.
   - The leaf piles grey with the world.
   - The summit's crest is bare until the great windmill is drawn (WORLD-37).

## What this cannot judge

- Whether players think to climb the lowered lintel without a sign. Geometry makes it easy, but not obvious.
- Whether boarding the rising lift from the east floor is clear to a player; the jump has about 0.5 s of slack.
- Not driven: a Redoma shell under either drop shaft. By the numbers, a shell plus a double jump (462 px) stays
  under the ridge's lips (512 px) and reaches the summit's (448-480 px).
