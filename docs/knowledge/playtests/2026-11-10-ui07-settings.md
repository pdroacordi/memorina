## What was tested

Godot 4.7.2 console build, windowed, debug, 1280x720. Each run used its own `APPDATA` under the session
scratchpad, and its first probe line printed that folder as `OS.get_user_data_dir()`.

The runs went through `tools/playtest/scripts/ui07_settings_probe_driver.gd`, a `-s` driver that loads the
runner (or Boot with `--title`). It prints `[probe]` on every change of:
- pause, time scale, `WorldFreeze.is_held()`;
- GUI focus (parent/name/text), Settings visible, page, status text, capturing, `MenuInput.capturing`;
- which preview players are playing (bus, volume_db), each bus's step and live dB;
- language and locale, `Settings.has_disk()`;
- the InputMap events of jump, map_zoom_in, map, attack and ui_accept;
- Ivo's position and vertical velocity.

The timelines are `ui07_settings_keyboard.json` (51 s), `ui07_settings_pad.json`,
`ui07_settings_roll_window.json` and `ui07_settings_title_keys.json`, all in `tools/playtest/scripts/`.
Input was raw `InputEventKey` (keycode and physical keycode) and `InputEventJoypadButton`
(Start 6, A 0, B 1, D-pad 11-14).

**Settings file.** The real user dir (`%APPDATA%/Godot/app_userdata/Memorina/`) held no `settings*.cfg`
before or after any run.
- The three runner runs: no `.cfg` in their own scratch dir either, and `disk=false` from the first frame.
  `Settings.use_memory_only()` held through a language change, two rebinds and a reset.
- The title run (a NONE session, so a disk session) wrote one `settings_debug.cfg` into its scratch dir on
  close, with `language="en"`. The commit-on-close works there.
- No run logged an engine error beyond the usual exit leaks. The one warning is the known `lake_chasm.room`
  UID warning.

## Findings

1. **Pause menu entry: pass** (`a02_settings_entry.png`). Resume, Settings, Quit to title, Quit game. Down
   from Resume focuses Settings; Enter opens it with the Sound tab focused. Opening it held the world
   (`paused=true ts=0.00 held=true`); Settings added nothing.
2. **The four tabs, English: pass on fit** (`a03_sound_tab.png`, `a13_language_focused.png`,
   `a16_keyboard_tab.png`, `a17_pad_tab.png`). Every label and value fits its row and the frame. The
   focused value row shows `< value >` in the focus ink. Pad names read A, X, B, RB, Y, A, X, B, LB, View
   (Xbox family, no pad connected).
3. **The four tabs, Portuguese: pass on fit** (`b01_general_pt.png`, `b03_master_pt.png`,
   `b04_keyboard_pt.png`, `b05_pad_pt.png`).
   - The language switched live on Accept: every tab, row, the reset question
     (`b07_reset_pad_ask_pt.png`) and the pause menu behind it (`b09_pause_pt.png`, "Opções") were
     retranslated with no reopen.
   - "Português (Brasil)" with `< >` and "Restaurar o padrão" are the longest texts, and both fit.
   - The patched tilde draws on ã and õ.
4. **Volume: pass.**
   - Left and Right on Master stepped 10 -> 9 -> 8 -> 9. The bar's last cells changed to the light
     "off" colour (`a06_master_8.png`), and the bus followed (-1.8 dB, -3.9 dB).
   - Every step started that row's preview: MasterPreview, MusicPreview, NotesPreview and SfxPreview, each
     on its own bus, all while paused.
   - The Music preview played at 0 dB for 1.2 s, then faded to -40 dB over 0.3 s and stopped (1.5 s in total).
   - Right at 10 clamps: Effects stayed at 10.
   - The pad D-pad steps it the same way (pad run, 10 -> 9 -> 8).
   - Whether the samples are *heard*, and how loud, was not judged (see below).
5. **Rebinding Jump to Space: pass.**
   - Accept on Jump showed "Press a key" (`a19_capture_prompt.png`).
   - Down arrow was refused with "This one is reserved", and the capture stayed open (`a20_reserved_status.png`).
   - Space was assigned: `jump=[Space,J0]`, and `map_zoom_in` followed it (`zin=[Space,J0]`).
   - After Esc (Settings closed onto its entry) and Resume, Space jumped Ivo: vy -489, apex 123 px, and the
     early release cut the jump (`a29_space_jump.png`). A 0.3 s Z press then did nothing (no motion logged),
     so the old key was really unbound.
6. **Refused bind onto Z: refused, but the message shows a raw key (bug, known).** With Jump on Space, Z
   belongs only to `ui_accept`. Map -> Z and Map -> Enter both read **"Used by ACTION_UI_ACCEPT"**
   (`a24_refused_z.png`). The reviewer had filed this already
   (`bugs/a-binding-refused-by-ui-accept-shows-the-raw-key-action-ui-accept`); this run confirms it in game.
   With the defaults, Map -> Z reads "Já usada em Pular" correctly (`b08_refused_pt.png`).
7. **The status line shares the Reset row's band (bug, new, low).** The rows measure 12 pack px, not the
   documented 11. Eleven rows end at 163, and the status line is at 154..164, so every status message
   prints beside "Reset to defaults", 2 pack px lower, like a value of that row (`a20_reserved_status.png`,
   `b08_refused_pt.png`). They do not collide horizontally in either language yet.
   See `bugs/settings-rows-measure-12-px-so-reset-to-defaults-sits-on-the-status-line`.
8. **A refusal's message outlives the capture (note).** After Esc cancels a refused capture, the message
   stays on the status line until Settings is reopened (`a26_capture_cancelled.png`), with nothing being
   captured.
9. **Reset to defaults asks first: pass** (`a33_reset_ask.png`). Accept on the Reset row hid the sheet and
   asked "Reset the keyboard to its defaults?" with No focused. Esc closed the question back onto the Reset
   row. Left moved to Yes, and Yes put Jump back on Z (`jump=[Z,J0]`, zoom too), with focus on the Reset row.
10. **Back-out: pass, keyboard and pad.**
    - Esc during a capture cancels only the capture (keyboard and pad pages).
    - Esc on a page closes Settings onto the pause menu's Settings entry, and Esc again resumes.
    - Pad: B during a pad capture is a candidate, not a cancel. Jump -> B was refused "Used by Roll", and
      Start cancelled.
    - B closed Settings onto its entry, and B again resumed with `paused=false ts=1.00 held=false`.
    - That B did not roll. An unpaused B 1.4 s later did roll (x 200 -> 326), so the check is not vacuous.
    - The hold was released on every Resume in all four runs.
11. **Window size: pass.** Left on Window size resized the window to 640x360 at once, with the row showing
    "< 1x (640 x 360) >" (`window_1x.png`), and Right restored 1280x720. Fullscreen was not toggled: it
    would take over the tester's screen.
12. **Title: pass** (`title_column_pt.png`, `title_column_en_after_switch.png`,
    `title_settings_general_pt.png`).
    - The fresh title booted in pt_BR, following the OS locale: Novo jogo, Continuar (disabled), Opções,
      Sair do jogo.
    - Down from Novo jogo skips the disabled Continue to Opções.
    - Settings opens on the Sound tab. Switching to English rewrote the open page.
    - Esc returned to the title, already in English, with Settings focused.
    - A keyboard capture there worked, and Esc cancelled it without leaving Settings.
13. **HUD notes show around the panel's corner (cosmetic).** The panel nearly fills the screen. The HUD's
    three life notes (top-left) stay drawn: the first beside the frame, the stems of the other two above it
    (`hud_notes_peek_at_corner_x4.png`, 4x crop).
14. **No tab marks the current page once a row has focus (note).** All four tabs then look the same
    (`a06_master_8.png`). The page itself shows which tab it is, but a "current tab" state would help on the
    two lookalike binding pages.

## What screenshots and logs cannot show

- **Audio.** Whether each preview is audible over silence, how loud, and whether Effects' `gate_bolt.wav`
  reads as "effects" were not judged. The logs prove only that the right player played on the right bus at
  the right volume_db, and that the fade ran for 1.5 s.
- **Feel of navigation.** Key repeat on a held Left or Right (the timeline only tapped), stick-tilt focus
  (not exercised here), and whether 12 px rows feel cramped at 1x.
- **Fullscreen**, multi-monitor centring, and the 3x scale on a screen that fits it.
- **Persistence across a real restart** (disk session): only the title run's single file write was seen.

## Ratings rationale

- fun 0: a menu; not applicable.
- fluidity 4, inferred from the focus logs: every press moved focus once and as documented, and nothing got
  stuck or needed a second press. Not 5, because of the lingering refusal text and the missing current-tab
  mark, and because key repeat and the stick were not tested.
- aesthetics 4: the panel, tabs and rows are clean and legible in both languages. Docked for the raw
  `ACTION_UI_ACCEPT`, the status text sharing the Reset row, and the HUD notes around the corner.
