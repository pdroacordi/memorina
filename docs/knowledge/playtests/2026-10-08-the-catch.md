## Summary

Run by the orchestrator. `song_release_root_catch.json`: Soltar drawn at t 1.5 from under the
platform, Enraizar drawn at t 7.5; the roots seize the platform 106 px above the floor; Ivo jumps
onto it (t 15) and from it into the exit (y 5840 at t 18). `song_release_root_catch_control.json`
(Soltar only): the platform lowers swinging to the floor and the same jumps never leave it.

## What was seen

- The platform swings on its rope while it lowers and only becomes footing when the roots hold it.
- The two roots meet the plank one texel apart in height (`bugs/root-bridge-strands-meet-at-a-step`).
- Found while running it: an AnimatableBody2D writes its position a physics step late
  (sync_to_physics is on by default), so the platform first hung at the floor instead of 300 px up.

## What this cannot judge

Whether a player reads the swinging plank as "no footing yet", and how wide the timing window
(about 3 s of lowering) feels by hand. Enraizar first was checked by the map test, not run.
