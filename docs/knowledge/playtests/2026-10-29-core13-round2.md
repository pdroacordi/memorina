# CORE-13 round 2: crab, hornet, icicle bat, spider, and the new scorpions

**Ratings (low confidence, from frames and logged timing; a human must confirm):**
fun 3/5, fluidity 3/5, aesthetics 3/5. The bat would pull fun down until its homing bug is fixed.

## Summary

- **Crab:** works to the contract. A front slash flashes the crab white with no damage and no
  recoil on Ivo, and the snap still comes. A pogo lands. Its back cannot be reached in play
  (below). At Frost Edge's memory it is hard to see against the wreck.
- **Hornet:** works to the contract. It gathers, hovers ~130 px beside Ivo, tells 0.5 s and dashes on
  a held aim. Its recovery can be punished by a pogo, only just.
- **Spider:** works to the contract. It is drawn asleep with a fraying thread, lowers to Ivo's chest,
  tells, bites (1 life), can be pogoed, and climbs back 1.5 s after Ivo leaves its column. The
  thread stays tied to the branch.
- **Icicle bat:** hangs flush and drops, and the swoop reads as one motion through Ivo's chest.
  **It never flies home** (6 of 6 runs). Filed:
  [`bugs/the-icicle-bat-never-flies-home-after-a-swoop`](../bugs/the-icicle-bat-never-flies-home-after-a-swoop.md).
- **Scorpions:** the cistern scorpion works. The sun stair's plate and gate puzzle still opens
  with the two shelf scorpions in place. The old WORLD-35 base climb timeline now dies to them,
  but the climb itself can still be done.
- **Across all creatures:** every attack and every touch cost exactly 1 life. Every creature stayed
  grey right beside Ivo (the round-1 shield bug did not show).

## Build and method

- **Build:** the working tree, uncommitted, on top of `c3a3270`, with `--import` at 12:55:34.
  - At 13:14:02 a sibling changed `ceiling_ai.gd` and `flyer_ai.gd` (the fix for
    `bugs/a-flyer-dashes-tail-first-when-its-target-crosses-during-the-telegraph`). The bat and
    hornet runs before that were `bat1-5` and `h1-h2`.
  - The decisive bat runs (`bat1n`, `bat2n`) and the four final timelines were re-run after it.
    Every result was the same.
- **Harness:** `tools/playtest/playtest_runner.tscn` with a fresh `APPDATA` per run, checked by a
  headless `OS.get_user_data_dir()` probe before each run.
  - Facts come from the runner's per-frame `trace` foes field. Each creature's world position is
    Ivo's position plus `dx`/`dy`.
  - Frames were read as world-anchored crops, placed from each frame's logged `cam_centre`.
- **Draft timelines:** two started Ivo inside solid ground. The crab at (8506, 288) is in the rock
  spur, cols 3-7. The hornet at (-3812, -2016) is in the hill, row 8, cols 19-26. The other two
  started inside the creatures' reach, so the asleep or dormant state could not be seen. All four
  were rewritten (below).
- Two runs segfaulted on exit (exit 139) after writing every frame and log line. This is harmless,
  but it is noted for the harness.

## Findings

### Crab (`frost_shore_crab_1`, (8656, 288); Ivo walks in from (8912, 280))

- **Gathers out of the grey** when Ivo comes within ~206 px (t 1.51). It then sidesteps toward him
  at ~65 px/s, always facing him. When Ivo is past its leash it parks at the leash end
  (x 8849.7 = home + 192) and waits, idle, 50 px from him.
- **Shell:** a slash at dx 38-45 (t 4.0) flashes the crab white in frames 4.20-4.25. Its hp stayed
  3, and Ivo's x stayed exactly 8839.67, so there was no recoil. The block did not cancel the
  crab's move: its telegraph started at 4.22 and the snap hit Ivo at 4.62-4.67 (hp 3 -> 2, one
  life). See `crab_shell_flash_tell_snap.png`.
- **Tell:** 0.40 s from telegraph to snap, in 4 runs out of 4. In frames 4.45-4.60 the claws are
  raised and the edge is agitated. It reads, but the crab is small and mid-grey.
- **Pogo** on the shell lands (crab hp 3 -> 2, Ivo bounces; `crab_pogo_on_the_shell.png`). Press
  timing matters:
  - Ivo's pogo hitbox is live from 0.167 s to 0.417 s into `air_downwards_attack`.
  - A press 0.12-0.2 s before contact (feet ~45-30 px over the crab's feet) is too late: contact
    hits Ivo first (hp -1, in 2 runs).
  - A press at feet ~100 px up landed.
  - This is Ivo's existing pogo timing, not a crab defect. On a 22 px tall body it is easy to
    miss.
- **The back cannot be reached in play (design finding, not a bug).** `always_face_target` turns
  the crab the moment Ivo crosses it. Its back is exposed only while a move holds its side:
  active plus recovery, 0.6 s. None of these reached it:
  - **Jump over during the tell:** the lowest hop is ~79 px and ~0.95 s in the air. Ivo lands at
    5.21, and the recovery ends at ~5.25. A ground slash pressed at 5.12-5.18 is dropped by Ivo's
    landing recovery (3 timings, no attack started). An air slash from behind at 5.00-5.06 whiffs,
    because Ivo is 30-56 px above the crab.
  - **Roll past** (`skills` [2]): the roll carries Ivo 165 px and ends 135 px behind the crab, too
    far to return within its recovery.
  - So a hit from behind lands mechanically (`HitGuard`), but on flat ground the pogo is the only
    real answer. If the design wants a back attack, the crab needs a longer recovery or a turn
    delay. That is for the designer to decide.
- **Readability (subjective):** in front of the wreck hull at memory 0.05, the crab's greys match
  the planks. At 1x it is a faint shape until it raises its claws
  (`crab_tell_against_the_wreck_1x.png`). The cistern scorpion (light grey on a dark vault) and the
  spider (dark on a light canopy) read far better.

### Icicle bat (`lake_ice_caves_bat_2`, anchor (4304, -1808); Ivo walks in from (4470, -1640))

- **Asleep:** it hangs flush. The feet sit on the block's bottom row, and the hang reads as an
  icicle-like shape (`bat_hangs_flush_then_drops.png`).
- **Drop:** it starts the moment Ivo enters the reach (x 4426, t 1.78) and holds still 0.30 s.
- **Swoop:** the swoop crosses Ivo's x at y -1660, which is exactly his chest (feet -1632, chest
  -28). Frames 2.0-3.5 read as one dive and climb. The hit cost 1 life.
- **It never returns to hang.** It pins mid-air and swoops again from there, in 6 of 6 runs on
  both builds:
  - Ivo at x 4369: it pins on the corner of its own ceiling block at (4364, -1824).
  - Ivo at x 4417, the spot a player coming from the arena door reaches first: the swoop's
    mirrored end (x ~4530) lies past the room's east edge (4480). The bat leaves through the
    open col 39 into `lake_frost_arena` and pins at (4524, -1766), out of frame, because the
    camera is clamped to the room. Then it swoops back in from off-screen.
  - Evidence and the likely cause (FLOATING `wall_min_slide_angle` 15 degrees against
    near-head-on homing at 14.9 and 10.8 degrees) are in the bug.
- **Hittable on the way back:** a single jump slash hit the pinned bat (hp 2 -> 1). The knockback
  lifts it ~20 px.
- **Hittable asleep:** this could not be tested from the floor. Its reach covers 128 px to either
  side, from 48 to 224 px below, and the anchor is 176 px over the floor, so Ivo is always inside
  the reach before he can strike. The drop starts first. A hit during the 0.3 s drop needs Ivo
  within sword reach of a body 176 px up, which a jump cannot reach in time.

### Spider (`woods_canopy_spider`, (-4080, -976) under the launch branch; Ivo from (-4200, -870))

- **Asleep:** drawn under the branch with a short thread. The thread shows gaps that change frame
  to frame (`spider_asleep_lowers_and_climbs_back.png`).
- **Lowers** once Ivo is within 64 px of its column. Descent is ~140 px/s, and it stops at y -893
  against Ivo's chest at -892. The thread stays tied to the branch's underside the whole way.
- **The bite has a narrow band.** The bite reaches x 0..30.
  - With Ivo 22-28 px from its column it tells (2.53) and bites (2.90, 0.37 s), costing 1 life
    (`spider_bob_tell_and_bite.png`).
  - Closer than ~21 px, it lowers onto his head, and contact costs the life instead (`sp2`, t 2.29).
  - At 31-64 px it hangs beside him, harmless, for as long as he stays (3+ s in `sp1` and `sp4`).
  - Players who stand still near it will learn it is safe. Whether it should drift toward him
    is a design question.
- **Tell:** the bob was not visible at 0.05 s sampling (its y held at -900). The tell reads
  through agitation: the body loses about half its pixels for 0.35 s. At this size it reads more
  like "crumbling" than "about to bite". Confirming that needs a human.
- **Pogo** off it works (hp 2 -> 1, Ivo bounces). It rises with Ivo's chest while he jumps (to y
  -912), so the pogo meets it higher than its rest point.
- **Climbs back:** Ivo left the column at ~5.6, and it started up at 7.18 (the 1.5 s linger). It
  climbed at ~80 px/s back to -975. The thread stayed right.

### Hornet (`orchard_chasm_bridge_hornet_2`, (-3504, -2160); Ivo from (-3300, -2020))

- **Gathers** at ~205 px (t 1.68-1.75). The pixels coalesce over ~0.5 s (`hornet_gathers.png`).
- **Hovers** 127-132 px beside Ivo. On its second approach it settled at exactly chest height
  (dy -28). On the first it began the tell while still sinking, at dy -47.
- **Tell:** 0.50-0.60 s. The curled stinger is barely legible at this size under the agitation
  fray (`hornet_tell_frays_then_dashes.png`). What reads is the fray, then a lunge.
- **The dash holds its aim:** it aims at the feet. When Ivo jumped as it started, the dash carried
  on low under him. One dash hit cost 1 life. It overshoots 60-100 px and drifts through the 0.6 s
  recovery.
- **Recover is punishable, barely:** jump the dash, drift after it and pogo. That landed at
  4.98, at the very end of the recovery (hornet hp 2 -> 1, Ivo unhurt). A ground punish after
  a jump dodge is out of reach: Ivo lands ~4.85 with the hornet ~96 px away.
- **The weak dash clip:** timing plus fray carry it. The dash reads as a streak in 2 of 3 sampled
  frames. This is inferred from 0.1 s frames. Motion blur and feel need a human.

### Scorpions

- **`caldera_cistern_bottom`** (464, 1824): gathers when Ivo steps off the dais, walks in, tells
  at dx 46 (4.81) and strikes (1 life). It reads well, light grey on the dark vault.
- **`caldera_sun_stair`**:
  - **The plate puzzle still works.** `world35s5_b_shrine_by_sombra.json`: Sombra's shadow holds
    the plate, the gate opens, and Ivo walks to the reward slot (x 4437). Scorpion 2 stays
    dormant 576 px above.
  - **The old climb timeline now dies.** `world35s5_a_base_climb_to_the_grate.json` walks Ivo
    blind into both scorpions:
    - scorpion 2 on the R shelf: contact during its tell (hp 3 -> 2 at 19.71), then contact in
      its recovery after the invulnerability (-> 1 at 20.57);
    - scorpion 1 on the L shelf: -> 0 at 26.26.
    - He respawns in `village_house` (the fresh save has no bench). The timeline is stale, not a
      bug, and needs re-scripting.
  - **The climb can still be done.** Standing on the R shelf at x 4090, scorpion 2 walks in and
    tells at 3.98. A jump right during the tell clears it unhurt (`sc2`): the head touches the
    L shelf's underside at y 1088 and still clears. A strike that lands knocks Ivo off the shelf
    onto the one 192 px below (`sc1`). That is a setback, not a trap.

### Across all creatures

- **One attack costs one life, and touching costs 1:** crab snap, bat swoop, spider bite, hornet
  dash and scorpion strike each took exactly 1 hp. Spider and scorpion contact each took 1.
  - The scorpion's two hits in 0.86 s were two touches: one in its tell (not the move's hit) and
    one in its recovery after Ivo's invulnerability. That matches the contract.
- **Grey inside Ivo's colour:** the crab at 30 px, the scorpion at 46 px, the spider at 22 px and
  the bat crossing his chest all stayed grey.
- **Fray:** reads at Frost Edge (memory 0.05, per the brief) as scattered edge pixels on the crab.
  It reads in the woods canopy (0.4, per the brief) as clear desertion along the spider's legs.
  The runner does not print the memory value, so these levels are the brief's, not measured.
