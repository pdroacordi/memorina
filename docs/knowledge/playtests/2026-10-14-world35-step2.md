# WORLD-35 step 2: the hollow tree and the woods crown

Windowed debug runs of `tools/playtest/scripts/world35s2_*.json` (seven timelines), with a per-frame trace. Positions are
Ivo's feet. Final layout: the well is 4 cells (cols 5-8) over a 2-cell top landing, and the tree shows the heartwood wall.
The top-hop fix (`bugs/the-climbs-over-the-top-hop-never-fires`) is in. The crown, bloom-mouth and arena-exit stills come
from runs before the well and backdrop changes, which do not touch those rooms.

## Findings

1. **The climb's top hop works now.** Before the fix, up held at a pole's top bobbed between feet 137 and 148 under the
   knot (top 96), because the sensor left the pole 16 px before the feet passed its top. The exit is now judged by the
   sensor's bottom, and the 54 px hop lands on each knot.
2. **The double jump needs both jumps held.** With 0.25-0.3 s holds a double jump rises 212-236 px; with both held it
   rises 270-284 px, as the guide says. The crown's 7-row entry (224 px plus the lip) clears by 49 px only with full
   holds. A first draft put a one-way knot in the hole, 5 rows up, but the audit found it led nowhere, so it was
   removed.
3. **The region cut sits on the well's top landing.** Climbing up, standing on the top landing (feet -1152, body in
   the mill town's rect) puts Ivo in the mill town: the frame cuts to the orchards' grey there. Dropping in, it cuts
   back on the fall below it. No void or second picture showed on either side.
4. **The well is a hop, not a hazard.** At 4 cells, with no rim posts, a base-kit running jump clears it with room to
   spare. A player who walks in lands on the top landing (3 rows) or the first knot (9 rows).
5. **The seams hold.** In the stills, the tunnel mouth shows the dark `[back]` column on the tree's side and the
   woods' set on bloom's side. The well shows its earth lining from the street. The frame never reached the four
   listed ring cuts.
6. **Look.** The heartwood wall (long grain, knot holes) reads as the inside of a trunk behind the earth knots and
   walls. The crown shares the woods' set with woods_edge and woods_deep, so it shows no seam.

## What this cannot judge

Whether eight Enraizar songs up a 45-row climb feel like a chore. No real kit has Enraizar without the double jump
(Bloom gives both), so this route checks the gate, not a path players take. Whether a player looks for the crown from
the hut roof: the camera clamps the woods_edge frame at y 0, so the crown's floor shows only during the jump.
