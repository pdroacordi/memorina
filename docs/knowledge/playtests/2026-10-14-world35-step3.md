# WORLD-35 step 3: the glacier, the icefall and the ravine secret

Windowed debug runs of `tools/playtest/scripts/world35s3_*.json` (seven timelines) with a per-frame trace. Positions are
Ivo's feet in world px.

Final layout (2026-10-15, after the review and the user's "Straight drop into deep snow (Recommended)"):
- lake_glacier's 34-cell pool, with the letter on a one-way ledge over its middle;
- lake_icefall (c 11, r -5..-2), one straight 2-cell shaft;
- threshold_icefall_foot (c 11, r -1), the shaft's last screen, in the Threshold region;
- a SoftLanding zone under the snow drift on threshold_bridge's fallen span.

## Findings

1. **The drop is a soft landing.** About 1890 px and 2.4 s from the mouth, landing at 1000 px/s. Inside the
   SoftLanding zone, `LandingRule` makes it a plain landing, and Ivo runs and jumps at once. The drift reads as snow on
   the span (`a09_snow_landing`).
2. **No jump from the bridge enters the Frozen Lake.**
   - The east deck's double jump stops under threshold_icefall_foot's rock with its feet 22 px short of the 64 px
     that swaps rooms.
   - lake_icefall's bottom lies 544 px over the deck, past a double jump plus a Redoma shell (462 px).
   - Only a jump straight into the shaft's 2-cell mouth enters the foot room, a Threshold room, so the camera glides
     and no title plays.
3. **The east deck's jump is free.** The rock starts at y -96, and a base jump's head reaches -38.
4. **The icefall is one-way for every kit without wall climb.**
   - The kit check, run from the bridge with the glacier's west mouth sealed, finds no such kit in lake_icefall,
     threshold_icefall_foot or lake_glacier.
   - The four wall-climb kits climb the shaft's walls. Orchestrator call, 2026-10-14: the plan's backtrack payoff,
     next to the user's "a one-way icefall drop into the Threshold ravine".
5. **Only Congelar reaches the letter.** The kit check reaches the ledge with the six kits that know Congelar, and
   with none of the others. woods_orchards, a wall climber without Congelar, reached the first draft's east-lip shelf;
   it no longer reaches the letter.
6. **Nothing reads as a floor that is not one.** threshold_icefall_foot's open rows show a `[back]` wall whose top is
   behind the rock's underside. The shaft's mouth over the bridge shows the shaft's rock wall above the cliff's face,
   a listed `SHAFT_MOUTH` cut.
7. **Look.**
   - The shaft is a 2-cell slot in rock, so its frames are mostly rock tiles; a frozen waterfall waits for MEM-10.
   - The region title plays only when the save has not yet seen the Threshold (these runs started fresh).

## What this cannot judge

- Whether a 2.4 s fall in a narrow slot feels like a thrill or a wait.
- Whether a player finds the ravine secret: one crack and a dust draft in a grey (0.3) region.
- Not driven windowed: a wall-climb ascent of the shaft (the audit's lattice shows it), and a Redoma shell under the
  shaft (checked by numbers).
