# WORLD-35 step 7: the connections pass

Windowed debug runs on the built world, with a per-frame trace.
- **New timelines:** 11 `tools/playtest/scripts/world35s7_*.json`, for the joins no step had crossed.
- **Re-runs on this build:** 35 existing leg timelines, which cover the legs of loops A, B and F.
- **Units and save:** positions are Ivo's feet in world px. The runner never writes a player's save (`BootPolicy`).
- **Kits** are seeded per timeline: skills, songs and restored guardians.
- **Chained legs:** a loop is run as legs. Each leg starts where the previous one ends, because the runner has no mid-run teleport and long fixed-time timelines drift (finding 6).

## Findings

1. **No map bug.** Every leg of every loop passes on the built world with its kit, and no `.room` file changed in this step. Each failed run was traced to the timeline: drift, stale geometry or a short jump hold, never to the world.

2. **Awkward: the square's well on the hub street** (predates WORLD-35, `systems/world` "the square's well (rimmed)").
   - Walking between village_gate_east and the square bench takes three hops: onto the east rim post, over the 4-cell shaft, over the west rim post. No single running jump clears it: about 192 px against a range of about 179 px.
   - A miss drops Ivo onto the plank beam under the square, one jump from the street (g08; B3 shows the way back).
   - Loop G ends on this crossing, and so does every walk west from the threshold, the lighthouse or the lake. Whether it should stay is a design call: the rims stop an accidental fall into the Caldera.

3. **Tight spots on loops** (not bugs; each passes):
   - **The hollow tree's exit.** The top landing is two cells (cols 6-7). A straight double jump from the row-6 knot's east end (x -7424) misses it. The hop up into the street must drift past the well's east rim. A miss falls three knots, to row 12.
   - **The Redoma-first way out of the Caldera.** The pool step to caldera_cistern's slab is 128 px and needs a full-height jump (142 px): 14 px spare. The old `caldera_cistern_redoma_back_up` holds jump 0.35 s, which rises 109 px, and fails.
   - **The bridge gap westward** needs about 172 px of the jump's 179. The brute stands on the east deck and knocked Ivo back twice in two of five runs.
     - A missed gap jump lands on the ravine floor. Walking west there passes into the ravine secret's tunnel and the cave (by design).
     - The ravine is left east by the fallen span, as before.
   - **The drop down the terraces' hole into woods_canopy** (384 px, loop C's return) is a hard landing (0.75 s stun). There is no soft landing; acceptable for a loop's way back.

4. **Long.** Loop G's stair is 15 alike hops (about 45 s; step 5 could not judge it either). Loop A crosses 11 rooms; three benches lie on it.

5. **Unlabelled.**
   - Nothing shows that orchard_arena's east face leads to the crag (WORLD-38).
   - Before GRD-04 the sun stair's grate is always closed: a visible "later".
   - Reward slots draw nothing in release, by design.
   - The loops themselves have no signs. Benches and the bright village are their landmarks.

6. **Harness: inputs land on render frames** (TECH-11).
   - The runner applies steps in `_process`. On this machine, with an editor open, process frames arrived about 0.16 s apart, while physics ran at 60 Hz. A press therefore lands up to 150 ms late.
   - Effect: `world25_waterfall_zigzag_climb`, which passed in step 1 on the same room text, walked off its fifth ledge before the jump arrived, in two runs.
   - Effect: double jumps with presses 0.1 s apart merged into one frame and lost the second jump.
   - The new timelines use presses 0.2 s apart, and jumps taken against a wall (walk until blocked, then act).
   - Nine older timelines no longer reach their goal for other reasons (stale geometry, retuned brute, short holds); they are listed under TECH-09.

7. **Look.**
   - The plank under the open grate (g03) and the hop up into the street (g04) read cleanly.
   - The snow landing shows the Threshold's title, only because a test save has not seen the Threshold (d15).
   - Each bench's bloom marks a loop's end (c25, c33, d17, e17, g09).

## What this cannot judge

- Whether players find the loops without signs, or think to climb orchard_arena's east face.
- Feel of the 15-hop stair and the 2.4 s icefall fall.
- The bridge gap and the square's well at a steady 60 fps: the runner's input lag makes both look harder than they are.
