## What was tested

New timelines (`tools/playtest/scripts/world34_*.json`), each run on the WORLD-34 worktree and on a `git archive` export of main (7d05dca) for the before frame:

| Timeline | Action |
|---|---|
| `world34_frost_shore_pillar` | From frost_shore's breaker (9400, 192), double jump east with right held toward the col-39 pillar top. |
| `world34_ceiling_bonk` | From woods_pond's rock shelf (-5264, 64), a straight double jump under the open top. |
| `world34_trial_side_wall` | On the winter trial's west wall top (48, 5776), walk west for 1.2 s, then jump west. |
| `world34_ceiling_<room>` (5) | Stand at the room's highest floor, hold look_up (lake_chasm: a double jump), one still per room. |

Risk timelines re-run (plan risks 1 and 3), from `traverse_*`, `world25_*`, `world29_*`, `camfall_*`, `song_*` and `open_sky_*`: rv_n2 and rv4_s1 (pond double jumps), open_sky_pond_shelf_jump, l_lane_west_fence, s_chasm_climb_and_root_bridge, o5b chasm bridge, t_chasm_control_no_root, o7 counterweights drop column, camfall_a1 counterweights to mill, song_release_counterweight, rv_w2b windy gap, i/j frost cliffs, world25 waterfall and high wall, o/v waterfall top, world29 seam, r shore causeway.

## Findings

- **Nothing outside a room is stood on.** Before: the double jump ends standing on frost_shore's col-39 pillar top at (9589, 0), outside every room (`frost_shore_pillar_before.png`), which is the user's case. After: Ivo is held at x 9557 against the edge over the pillar (`frost_shore_pillar_after_edge.png`) and drops to the beach at y 288 (`frost_shore_pillar_after_landed.png`).
- **The ceiling stops a jump and he falls back.** Feet peak at -98 over a top at 0, so the body top is at -152, exactly `CEILING_RISE`. Before, this timeline's feet peaked at -149 and rv_n2's at -206. He lands back on the shelf at (-5264, 64) with no fade (`pond_shelf_ceiling_bonk.png`, `pond_shelf_landed_back.png`). His head stays in frame at the bonk.
- **A walk into a side with no neighbour stops.** Before: Ivo leaves the winter trial (x -36) and the safety net fades the screen (0.78) (`trial_side_before.png`). After: his body stops at the bound (x 11.07, body edge at 0), with no fade, no wall slide and no leak warning (`trial_side_after.png`).
- **Five drawn rock ceilings.** Row 0 reads as the room's own stone in frost_cliffs, lake_waterfall_path, lake_high_wall, lake_chasm and orchard_counterweights (`ceiling_*_after.png` against `_before.png`, which show the rock backdrop running off the frame). Stone meets stone without a seam, except in lake_chasm, where the new stone row meets the earth column 39 at the top right (the map's own earth wall). The ceiling's top edge shows the stone's lit lip, seen only when the camera yields into the ring above.
- **Routes still work.** The lake_chasm root bridge (s, o5b), the counterweights drop column and the fall to the mill (o7, camfall_a1), the waterfall climb (world25, o, world29), the high wall ladder, the shore causeway to the chasm and the lane_west fence all reach their rooms. No run logged an edge-leak warning.
- **rv4_s1 no longer crosses woods_pond's col-0 pillar through the sky** (peak feet -97.8, back on the pond floor). This is the intended consequence of "never land on anything outside" (A-Q4). The timeline is retired (deleted), and the bug entries citing it say so.
- **Pre-existing drift, not WORLD-34.** `traverse_opening_i_cliffs_climb` stops at y -672 (report 2026-10-09: -864), `rv_w2b` lands in the mill town, and `v_waterfall_top_west_branch` ends where it starts. All three give the same positions, to the pixel, on the main export, so they are older timeline drift.

## Ratings rationale

- Fun 3: the change removes exploits rather than adding play. A double jump over an open top now bonks at about one jump. Whether that feels arbitrary under a visible sky cannot be judged from stills.
- Fluidity 3: the bonk drops vertical speed to zero and he falls straight back; no snag, slide or jitter shows in the traces. Feel at 60 fps was not judged.
- Aesthetics 3: the five ceilings read as rock, and the open-sky ceiling is invisible by design. The lake_chasm stone/earth meeting at the top right is a visible material change.
