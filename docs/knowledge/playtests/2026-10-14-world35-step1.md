# WORLD-35 step 1: reward slot markers and new secrets

Windowed debug runs of `tools/playtest/scripts/world35_*.json` (seven timelines), plus a re-run of `world25_waterfall_zigzag_climb.json`. The chimney-base and lake_shore runs are from after the step's review (2026-10-14): the false floor moved into a ceiling nook (`bugs/the-caldera-chimney-base-false-floor-lies-on-the-only-route`), and lake_shore's secret shrank to cols 11-13.

## What was checked

| Timeline | Result |
|---|---|
| `world35_markers` | The lookout marker stands on lighthouse_base's top east step. Two jumps with right held climb the steps to it (Ivo ends at x 12117, on the top step). |
| `world35_marker_canopy` | The nest marker stands at woods_canopy's west end, in view from the spawn. |
| `world35_secret_frost_cliffs` | From the block east of the bench cave, one jump west with left held to t 3.5 lands in the niche (y 128, col 11). The wall fades open on entry and shows the slot. |
| `world35_secret_chimney_base` | With the double jump: a jump and an air jump drifting right from col 3 land on the shelf (y 1632, col 5). Walking east opens the cracked hollow (Ivo at col 8) and shows the slot in front of the stone back wall. |
| `world35_chimney_base_cross` | Walking east from col 2 to col 10 and back keeps Ivo at y 1824 the whole way: the floor holds where the false floor was. |

| `world25_waterfall_zigzag_climb` | After the wall ledges were merged, the 16-jump zigzag still reaches the top and walks out west into the fishing village. |

## Findings

- **The marker is grey wherever the world is grey.** The greyhush composite greys the root canvas, so the gold (COLLECTIBLE) and brown (CACHE) fills read as two greys in the Frost Edge (memory 0.05) and the woods. The diamond and its outline stay readable, so the kind is not visible in these stills. This is accepted for a debug marker; nothing ships in release.
- **The first marker sat in the grass.** On woods_canopy it was half hidden by grass blades. Fixed in this change: the marker is drawn 22 px above the floor at z 30 with a 2 px outline. z 30 stays under a closed secret's world copy (z 40), so a slot inside a secret stays hidden until the secret opens (seen in all three secret runs).
- **The terraces niche showed the sky through solid rock.** When the niche opened it showed the orchard sky backdrop. Fixed with a stone `[back]` wall behind it (and behind lake_ice_caves' hollow), the WORLD-31 rule for an enclosed hollow. Re-captured: `terraces_secret_open.png` shows a recess in the rock.
- **The crack hints are faint.** The `k` decal is a few pixels wide. It is visible on the frost crag face and the terraces block, but barely on the chimney-base basalt floor (`chimney_base_secret_closed.png`). Same decal as WORLD-21's secrets; not changed here.
- **The chimney-base secret was found by walking over it (fixed).** The first version was a false floor on the only route east. It has no collision, so every player fell in at col 6 on the way through. After review it is a double-jump nook in the ceiling block, off the walking line (`bugs/the-caldera-chimney-base-false-floor-lies-on-the-only-route`).
- **Timing notes for timelines.** The frost niche needs left held about 1 s after the jump; releasing at 0.55 s drops Ivo short, onto the cave floor. The chimney shelf takes a jump held 0.3 s and an air jump 0.1 s later, with right held for 0.4 s from the air jump.
