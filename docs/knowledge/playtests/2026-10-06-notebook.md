## What was tested

- **Setup.** Godot 4.7.2, windowed. Every run used a fresh `APPDATA` folder, checked by a
  headless probe before each launch. The real `save_debug_1.tres` md5 was `49ae7e28…` before
  and after all 14 runs.
- **Timelines** in `tools/playtest/scripts/notebook_*.json`:
  - `keyboard_nav`, `pad`;
  - `pages_en`, `pages_pt_BR`;
  - `song_and_screens`;
  - `quill_lesson`, `quill_fight`;
  - `read_survives_death`;
  - `scroll_up_stuck_en`, `scroll_up_stuck_pt_BR`.
- **Runner changes, made for this session:**
  - `log` now prints the notebook's phase, section and page, and its rows (`>` focused,
    `*` unread, `()` hidden). It also prints the cues, the tab marks, the read ids, the
    quill's alpha and the watcher's queue and hold.
  - New timeline fields: `locale`, `met_guardians`, `restored_guardians` and `notebook_read`.
- **Evidence.** State claims come from the log lines. Frames are for the look. 1 pack px is
  4 screenshot px.
- **Locale.** The OS locale is pt_BR, so every run without `locale` is in Portuguese.

## Findings

1. **Toggle, close, hold, refusal: pass.**
   - E and Select (button 4) open and close the book. Esc, B and Start close it, without
     pausing or rolling (`roll=false`; an unpaused B in the same run rolls at 345.9 px/s).
   - While it is open: `paused=true`, `scale=0.00`.
   - Three frames over 2.5 s with a Freeze pulse lit are pixel-identical. The pulse, the
     trees and the life notes all hold (`world_held_under_pulse.png`). After the close,
     612 px change in 0.5 s, 484 of them in the life notes.
   - During the performance (`paused=true scale=1.00`) four E presses and two Selects are
     refused.
   - It opens during the ~1.6 s ring-out before a performance, as the pause does ("held,
     not refused"). Each open pushes the performance back.
   - **Animations.** The open, the close and the turn each step 8 frames over ~0.4 s of real
     time under the hold (`open_animation.png`, `page_turn_and_close.png`). Presses during a
     turn are ignored. The cover's cut at the top shows on frames 0 to 3, as accepted.
2. **Opening rule: pass.**
   - With everything unread and no news this session, it opens on the last unread entry in
     catalog order (Items / Memorina, `opens_on_newest_unread.png`).
   - After an F9 lesson it opens on the entry announced this session (Songs / Longest Day),
     not on the later item.
   - With everything read, it reopens on the last section and row (Songs / Noon Sun).
   - After a death the book is a new instance and opens on Memories. Whether "last" should
     survive a death is a design call.
3. **Navigation: fail on Up in a scrolled list.**
   - Left and right (arrows, D-pad, stick) turn one section and stop at either end.
   - A stick held 1.2 s turns once. Up and down move one row per press or per tilt.
   - "? ? ?" rows are skipped. A section with no known entry (Guardians, fresh) focuses
     nothing.
   - Rows scroll whole, with no row cut. The cues show exactly when rows are hidden
     (`scroll_by_whole_rows.png`).
   - **Fail.** Once Songs has scrolled, Up stops at the first shown row. The rows above it
     cannot be reached with keys, D-pad or stick, even after turning away and back
     (`up_stuck_at_scrolled_row.png`). Reproduced in en and pt_BR. See
     `bugs/the-notebook-list-cannot-scroll-back-up-past-a-hidden-row`. The engine rule behind
     it is `gotchas/gui-focus-skips-a-neighbour-wholly-clipped-by-a-scroll-container`.
   - Cosmetic, same code. When the scroll is clamped at the list's end, the partly clipped
     row is hidden and leaves an empty line under the title rule. Returning to a section
     frames the remembered row at the top, hiding rows that fitted on the first visit.
4. **Unread marks: pass.**
   - A row's mark and its tab's mark clear when the entry leaves the right page: focus moves,
     a page turns, or the book closes.
   - The read ids survive a death. After a fatal fight the rebuilt save still had
     `[item_memorina, item_sword, song_freeze, song_bell_jar]`, and the reopened rows and
     tabs showed no mark.
5. **Song page and glyphs: pass** (`glyphs_follow_device.png`, `pages_en.png`,
   `pages_pt_BR.png`).
   - The page shows the title, the six glyphs at mockup size, the season and material line,
     and the body. Nothing crosses the page or the gutter on any of the 14 entries in either
     language, with the fullest save.
   - The glyphs redraw on the spot: Xbox after a D-pad press, arrows after the Down key,
     WASD after S (which moves nothing), Xbox again after the D-pad.
6. **Guardians and Memories: pass.**
   - Frost restored: the colour portrait on the left page, Restored, "Taught: Hymn of Frost,
     Roll".
   - Bloom met only: a grey portrait, Corrupted, "Taught: Double jump" (the skill was
     granted through `skills`).
   - Roll and Double jump diary pages exist once the skills are held.
   - Not tested: a recall inside a real fight. The fight cannot be won by a timeline cheaply.
7. **HUD quill: pass.**
   - **F9 (Longest Day, 32 s track).** It waited through the 0.5 s unpaused lead-in
     (`holding=true`, `paused=false`) and the whole lesson. It appeared 0.1 s after the
     thaw, at full alpha by 0.5 s, held ~3 s and was gone 0.75 s later
     (`quill_after_lesson.png`).
   - **Bloom fight.** `guardian_bloom` was queued when Ivo entered the trigger, and held for
     the 13 s he was in the fight. The arena's wall stops a retreat at x 2860, so "after the
     fight" could not be reached without winning.
   - **No quill on load or on death:**
     - 0.00 at start with 11 unread entries;
     - 0.00 for 52 s after a fight death, which dropped the queued id;
     - 0.00 at every frame while the notebook was open.
8. **No input leaks: pass.**
   - E and Select are ignored under the pause. M and LB are ignored under the notebook. E is
     ignored under the map.
   - Esc on the notebook closes it and never pauses.
   - Z and A while open do nothing, and Ivo does not jump after the close.
   - A held through a Select close and released after it: no jump.
   - A stick held right through the close resumes the walk at once (192 px/s), as on the
     map.

**Feel and look** (frames, plus inference from timing):
- The book reads well at 2x. The dark-wood ink on the parchment is legible in both
  languages, and the red focused row is clear.
- The patched tilde in "Guardiões" and "mãos" reads as a tilde.
- The 0.4 s open and turn are short enough not to slow reading. That is inferred from the
  timing; how it feels needs a human.
- The quill is small (feather ink ~12x14 game px). It reads against the pale winter sky
  (cream 245 against grey ~128), but it is easy to miss in a busy scene.
- The scroll cues are small but clear ink marks in the gutter.
- The empty line left at the top of a clamped list looks like a layout error.

What frames cannot show: latency between a press and the first frame, the audio, and
whether 0.05 s strip frames look smooth or steppy in motion.

## Ratings rationale

- **Fun 0**: a menu is not play (the convention of the pause and map sessions).
- **Fluidity 3**: every toggle, refusal and close landed on the first press, and nothing
  leaked into the world. The scroll bug holds it at 3: a player who scrolls Songs to the
  bottom cannot go back up to Hymn of Frost. With that fixed, 4.
- **Aesthetics 4**, low confidence. It was not compared side by side with the approved v2
  mockup. The book, tabs and grey portrait read cleanly, and every page fits with margin.
  Points off for the empty top line in a clamped list and the faint quill.
