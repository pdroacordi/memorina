Cargo net, a set-dressing prop for the Frost Edge and the lighthouse (WORLD-13), seen straight on from the side, minimalist 16-bit, no outlines, flat colours. Measured width 38 art px against Ivo's 29 (`frost_edge` `cargo_net:w38` in tools/art/region_prop_sizes.json). Cut from one Codex sheet (tools/art/sources/frost_edge/frost_hold_keeper_codex.png; tools/art/sources/regions/dressing_frost_edge_concept_codex.png, our own approved concept, the only reference) by `python -I tools/art/cut_props.py tools/art/sources/frost_edge/frost_hold_keeper_codex.png <dir> --figure=none --flat=9 --base-shade --colors=64 --names=hammock,ship_lantern,cargo_net,sea_chest,cot,logbook_stand,keeper_chair --size=hammock:w42,ship_lantern:h16,cargo_net:w42,sea_chest:h13,cot:w34,logbook_stand:h20,keeper_chair:h19`. The Codex cut (tools/art/sources/frost_edge/frost_hold_keeper_codex.png, cut as for its sheet) turned to mush, so it was redrawn at its art size by PixelLab pixflux with that cut, bottom-centred on a 48x36 canvas, as the init image (`tools/pixellab/pixellab.ps1 generate -Width 48 -Height 36 -NoBackground -Shading "flat shading" -Detail "low detail" -Outline lineless -InitStrength 60 -Reference tools/art/sources/frost_edge/pixellab/cargo_net_init.png`, described as "a pale rope cargo net hanging between two wooden pegs at its top corners, sagging in the middle, one connected square mesh of thick rope"; raw kept as tools/art/sources/frost_edge/pixellab/cargo_net_px.png) and cleaned by `python -I tools/art/clean_prop.py tools/art/sources/frost_edge/pixellab/cargo_net_px.png <target> --flat=10 --palette=tools/art/sources/frost_edge/pixellab/frost_hold_keeper_palette.png --base-shade --drop-specks=4`; the palette is the sheet's cut colours. Redrawn because the cut was unreadable at game size ("Fix the weak ones, then place (Recommended)", user, 2026-11-23). Approved with the board docs/design/drafts/early_regions_dressing_board.md (user, 2026-11-22: "approved"; concepts "Approve, fix in sheets (Recommended)"); the sheet is shown to the user before it is placed. Recoloured after the WORLD-12..14 playtest (a white grid that read as a gate), "Fix them now" (user, 2026-11-23): its pale rope colours mapped by brightness onto dull hemp (98,82,62), (116,98,74), (132,112,84); shape and size unchanged.
