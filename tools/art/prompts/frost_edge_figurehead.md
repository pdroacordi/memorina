Figurehead, a set-dressing prop for the Frost Edge and the lighthouse (WORLD-13), seen straight on from the side, minimalist 16-bit, no outlines, flat colours. Measured width 47 art px against Ivo's 29 (`frost_edge` `figurehead:w47` in tools/art/region_prop_sizes.json). Cut from one Codex sheet (tools/art/sources/frost_edge/frost_shore_built_codex.png; tools/art/sources/regions/dressing_frost_edge_concept_codex.png, our own approved concept, the only reference) by `python -I tools/art/cut_props.py tools/art/sources/frost_edge/frost_shore_built_codex.png <dir> --figure=none --flat=9 --base-shade --colors=64 --names=figurehead,mast_snapped,anchor,buoy,bollard,fog_bell,flagpole,railing,oil_cans,coal_bin,storm_lantern,blanket_folded --size=figurehead:w51,mast_snapped:h68,anchor:h28,buoy:h14,bollard:h14,fog_bell:h38,flagpole:h75,railing:h16,oil_cans:h14,coal_bin:h14,storm_lantern:h14,blanket_folded:w18`. The Gull's bow carving half buried in snow (concept fix "half-bury the figurehead as a bow carving", user, 2026-11-22). The Codex cut (tools/art/sources/frost_edge/frost_shore_built_codex.png, cut as for its sheet) turned to mush, so it was redrawn at its art size by PixelLab pixflux with that cut, bottom-centred on a 56x38 canvas, as the init image (`tools/pixellab/pixellab.ps1 generate -Width 56 -Height 38 -NoBackground -Shading "flat shading" -Detail "low detail" -Outline lineless -InitStrength 150 -Reference tools/art/sources/frost_edge/pixellab/figurehead_init.png`, described as "a carved wooden ship's bow figurehead of a gull: a clear gull head with a hooked beak and an eye, neck and chest, carved wings swept back, the lower half buried in a white snow mound"; raw kept as tools/art/sources/frost_edge/pixellab/figurehead_px.png) and cleaned by `python -I tools/art/clean_prop.py tools/art/sources/frost_edge/pixellab/figurehead_px.png <target> --flat=10 --palette=tools/art/sources/frost_edge/pixellab/frost_shore_built_palette.png --base-shade --drop-specks=4`; the palette is the sheet's cut colours. Redrawn because the cut was unreadable at game size ("Fix the weak ones, then place (Recommended)", user, 2026-11-23).
