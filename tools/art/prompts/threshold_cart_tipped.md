Cart tipped, a set-dressing prop for Threshold Road (WORLD-12), seen straight on from the side, minimalist 16-bit, no outlines, flat colours. Measured width 41 art px against Ivo's 29 (`threshold` `cart_tipped:w41` in tools/art/region_prop_sizes.json). Cut from one Codex sheet (tools/art/sources/threshold/threshold_built_codex.png; tools/art/sources/regions/dressing_threshold_concept_codex.png, our own approved concept, the only reference) by `python -I tools/art/cut_props.py tools/art/sources/threshold/threshold_built_codex.png <dir> --figure=none --flat=9 --base-shade --colors=64 --names=wayshrine,fence_broken,fence_stub,cart_tipped,scythe,sheaf_bound,sheaf_loose,milestone,parapet_stub,masonry_fallen,sack_worn,earth_rubble,crow_perched,crow_looking --size=wayshrine:h40,fence_broken:h18,fence_stub:h18,cart_tipped:w52,scythe:h33,sheaf_bound:h18,sheaf_loose:w22,milestone:h15,parapet_stub:w36,masonry_fallen:w32,sack_worn:h13,earth_rubble:w43,crow_perched:h10,crow_looking:h9`. The Codex cut (tools/art/sources/threshold/threshold_built_codex.png, cut as for its sheet) turned to mush, so it was redrawn at its art size by PixelLab pixflux with that cut, bottom-centred on a 56x32 canvas, as the init image (`tools/pixellab/pixellab.ps1 generate -Width 56 -Height 32 -NoBackground -Shading "flat shading" -Detail "low detail" -Outline lineless -InitStrength 150 -Reference tools/art/sources/threshold/pixellab/cart_tipped_init.png`, described as "an abandoned wooden farm cart tipped over on its side in snow, one big round spoked wooden wheel clearly visible in front, plank body, a shaft lying on the ground, a little snow on top"; raw kept as tools/art/sources/threshold/pixellab/cart_tipped_px.png) and cleaned by `python -I tools/art/clean_prop.py tools/art/sources/threshold/pixellab/cart_tipped_px.png <target> --flat=10 --palette=tools/art/sources/threshold/pixellab/threshold_built_palette.png --base-shade --drop-specks=4`; the palette is the sheet's cut colours. Redrawn because the cut was unreadable at game size ("Fix the weak ones, then place (Recommended)", user, 2026-11-23). Approved with the board docs/design/drafts/early_regions_dressing_board.md (user, 2026-11-22: "approved"; concepts "Approve, fix in sheets (Recommended)"); the sheet is shown to the user before it is placed.
