Log stack, a set-dressing prop for the Spring Woods (WORLD-14), seen straight on from the side, minimalist 16-bit, no outlines, flat colours. Measured height 15 art px against Ivo's 29 (`spring_woods` `log_stack:h15` in tools/art/region_prop_sizes.json). Cut from one Codex sheet (tools/art/sources/spring_woods/woods_small_codex.png; tools/art/sources/regions/dressing_spring_woods_concept_codex.png, our own approved concept, the only reference) by `python -I tools/art/cut_props.py tools/art/sources/spring_woods/woods_small_codex.png <dir> --figure=none --flat=9 --base-shade --colors=64 --names=log_stack,saw_horse,hand_cart,birdhouse_post,mossy_stump,mossy_rock,flowering_bush,ferns,mushrooms,shelf_fungus,wood_knot,resin_streak,cracked_mud,dead_reeds,lily_pad,blossom_heap --size=log_stack:h18,saw_horse:h16,hand_cart:w40,birdhouse_post:h40,mossy_stump:h18,mossy_rock:h14,flowering_bush:h17,ferns:h16,mushrooms:h10,shelf_fungus:w16,wood_knot:h16,resin_streak:h24,cracked_mud:w52,dead_reeds:h19,lily_pad:w16,blossom_heap:w48`. The Codex cut (tools/art/sources/spring_woods/woods_small_codex.png, cut as for its sheet) turned to mush, so it was redrawn at its art size by PixelLab pixflux with that cut, bottom-centred on a 32x32 canvas, as the init image (`tools/pixellab/pixellab.ps1 generate -Width 32 -Height 32 -NoBackground -Shading "flat shading" -Detail "low detail" -Outline lineless -InitStrength 150 -Reference tools/art/sources/spring_woods/pixellab/log_stack_init.png`, described as "a neat stack of cut firewood logs seen from the side, round pale log ends with a ring, held between two upright wooden posts"; raw kept as tools/art/sources/spring_woods/pixellab/log_stack_px.png) and cleaned by `python -I tools/art/clean_prop.py tools/art/sources/spring_woods/pixellab/log_stack_px.png <target> --flat=10 --palette=tools/art/sources/spring_woods/pixellab/woods_small_palette.png --base-shade --drop-specks=4`; the palette is the sheet's cut colours. Redrawn because the cut was unreadable at game size ("Fix the weak ones, then place (Recommended)", user, 2026-11-23). Approved with the board docs/design/drafts/early_regions_dressing_board.md (user, 2026-11-22: "approved"; concepts "Approve, fix in sheets (Recommended)"); the sheet is shown to the user before it is placed.
