STORY-42, the final fragment: Ermo's story, the origin of the Greyhush (text `docs/design/drafts/fragmento_final.md`, "Aprovar o texto (Recommended)", user, 2026-11-18; plan `docs/knowledge/architecture/story42-the-final-fragment.md`, "The look" and "Art"). Seven engraved panels in the bearer fragments' format (the same paper and woodcut line, `story_bearer_frost.md`) with a look of its own: TWO inks instead of one, his winter BLUE (#2f6fd0, key #FF00FF, tinted by `winter_palette.tres`) and the flower PINK (#e0609a, key #00FFFF, tinted by `flower_ink.tres`), and a third MIXED ink (drawn violet #8a4fc8, key #FFFF00) that the shader draws as the trembling grey of the dissonance (02 §5.3). Each panel is an opaque canvas of 384x176 art px (1 px per art px, drawn at Sprite2D scale 2) holding only the four `GREYS` of `tools/art/cut_engraving.py` and the three keys; only ink-only layers move.
