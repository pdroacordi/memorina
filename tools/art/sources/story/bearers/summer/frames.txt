SUBJECT: four frames of one memory, "the old hand": a very old woman, tiny and thin, long white hair loose, a simple sleeveless robe, bare feet, a walking stick.
Panel 1: noon inside a volcanic caldera: a wide bowl of rock walls and jagged ridges, a flat dark stone in the middle, the sun high overhead as a big ringed disc of ink rays. The old woman sits cross-legged on the flat stone, perfectly still, small in the wide landscape. A thin smoke plume rises from the crater rim far behind.
Panel 2: the same place: the sun stands still straight above; the old woman plays a small pipe; the hard black shadows of the rocks and of her own body are burned onto the flat stone like dark stains; heat ripples in the air (wavy ink lines).
Panel 3: close view: the old woman's two old hands and a long thorn, slowly boring a hole through a round dark volcanic stone held on her knee; around her a ring of small sun-and-moon marks scratched in the rock, counting many days.
Panel 4: (the most important picture) a great LION of black volcanic stone with a huge mane like sun rays or flame petals (see the fourth creature in the attached reference image, drawn in this woodcut style) lies on the flat stone, the round holed stone resting between its front paws; the old woman is gone, but her seated shadow remains burned on the flat stone beside the lion: a LARGE, clear, solid dark-grey silhouette of the old woman sitting cross-legged with her pipe (as in panel 1), as big as the lion's head, lying on the stone to the left of the lion, nobody casting it. The lion's body is dark grey volcanic stone (a dark grey fill), its mane in ink lines.
