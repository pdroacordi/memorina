SUBJECT: four frames of one memory, "the restless hand": a thin young man with wind-tossed hair, a short travelling cloak and a satchel, always leaning forward as if about to leave.
Panel 1: the young man stands on the edge of a high cliff, seen from the side, the wind at his back blowing his cloak and hair forward; far below a valley of orchards and rolling hills; long streaks of wind lines and a few leaves in the air.
Panel 2: an autumn orchard of round fruit trees. A flock of birds flies away low over the trees; the young man is shown ONLY ONCE: he kneels in the grass under the trees, holding one small fallen bird gently cupped in both hands, looking up after the flock that flies away over the trees.
Panel 3: the young man sits on a low stone wall in the orchard playing a short white flute (made from a thin bird bone) held sideways at his lips; around him ALL the leaves let go of the trees at the same moment, a big swirl of falling leaves filling the air, the branches becoming bare.
Panel 4: a bare autumn field with a tall SCARECROW-SPIRIT made of bones and autumn leaves (a skull-like face, long thin bony limbs, a cloak of leaves; see the third creature in the attached reference image, drawn in this woodcut style) standing still; the little flute hangs on a cord from its outstretched bony hand; the young man is small in the distance, walking away to the right with the wind and the leaves.
