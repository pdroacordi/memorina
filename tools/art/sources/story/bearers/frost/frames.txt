SUBJECT: four frames of one memory, "the winter hand": a lean man in a long hooded coat and boots, carrying a small hanging lantern, and a short dark branch in his belt.
Panel 1: night. A wide dark lake, still and black (dark grey mass), seen from the near shore. On the far shore across the water, a small village of a few huts with steep roofs. No bridge, no boat. Stars as tiny ink dots, a thin moon. Empty and wide.
Panel 2: night, the near shore. The man stands at the water's edge with his lantern, one foot stepping out onto the lake; under his feet and ahead of him a path of white ice slabs spreads across the black water, a few steps long, cracks and frost lines radiating from each step. He holds the branch to his lips like a flute.
Panel 3: dusk in deep winter. A long road of ice crosses the lake to the far village. A line of villagers walks across it behind the man, carrying bundles on their backs and a child on a shoulder. The man leads with his lantern.
Panel 4: the man, now old and stooped with a long beard, kneels on the frozen lake and pushes the short branch upright into the ice. Behind him towers a huge STONE-AND-ICE GOLEM (massive dark stone body with jagged ice crystals growing on its shoulders and back; see the second creature in the attached reference image, drawn in this woodcut style), bending down, its big stone hand reaching to take the branch.
